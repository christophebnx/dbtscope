# UI review: coherence, understanding and navigation (2026-11-17)

Branch `feature/visualization-optimization`. Review only, no code changed. Audience judged: data engineers, analysts, architects.

Method: read `docs/ui-spec.md`, the page assets, then built two pages (the committed acme_retail manifest, and the generated large Data Vault of 1,419 tables, `tests/large_project.py`) and drove them in headless Chromium at 1280×800 and 360×740. Screenshots (scratchpad `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-dbtscope/5161291f-7ad2-533e-aae8-daaf02fdeeb8/scratchpad/`): `large-1-open.png`, `acme-1-open.png`, `L-3-dom.png` (after double-click on Vehicle), `L-4-zoomed.png`, `L-5-dbl-table.png` (detail after double-click on a table), `L-6-traj.png`, `L-7-flow.png`, `L-8-layers.png`, `L-9-cards.png`, `large-2-detail.png`, `L-10-mobile.png`.

## What works

- The opening state of the large project is legible: 5 business constellations plus 7 source-system discs, names and counts, composition bars, routes. Cards off by default is the right call: with cards on (`L-9-cards.png`, layer mode) the rim arcs float detached from the cards, whereas the default shows a recognisable map.
- Flow view (`L-7-flow.png`) is the clearest screen: left-to-right, depth and layer on every box, a strong start marker, a clear "← Universe".
- Detail focus (`L-5-dbl-table.png`) is excellent for the "one table and its relations" task.
- Esc, the flow's Back button and the "Leave" button give a way back inside the trajectory and flow.

## Findings

### 1. No explicit drill-down path, no sense of place (major, the user's main complaint)
**Status: owned by M9.2** (breadcrumb, Esc stepping back, Domains rows as "go to"); M9.1 built only the first breadcrumb segment (D-086).
Evidence: the only way from the overview to a table is free zoom plus double-click, and the model has four unnamed levels (overview, constellation, table, flow) with three different back mechanisms (Fit, Leave/Esc, tab click). After double-click on Vehicle (`L-3-dom.png`) nothing says "you are in Vehicle"; the meta line still reads "1,419 tables · 12 domains", the panel is unchanged, and the Domains list in the panel (the natural place to choose a domain) only toggles a filter (verified: clicking the Vehicle row leaves 11 domains and 1,120 tables). The hint line is at the very bottom of a 1,195 px panel, so below the fold at 800 px: the gestures are not discoverable on first contact.
Recommendation: introduce a visible location line in the universe toolbar (replacing the count line, which moves to a tooltip or the panel): `All constellations › Vehicle › bumper_contact_link`, each segment a button; "All" = Fit, a domain = frame it, the table = open its detail. Make double-click, domain-row click, and card-row click all update it. Make Esc step one level back (table detail → constellation → overview), as it already does for trajectory and flow. Make the Domains/Layers rows navigate (label click = go to, checkbox = filter), or add a small "Go to" affordance. Show a one-line "Double-click a constellation to enter it" caption on the canvas at the opening state, until the first zoom.

### 2. Inside a constellation, names are missing and links form a hairball (major)
**Status: owned by M9.2** (entering a domain, layer headers, links on hover or selection); not in M9.1.
Evidence: `L-3-dom.png`: Vehicle with 299 tables at constellation zoom shows 4 hub names and ~2,600 lineage and relation edges drawn as grey spaghetti ("Links: both" by default). Only after about 6 wheel notches (`L-4-zoomed.png`) do names appear, and then without edges meaning. The user's wish is exactly "one constellation with table names".
Recommendation: a "constellation level" with a calmer default: when a constellation is entered, draw no table-to-table links until a table is hovered or selected (spotlight its links only, as routes already do), and offer a names list for that constellation (a searchable list or a name per star up to N tables, with the layers as column headers, since the layer bands are already laid out left to right). Show the layer names as headers above the bands at that level (the spec says today "No layer names on the canvas").

### 3. Hand-off between tabs is one-way and silent (major)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** (**← Universe** in the Detail toolbar, the table marked and framed on return, tab `title`s; D-086). Preselecting the detail's group from a framed domain is owned by M9.2 (over `detailDomains`).
Evidence: double-click on a table switches to Detail with `group = vehicle`, `focus = bumper_contact_link` (good context carry-over, `L-5-dbl-table.png`), but nothing tells the user how to go back: the only way is the "Universe" tab, and the universe does not highlight the table they came from. Opposite direction: the Detail tab opened directly shows the first group with no hint of which group the user looked at in the universe. The trajectory to flow path adds a third overlay with its own back button.
Recommendation: show a "← Universe" link (or the same breadcrumb) in the Detail toolbar when the detail was opened from the universe; on return, mark the table (the search ring already exists). Opening Detail from a framed constellation should preselect that group. Say what each tab is for in the tab itself (title attributes: "Universe: the map of every table by domain" / "Detail: the ER diagram of one group").

### 4. Universe panel is a flat stack of 11 blocks in an unhelpful order (major)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** (Navigate / Display / Filters, "More display options" closed at load, hint moved to the **?** popover, "all (N)" inline; D-085). The Domains rows keep acting as filters until M9.2.
Evidence: panel order is Constellations, Entities, Links, Routes, Show all routes, Tables, Summary cards, Layers, Types, Domains, Source systems, then the hint (1,195 px tall on the large project; `large-1-open.png`). It mixes four kinds of control: grouping (Constellations), display options (Entities, Links, Routes, Tables, Summary cards), filters (Layers, Types, Domains) and help. The most-used filter, Domains, is below the fold; "Show all routes 43" and "Entities Fold… 440" carry unexplained numbers. In layer mode the Domains list stays, but "Show all routes" disappears silently.
Recommendation: three titled groups in this order: "Navigate" (Constellations by domain/layer, then the Domains/Source systems list as the table of contents, with counts), "Display" (Detail level, Entities, Links, Routes, Cards, in one collapsible `<details>`, closed by default except Detail level), "Filters" (Layers, Types). Move the hint out of the panel into a "?" popover near the toolbar, or a first-visit caption on the canvas. Collapse "Show all routes" into the Routes line as a link.

### 5. Wording of the panel is cryptic for the audience (major)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** (Show, Links relations / lineage, "Draw … inside their table", Domain or Group by truth, Table kind; D-084).
Evidence, all in `large-1-open.png`:
- "Tables: names / keys / all fields" is the detail level of the stars but reads as a table filter; Detail tab calls the same idea "Columns". Rename both "Show: names / key columns / all columns".
- "Links: business relations / lineage / both" mixes two things; engineers know them as "FK relations" and "lineage (ref/source)". Use "Relations" and "Lineage" and keep "Links" for neither.
- "Fold Satellite · PIT 440": "fold" is jargon; say "Group satellites and PITs into their hub (440)". The ticked state is also the default and hides 440 tables, so the meta line "428 entities" appears with no explanation; say "1,419 tables (428 drawn as entities)".
- Detail's "Group" vs universe's "Domains" are the same thing: use "Domain" in both, or say "Group" in both.
- "Types" is the kind (Hub, Link, Satellite, Staging); in a Data Vault audience "Type" suggests column type. Use "Table kind" ("Kind" in config).
- "Around the focused table: direct links / 2 levels / 3 levels" is good once a table is focused but appears with no focus (see 9).

### 6. Metaphor: "Universe / constellation / star / trajectory / route" (minor to major)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** (Lineage, Lineage graph, Dependencies between domains, domain / layer instead of constellation; D-084). Code words, ids, config keys and export file names keep the old words.
Evidence: the visual metaphor (discs with dots) is readable and the first view is attractive; the audience nevertheless meets "Trajectory" (lineage up/downstream from one table), "Flow" (the same, laid out as a DAG), "Routes" (aggregated links between domains) and "Links" (table-level edges) as four near-synonyms. Engineers and architects search for "lineage", "upstream/downstream", "dependencies", "domain", "ERD".
Recommendation: keep "Universe" and "Detail" as tab names (they are the page's identity) but rename the verbs and nouns that have a standard equivalent: "Trajectory" → "Lineage" (the bar already says "16 upstream · 0 downstream"), "Flow" → "Lineage graph", "Routes between constellations" → "Dependencies between domains", and in the UI say "domain" in place of "constellation" wherever the grouping is by domain (use "layer" in layer mode). The "constellation" word then only survives in docs. This is a string-table change (`L`/`T`), no layout change.

### 7. Detail view on a large group: legible structure but unsorted names and a double-row toolbar (minor)
**Status: partly fixed in M9.1** (meta wording "138 of 277 tables shown · 139 drawn inside", "dashed outline = other domain", `aria-label`s on the inputs). **Deferred**: visible labels on the two inputs (the detail toolbar already wraps to several rows at 360 px) and bigger hub names at Fit (capping the skeleton is a layout change, not a wording one); the breadcrumb is M9.2.
Evidence: `large-2-detail.png`: the skeleton for Vehicle (138 of 277 tables) is a good overview but the names are 8 px at Fit and the table/column "+ N folded tables · Satellite" text is unreadable at Fit; the toolbar takes two rows at 1280 (meta wraps, export drops to a second row); "dashed: other group" legend is unclear ("dashed" what?); two inputs "Focus on a table…" and "Highlight…" are visually identical, and there are two ways to focus (typing and double-click) but one is an input and the other a gesture with its hint at the bottom of the panel.
Recommendation: put hub names in a bigger type at Fit by capping the skeleton to the top N hubs per column; reword the meta to "138 of 277 tables shown · 139 folded · dashed outline = other domain"; label the two inputs ("Focus on" / "Highlight") with visible labels, not only placeholders; add the same breadcrumb as in 1.

### 8. Accessibility defects found while testing (major for AA, small to fix)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** for the unlabelled inputs (`aria-label` on `#u-q`, `#d-focus`, `#d-q`; Esc closes popovers). The keyboard path into a domain and the Domains rows' action are owned by M9.2.
Evidence (DOM check): `#u-q`, `#d-focus` and `#d-q` have no label, no `aria-label` and no `title`; they rely on the placeholder only (WCAG 1.3.1, 3.3.2, 4.1.2). The canvas is the only access to table-level navigation and has no keyboard path to enter a constellation (search is the only one). The Domains rows open no action by keyboard besides the checkbox.
Recommendation: give each input an `aria-label` from the string tables; make the breadcrumb of 1 keyboard-operable, which also gives keyboard users the drill-down they lack today.

### 9. Panels show controls that do nothing yet (minor)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** (depth select, Clear focus, fold row, Lineage button and "all (N)" are disabled with a visible reason; Summary cards sits in the Display group; D-085).
Evidence: Detail panel "Around the focused table: direct links / 2 levels" and "Passing through Link costs no level" are shown with no table focused (`large-2-detail.png`); the Entities checkbox is greyed with a one-line reason only after focus. In layers mode (`L-8-layers.png`) "Show all routes" vanishes; the "Domains" list remains with a filter that now reads as a legend. A checkbox "Summary cards" is in the Tables block but is really a zoom-out display mode.
Recommendation: disable (not hide) controls that need a focus, with a "focus a table to use this" line; group "Summary cards" in the Display group of finding 4.

### 10. Layer mode and cards: detached arcs (minor)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** (the rim arcs fade with the disc when summary cards take over; D-085).
Evidence: `L-9-cards.png`: with cards on in layer mode, the domain-coloured rim arcs of the discs float around and behind the white cards (Raw vault shows an arc 60 px above its card), as the discs are larger than the cards. This reads as a rendering defect to a first-time user.
Recommendation: when cards are live, draw the arcs on the card border, or skip them.

### 11. Stale tooltip while zooming (minor)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** (the universe and lineage-graph tooltips close when a zoom starts and return on the next mouse move; D-085).
Evidence: `L-4-zoomed.png`: after wheel-zooming with a still pointer, the previous domain tooltip ("Double-click: zoom on this domain") stays beside a table, whose actual double-click action is "open in the Detail tab". The tooltip should refresh or close on wheel.
Recommendation: hide the tooltip on wheel and zoom start, and recompute on the next mouse move.

### 12. Cross-view toolbars: same words, different places (minor)
**Status: fixed in M9.1** ("Show all" → **Clear focus**, one **Layout ▾** menu, **?** help in both toolbars; D-084, D-085). **Deferred**: "Find a table" in the detail (its box focuses a table, a different job from the universe's search; labelled "Focus on a table").
Evidence: Fit, Export image and the search box are at the right in all three views, good; but the universe's is "Find a table…" / "Trajectory" button, the detail's "Focus on a table…" + "Clear/Show all" + "Highlight…", the flow has none. "Show all" (detail) clears the focus; "Fit" (all). Layout actions (Reset, Export, Import layout) sit in the tab bar, global but valid only for the current view (reset asks confirm for the current view only), and "Export layout" vs "Export image" look alike.
Recommendation: same verb for the same job: "Find a table" in both views (focus = a way to use it), "Show all" → "Clear focus"; move layout actions into one "Layout ▾" menu (`<details>`) so they stop competing with the tabs.

### 13. Mobile 360 px (minor)
**Status: deferred**: no change in M9.1 (acceptable for a desktop-first tool; opening on layers or cards at 360 px is a behaviour change outside the string-table scope). The new controls keep 360 px without horizontal scroll and 44 px targets.
Evidence: `L-10-mobile.png`: the first view opens with the panel closed and the discs overlap their labels ("raw_dms", "raw_lms" labels stack on neighbouring discs); the toolbar is clean. Reading names is only by zoom.
Recommendation: acceptable for a desktop-first tool; at 360 px open on the layers grouping (4 big discs) or on cards.

## Answers to the review questions

- First contact: the large project's opening picture is readable, but nothing says what to do; the gestures are in a hint under the fold.
- Navigation model: no breadcrumb, three different back mechanisms, no Esc across levels (findings 1, 3).
- Coherence: naming differs between views (Group/Domains, Tables/Columns, Types/Kinds, Trajectory/Flow/Routes) (5, 6, 12).
- Panel complexity: too many peers; group and order them (4).
- Terminology: the metaphor helps the opening picture and hurts the controls; rename the controls to the audience's words (6).
- Tabs: carry-over exists from universe to detail (group and focus), not the way back (3).

## Prioritised shortlist

1. Add a breadcrumb / location line `All › Domain › Table`, with Esc stepping back one level and the Domains rows acting as "go to" (findings 1, 3, 8).
2. At constellation level, hide the table-to-table link hairball (spotlight on hover or select) and show names with the layer headers; this is the missing middle step of the drill-down (2).
3. Rebuild the universe panel as Navigate / Display / Filters, with the hint moved out and the Domains list first (4, 9).
4. Rename the controls in the audience's words: lineage, dependencies, domain, kind, show names/columns, "group satellites into hubs" (5, 6, 12) — string-table change only.
5. Fix the accessibility gaps (unlabelled search inputs) and the stale tooltip on zoom (8, 11); add a back link in the Detail tab after a hand-off from the universe (3).

Decisions needed: renaming the metaphor (6) and a breadcrumb (1) change user-visible behaviour not covered by an approved decision: `NEEDS USER APPROVAL`, recorded in `docs/decisions.md` before the developer starts.
