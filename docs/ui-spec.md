# UI specification

User-visible behaviour of the generated page, kept true by the ui-designer and the documentation agent. Detailed designs (wireframes, exact styles) live in the design notes under `docs/architecture/`; this file states what the user sees and can do. User-facing wording: `docs/user-guide.md`.

## General

- One self-contained page, in English or French (`language`, D-016). Every string comes from the string tables of the scripts (`STRINGS` / `L` in `common.js` for page-wide strings, `T` for one view's own), in both languages.
- Light theme: background `#f5f6f8`, surface `#ffffff`, text `#1f2933`, muted `#5f6774` (5.3:1 on the background, M5.4), one accent `#2f6fb5`.
- **Text on colours reaches WCAG AA, 4.5:1 (D-037)**, whatever colours the config chooses. A table name on its kind colour (universe card, detail and flow header) is white when white reaches 4.5:1, else black: the built-in source and `other` colours, and the link, satellite and fact colours of the presets, take black; the others, the built-in seed red `#c62a36` included (D-042), stay white. A domain name on the universe is drawn in its colour, darkened just enough to reach 4.5:1 on the page background. The detail's column types and "+ N columns" row are the theme grey, darkened on the tints where it falls short. The universe card's column types are muted grey (were `#9aa3af`). Exported images show the same inks.
- **Colour marks reach 3:1 against the page (D-042, WCAG 1.4.11)**, whatever colours the config chooses. Every kind or domain colour drawn as a mark (universe star, card border and header band, domain circle and halo, domain composition bar; every filter, legend and title dot; detail table border and header; flow domain band and kind header) is checked against the page background `#f5f6f8`, hence against white. A colour that reaches 3:1 is drawn exactly as before. A lighter one (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#ffff00`, and the shipped `#94a3b8`, `#8a94a6`, `#c9a227`, `#a8924a`, `#d9822b`, `#4da3ff`) keeps its fill and gets a 1 px outline in the colour darkened toward black just enough to reach 3:1 (same hue); a line, a border or a bar of 6 px or less (card border, domain circle and halo, composition bar, flow domain band, detail table border) is drawn in that darkened colour. Domain circles keep their transparency. Exported images show the same marks.
- **Numbers follow the page language (D-039)**: "1,234" in English, "1 234" (narrow no-break space) in French, in every count, filter count, cap message and depth label.
- **French spacing (D-039)**: in French, a no-break space comes before `:` and a narrow no-break space before `?`, `!` and `;` ("Ciblé : …", "Image créée : …", "… de cet onglet ?"); English has none ("owner: x" in the domain tooltip). Names, descriptions and config labels are shown as written.
- Native controls (`button`, `select`, `input` + `datalist`, `details`). Visible focus.
- **Counts agree in number** (M5.2): "1 table", "2 tables", "1 column", "+ 1 column", "1 relation", "1 table brought back by the lineage". English is singular for 1 only; French for 0 and 1 ("0 table · 0 lien · 0 domaine", "1 colonne"). Counts with no noun after them ("5 upstream", checkbox counts, the card's "+ N") have no noun to agree with. Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-04-m5.2-page-polish.md` § UI design.
- **Touch targets**: on coarse pointers (`pointer: coarse`), every control is at least 44 px high (buttons also 44 px wide): tabs, the Layout menu, ☰, search and focus inputs, toolbar and export buttons, the ? help, the Display group's summary, panel selects, checkbox and radio rows, the all / none links, the lineage bar controls. Fine-pointer (desktop) rendering is unchanged.
- **No horizontal scroll at 360 px**, panel open or closed; checked at 360 and 1280 px. The toolbars and the tabs wrap.
- The ☰ button of each toolbar opens and closes the side panel; it is named "Panel" ("Panneau", M9.1; "Filters" is now a section of the universe panel) and exposes `aria-expanded`. Focus stays on it.
- **Narrow screens (D-035)**: a view that starts in a window 600 px wide or narrower opens with its panel closed (`aria-expanded="false"`), so the drawing fills the screen; the ☰ button, first in the toolbar, reopens it. At that width an open panel sits above the toolbar, full width, scrolling inside itself: at most 45 % of the screen height in the universe, 35 % in the detail (D-038; its toolbar wraps to several rows, so the diagram and a filter's effect stay in view). Nothing is saved and later resizing does not change the state. Wider windows open with the panel beside the drawing, as before.
- **Zoom and pan, the same in every view (M5.10, D-045)**: in the universe, the detail and the flow, the mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, and dragging the background pans, anywhere in the drawing area, over a table or not; a two-finger pinch zooms the drawing, never the page (touch screens, and trackpads: ctrl+wheel in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, Safari's own trackpad gesture events on macOS). One wheel notch is about 1.15×; the scale goes up to 12× real size (and down to 0.01×, or the fit if smaller). Double-click keeps each view's own action (focus in the detail; in the universe, open a table in the detail or zoom onto it or its domain), never the generic double-click zoom. A drag of more than 3 px is a pan, never a click: it does not reset the detail's highlight nor recentre the lineage graph. In the detail, dragging a table still moves it (mouse); on touch a drag on a table pans. In the universe too, tables and domains move with the mouse only: on touch a one-finger drag pans and a pinch zooms wherever it starts, on a table or a domain name included. **Fit** contains and centres the whole drawing. The detail's hint (in its **?** popover since M9.1) reads "Wheel or pinch: zoom · drag the background to pan · …" ("Molette ou pincement : zoom · glisser le fond : déplacer la vue · …"); under a coarse pointer (touch), where a table cannot be moved, it reads "Pinch: zoom · drag: pan · tap a table: highlight its links" ("Pincement : zoom · glisser : déplacer la vue · toucher une table : surligner ses liens").

## Universe view — placement (M5.5, D-040)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-05-m5.5-universe-layout.md`.

- **Layer bands.** Inside each domain, tables sit in layer bands, left to right in the order of the panel's Layers list (in the example project: sources, bronze, silver, gold). Every table of a layer is strictly left of every table of the next layer. Inside a band, a table comes after the tables of its layer it reads, then near its neighbours of the earlier bands, then by name.
- **Tables without a layer.** With configured `layers`, a table that matches no rule ("Other") is placed by lineage, in the band after its latest parent (the first band when it has none). Without configured layers, a folder literally named `other` that sorts last is placed the same way.
- **Room.** Each table reserves what it shows: at the "names" level its star and the name below it, on two lines when the name is longer than 14 characters (split after `__`, else after the `_` nearest the middle; label width estimated, so the shape is the same on every machine), at keys and all-fields levels its card. Reserved boxes never overlap. A band is one column, split into balanced columns when tall; the bands sit side by side and the circle is the smallest around them, with 16 px of room at names level and 24 px around cards.
- **Flat domains.** A circle cannot be narrower than its bands side by side: a domain with a few bands of few wide tables (acme_retail's Customer, or Product at the card levels) forms a wide row and its circle stays partly empty above and below.
- **Names at a distance.** Names keep their 11 px size at every zoom; from afar, an ordinary name that would cover another star or leave its domain's circle is not drawn (it comes back when zooming in). Lineage and hovered names are always offered.
- **Star size by role (M8.1, D-058).** A star's radius is `min(cap, base + slope × √degree)` (degree: relations plus lineage links): core kinds 6 + 1.6√d, at most 16 px; connector and unclassified kinds 3 + 1.6√d, at most 14 px; descriptive and technical kinds 2 + 0.8√d, at most 6 px. Without roles, only sources, seeds and snapshots (technical) are small.
- **Name priority and names at Fit (M8.1).** Names compete by tier (core, then connector and unclassified, then descriptive and technical), then by degree and kind order as before. Below scale 0.6, when a core table is drawn, the only ordinary names offered are the 3 core tables with the most relations in each domain (layer) (fewer when they collide); one with no core table offers none. Without any core table drawn the rule is as before.
- **Table kind list order (M8.1).** Rows follow the role (core, connector, descriptive, technical, none), then the payload order. No role heading, no new string. The detail's list is unchanged.
- **Layer markers.** At domain zoom (scale 0.6 or more), a faint 1 px line (`rgba(100,116,139,.10)`) separates consecutive bands of a domain, clipped to its circle; not drawn from afar, where dependencies speak. No layer names on the canvas.
- **Domain tooltip.** A line lists the domain's bands in order: "Layers: Sources → Bronze → Silver → Gold" in the example project ("Couches : …"); the catch-all band reads "Other" ("Autre").
- **Entities (M8.2, D-063).** A table whose parent passes the filters may be folded into it: only the drawn tables are placed and reserve room (an entity holding tables reserves 6 px more on its top and right for its badge, a card the rows of its folded tables and 4 px under them), while every count (meta line, domain and band counts, composition bars) is over the tables that pass the filters, folded included. Details: § Universe view — entities.
- **Reproducible.** The same project gives the same shapes at every generation and on every machine; unticking and ticking back a domain puts it back in place.
- **Saved arrangements (D-013).** Files and stored arrangements from before M5.5 load unchanged. A pinned domain stays where it was saved; a dragged table keeps its offset from its domain's centre (now the disc centre), so an old offset may sit outside the compact content: the halo grows to include it and computed tables under it are pushed off it. Tables never dragged take the new placement. Where a disc grew, two pinned domains may overlap: the pins win, and **Reset this tab's layout** gives the computed placement. Since M5.13 the layer grouping has its own arrangement next to these (D-048, below).

## Universe view — lineage mode (M1, D-018; shown as "Lineage" since M9.1, D-084)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-03-m1-trajectory-mode.md` § UI design. Code and file names keep "trajectory".

- **Purpose.** From one table, show where its data comes from (upstream) and where it goes (downstream) along the `ref` / `source` lineage, without moving the universe.
- **Entering.** A single click on a table when zoomed in enough to see tables, or the **Lineage** ("Lignage") button next to the search box (disabled, title "Find a table first, or click one on the map", until the box holds an exact table name). Clicking another table moves the lineage. A drag or a pan never enters it.
- **Bar.** Overlaid at the top of the canvas, in this order: "Lineage of" + the start table; **Direction** (both, upstream, downstream; default both); **Depth** (all levels, 1, 2, 3 levels; default all); the counts ("5 upstream · 1 downstream"), which open a per-domain breakdown on click and show it on hover; "N hidden by the filters" when a filter changed in the mode hides lineage tables; "N tables brought back by the lineage" ("1 table brought back by the lineage"); a legend (start, upstream, downstream); **Lineage graph** (opens the lineage graph, below; disabled when the lineage is empty); **Leave**. Direction and depth are kept for the session.
- **Empty state.** "No upstream or downstream table" (or "No upstream table" / "No downstream table" for one direction).
- **Drawing.** The start table has a double dark ring; upstream tables a dashed violet ring (`#6d28d9`), downstream tables a solid burnt-orange ring (`#c2410c`); lineage edges are drawn with arrows along the data flow at every zoom, dashed upstream, whatever **Links** shows. Everything else is dimmed (tables, names, links, dependencies at 20 %; domains without a lineage table at 45 %). Lineage names are placed first.
- **Tooltip.** "Start of the lineage", "Upstream, level n" or "Downstream, level n"; the action line adds "Click: show its lineage".
- **Filters.** Tables of the lineage whose domain, layer or table kind is unticked are brought back (their boxes are ticked again and stay ticked after leaving). A box unticked during the mode is respected until the next lineage change.
- **Fit** frames the lineage while the mode is on: its tables and the discs of the domains (layers) that hold them, inside the area the bar leaves visible.
- **Leaving.** **Leave** ("Leave the lineage (Esc)"), `Esc` (first closes the open breakdown or an open help or Layout popover), or a click on the background. Focus returns to the Lineage button (or the search box) when it was in the bar.
- **Screen readers.** A polite live region announces the start table and counts on each change, the brought-back count, and "Lineage closed".
- Nothing about the lineage is saved (D-013).

## Universe view — lineage graph (M5.3, D-036; shown as "Lineage graph" since M9.1, D-084)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-04-m5.3-flow-view.md` § UI design. Code and file names keep "flow".

- **Purpose.** See the current lineage in data-flow order: upstream on the left, the start, downstream on the right, every arrow pointing right, without changing the universe.
- **Opening.** **Lineage graph** ("Graphe de lignage") in the lineage bar, before **Leave**; title "Show this lineage as a left-to-right graph". Same start, direction and depth as the lineage. Disabled when the lineage has no table for its direction. The graph covers the whole Universe tab (panel included); the universe underneath cannot be reached and does not change.
- **Header**, in order: **← Universe** ("Back to the universe (Esc)"); "Lineage graph of" + the start as a button (kind dot + name, 2 px dark frame; title "Centre the view on the start table" / "Centrer la vue sur la table de départ"; M5.11): it centres the start in the area at the current scale, or 0.75 if smaller, and does nothing while laying out; **Direction** and **Depth** (they change the graph only, never the universe lineage); counts ("24 tables · 22 upstream · 1 downstream", plural agreement, or the empty text "No downstream table"); **N domains** (a list: start / upstream / downstream marks, then each domain present with its colour and table count); the cap message when the graph is limited; **Fit** ("Show the whole graph"); **Export image** PNG / SVG.
- **Tables.** A box per table: the name on the kind colour (as in the universe and the detail), a domain colour band on the left edge, then the depth (`−2` upstream, `+1` downstream) and the layer. The start has a thick dark frame and reads "start" instead of a depth; around it, a dark ring (3 px, 6 px outside the frame, white inside) and a 10 px soft grey glow (the text colour at 14 %) keep the same screen size at every zoom, so the start is found at a glance even at Fit on 150 tables (M5.11, design note `docs/architecture/2026-11-05-m5.11-flow-start.md`). They lie under every table and arrow, and are exported as shown. The Domains list marks "start" with a double dark frame, as the universe marks it with a double ring. Columns follow the lineage, with the configured layers as a floor, so layers form bands where the lineage follows them.
- **Arrows.** Upstream dashed violet (`#6d28d9`), downstream solid burnt orange (`#c2410c`), as in the lineage mode.
- **Initial view.** The whole graph if it is readable (scale 0.75 or more); otherwise scale 0.75 centred on the start. **Fit** always shows the whole graph. Wheel / pinch zoom, drag to pan; tables cannot be dragged.
- **Recentre.** A click (or Enter / Space on a focused table) redraws the graph from that table, with the graph's direction and depth; the header follows. The universe lineage does not move: **← Universe** then **Lineage graph** returns to its start.
- **Tooltip.** Name; domain · layer · kind · N columns; "Upstream, level n" / "Downstream, level n" / "Start of the lineage"; the description; "Click: show its lineage graph" (not on the start). Not shown on touch.
- **Limit.** At most 150 tables. Above that the depth is reduced to the largest level that fits ("Depth reduced to 2 levels: the chosen depth has 412 tables, a lineage graph draws at most 150"), or, when level 1 alone is too large, only the first 149 direct tables are drawn ("Only 149 of the 420 tables at level 1 are drawn (at most 150 per lineage graph)").
- **States.** "Laying out…" while Graphviz runs (export disabled); "Render error: …" with the header still usable; a direction with no table draws the start alone.
- **Keyboard.** On open, focus is on **← Universe**. Tab order: header controls (**← Universe**, the start button, Direction, Depth, Domains, Fit, PNG, SVG), then the tables left to right. Enter or Space on the start button centres the start; no single-key shortcut. `Esc` closes the domain list first, then the graph; it never also leaves the lineage. On close, focus returns to **Lineage graph**.
- **Screen readers.** A polite live region announces "Lineage graph of <table>: <counts>." on open and recentre (with the cap message), and "Lineage graph closed". The graph's tooltip is hidden when a zoom starts (M9.1). Each table is a button named by its name and level.
- **Small screens.** No horizontal scroll at 360 px; the header wraps; 44 px controls on coarse pointers.
- Nothing about the lineage graph is saved (D-013).

## Universe view — organise by layer (M5.13, D-048; "constellation" is the code word, the page says domain or layer since M9.1)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-05-m5.13-group-by-layers.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** Read the estate by tier ("what is in Gold, for which domains?") as well as by domain.
- **Switch.** First in the **Navigate** group of the universe panel: **Organise by** ("Organiser par"), two native radio buttons **Domain** / **Layer** ("Domaine" / "Couche"), each with the number of circles it gives over the whole project. On a fine pointer the radio rows have the height and left edge of the checkbox rows (the radio takes the checkbox's default margin); on a coarse pointer the rows are 44 px. One Tab stop, arrow keys switch. Hidden, and the page stays in Domains, when the Layers list has fewer than two rows.
- **Default and memory.** The page opens in the viewer's last choice when one is stored, else in `universe.constellations` of `dbt-erd.yml` (default `domains`). The choice is stored in localStorage `dbtscope-constellations:<title>` only when it differs from the config default (choosing the default again removes it); it is never exported; without storage the switch works for the session.
- **Layer mode.** One circle per layer, left to right in the flow order of the Layers list. Inside each, tables sit in domain bands in the order of the Domains panel, packed as in domain mode. The disc and its name are neutral (muted outline, text-colour name); the name, count line and composition bar are raised 8 px (`NAME_LIFT`), so the bar ends 4 px above the rim arcs; each band is tinted at 6 % in its domain colour (opaque, clipped to the disc) and marked by a 4 px arc of the disc's upper rim in its domain colour darkened to 3:1 (D-042), at every zoom with cards off; with summary cards the arcs fade with the disc (M9.1, D-085); faint separators at scale 0.6 or more, as in domain mode. At scale 0.6 or more each band shows its domain name (11 px, its colour darkened to 4.5:1 on its tint), when it fits and does not collide. The Domains panel's dots are the legend. Meta line: "N tables · N links · N layers" ("couches").
- **Tooltip.** Over a layer: its label, its table count, "Domains: Sales · Finance · …" (first 12, then "+ N"), over a band "Domain: Sales · 42 tables", and "Double-click: zoom on this layer" ("Domaines : …", "Domaine : …", "Double-clic : cadrer cette couche"). The domain-mode tooltip is unchanged.
- **Switching** is instant and framed by Fit (the lineage when it is on); the hover and the search mark are cleared, as on any change of Links or Show. Filters, search, the lineage, the lineage graph, double-click and image export behave the same in both modes.
- **Saved arrangements.** Each grouping keeps its own (scopes `layers:names`, `layers:keys`, `layers:all` in the `universe` section, D-013 format unchanged), and, while the descriptive tables are folded, `layers:folded:names|keys|all` (M8.2, D-064); a drag in one never moves the other; **Reset this tab's layout** clears all.
- **Names without a label.** A domain, layer or kind whose rule sets `label: null` is shown by its key everywhere (filter rows, tooltips, canvas, flow view), through the one rule `common.js labelOf`. Before M5.13 such a layer showed nothing in the panel and "null" in the tooltip and the flow.
- **Hint** (in the **?** popover since M9.1). "… drag a table, or a domain (layer) by its name, to arrange them · … double-click a domain (layer) to zoom in, a table to open its detail" ("… ou un domaine (une couche) par son nom … double-clic sur un domaine (une couche) pour le cadrer …"), by the current grouping.

## Top bar — Layout menu (M9.1, D-085)

- One **Layout ▾** ("Disposition") menu, a native `<details>` at the right of the tab bar, holds **Reset this tab's layout**, **Export layout**, **Import layout** (fr "Réinitialiser la disposition de cet onglet", "Exporter la disposition", "Importer une disposition"). They act on the manual arrangement of the page (D-013); they never export an image. The menu closes after an action, on `Esc` (focus back on its summary) or a press outside.

## Image export (M2, D-021)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-03-m2-export-offline.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** Put the current view into a slide, a document or a ticket, exactly as it is on screen.
- **Control.** At the right end of each tab's toolbar, after **Fit**: the muted label **Export image** ("Exporter l'image") and one button per format, **PNG** in the universe, **PNG** and **SVG** in the detail. A native button group named "Export image"; tooltips "Save the view as shown as a PNG image (high resolution)", "Save the visible diagram as a PNG image (high resolution)", "Save the visible diagram as an SVG image (vector, opens in any browser)".
- **What is exported.** (A skeleton, M8.5, is exported as drawn: its folded rows and its columns.) The drawing as it is on screen: zoom, pan, filters, detail level, search mark, highlight, trajectory rings and dimming. Not the panels, toolbars, trajectory bar, tooltips or hover. No caption or legend is added. Background filled with the page background.
- **Formats.** Universe PNG and detail PNG at twice the screen resolution (lowered on very large screens to stay within browser limits); detail SVG cut to the visible area, styles inlined, opens alone in a browser.
- **File name.** `<page title>-universe.png`, `<page title>-detail.png`, `<page title>-detail.svg`, and for the flow view `<page title>-flow.png` / `-flow.svg` (M5.3); the title keeps ASCII letters, digits, `-` and `_` (accents dropped), `dbtscope` if nothing is left.
- **States.** Disabled while nothing is drawn (no table for the filters; detail laying out or in error). While an export runs, the clicked button ignores further clicks (`aria-disabled`, progress cursor) and keeps focus. Success: a polite live region in the top bar says "Image created: <file>" ("Image créée : <file>", no-break space). Failure: an alert "The image could not be created." ("L'image n'a pas pu être créée."), nothing is downloaded.
- **Keyboard.** In tab order after Fit; Enter or Space exports; no shortcut. Focus stays on the button.
- **Small screens.** The group wraps as one piece with the toolbar; no horizontal scroll at 360 px. 44 px targets on coarse pointers.

## Offline page (M2)

- Built with `--offline` or `offline: true`. The page looks and behaves exactly like the default one and shows nothing about it.

## Universe view — entities (M8.2, D-063)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-12-m8.2-entities.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** On a large Data Vault, read the estate as its entities (hubs, links, dimensions, facts) and keep satellites, ref sats and PITs one step away.
- **Entity and mark.** A descriptive table with a parent (its core or connector table in the same domain and layer, computed by dbtscope from relations then names) is drawn inside that table instead of as its own star. The parent is an **entity**; when it holds at least one table its star carries a mark: from scale 0.6 a **badge**, a dark disc (`#1f2933`, white 1.5 px outline, 13.6:1 on the page) on the upper right of the star, 7 px with the count in white (99+ above 99); from afar (below 0.6) a thin dark ring (`#1f2933`, 1.25 px wide, 1.5 px off the star, tighter than every trajectory ring) so the kind colour of the star still shows. A card keeps a 4 px dot from afar. The star's size is unchanged. In both groupings, at every zoom and in image export; trajectory rings, the start's double ring and the search ring stay visible around it. On cards the badge sits on the top-right corner.
- **Tooltip.** An entity lists its folded tables, name and kind, 12 at most then "+ N" ("3 folded tables" / "3 tables repliées"). A table that has a parent but is drawn shows "Folds into: <parent>".
- **Cards.** At keys and all fields, folded tables are rows under the columns (name, kind label in grey at the right), 12 at most then "+ N".
- **Fold control (M9.1 wording, D-084).** Panel, first row of **More display options** (Display group): one checkbox "Draw Satellite · PIT inside their table" ("Dessiner Satellite · PIT dans leur table", the descriptive kinds with a parent, from the roles; the full sentence in its `title`; the row may wrap) with the number of tables with a parent. Checked = folded (drawn inside); space toggles; 44 px on coarse pointers. Hidden when no table has a parent (the page is then as before). Default `universe.fold` (default true); the viewer's choice is kept in localStorage `dbtscope-fold:<title>` when it differs from the default, never exported. Changing it relayouts and fits, like the Organise by switch, and is announced to screen readers.
- **Counts never drop because of a fold.** The meta line counts every table that passes the filters, folded included, and says how many are drawn as entities while folding is on: "1,419 tables (428 entities drawn) · 2,621 links · 12 domains" ("1 419 tables (428 entités dessinées)"). Domain and layer counts, composition bars, band counts and the Table kind counts are the same folded or shown. Dependencies between domains carry the links of folded tables; no link of a folded table is drawn inside a domain.
- **Reach.** Searching a folded table opens its entity (one at a time), goes to the table, marks it and announces it ("car_unlinked_sat is folded into car_hub: shown"); a lineage shows its tables, the start included, unfolded, and gives back the folded drawing when left (the camera stays anchored on the clicked table when it unfolds or folds members: no refit, the table stays under the pointer; Fit still frames the lineage); double-click on an entity opens its detail as before.
- **Filters.** Unticking a kind or layer of folded tables lowers the entity's count and the meta line's tables as any filter does. Unticking the entity's own kind draws its folded tables as stars.
- **Saved arrangements (D-064).** The folded drawing has its own arrangement (scopes `folded:*` and `layers:folded:*`, D-013 format unchanged); an offset saved while shown applies again when shown; older files are arrangements of the shown drawing; **Reset this tab's layout** clears all.

## Universe view — summary cards (M8.3, D-065, D-066; optional since M8.6, D-081)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-12-m8.3-summary-cards.md` § UI design; the switch: `docs/architecture/2026-11-14-m8.6-cards-switch.md`.

- **Switch (M8.6, D-081).** The cards are **off by default**. A checkbox **Summary cards** ("Fiches de synthèse", the glossary's French word for a summary card) sits in the universe panel, last row of **More display options**, always shown, with a `title` ("From afar, draw each domain as a card: its counts by layer and its main tables"). Ticked, everything below applies; unticked, no card and no group card at any zoom: stars, names and dependencies as before M8.3, each source system its own neutral disc, no bundled dependency. It is a native checkbox (Tab, Space, the label is clickable; 44 px under a coarse pointer). Toggling redraws at once, runs no layout and never moves the camera; the change is announced in the live region ("Summary cards on: they replace the stars from afar" / "Summary cards off: stars at every zoom"; French: "Fiches de synthèse activées : …" / "… désactivées : …"). The page opens as `universe.cards` says (default false); the viewer's choice is kept in `dbtscope-cards:<title>`, only when it differs from that default, never exported, kept by Reset this tab's layout. The text summary below stays at every scale either way.
- **Labels without room (M8.6, D-081).** A domain label (name, count, composition bar) whose place is already taken by another label is not drawn: business domains first, then source systems in grid order. The name stays in the tooltip, the Domains panel and the text summary. On the large project with cards off no two labels overlap. A label is painted over the dependencies, with a halo in the page colour under its letters and its bar. It slides sideways to the nearest place that keeps it inside the canvas and within 1 px of any other disc, when there is one (the centred place otherwise); the source-systems grid keeps 40 world px under each disc for the next one's label. At 1280 × 800 no label reaches more than 2 px into another disc (the 6 px of the first build are gone); at 360 px a label may still reach up to 25 px into one.
- **Purpose.** On a large project, read each domain (layer) at a glance, without zooming: how big, how it is made, its main tables, where to zoom.
- **When.** Below the scale where a core star is drawn smaller than about 3 px, each domain is drawn as a **summary card** instead of its stars; the change is continuous (stars fade out and cards fade in over about three wheel notches, from 3 px down to 2 px) and works both ways, with no jump of the camera. With the box ticked the large project opens on cards (12 at M8.3, 6 since M8.4: five business cards and the group card of the seven source systems); acme_retail opens on stars, as before, at 1280 × 800. A narrow screen, or zooming out past Fit, can show cards on a small project too.
- **Card.** A white rounded rectangle on the constellation's place, 1.8 times its radius wide and at most as high (the height is its content's; it stays centred) (so zooming in lands on its stars), its corners rounded by 0.4 radius, at most 28 px or 0.24 radius on screen so they stay inside the constellation's collision radius and the text can be inset from them. Top to bottom: the name (domain mode: in its colour, darkened to 4.5:1; layer mode: text colour), "299 tables · 152 entities" (entities only while the descriptive tables are folded, and never "0 entities"), one row per layer present in flow order (one per domain in layer mode, with its colour dot): label, a 6 px bar split by kind in Table kind order (colours reach 3:1, D-042; the widest row fills the bar width; a kind is never narrower than 2 px), and the count right-aligned; then "Main tables" (shown with at least 2 names, or its only one; no block otherwise) with up to 5 core tables, the ones with the most relations, each with its kind dot (none when the domain has no core table). Text keeps a fixed size (13 px name, 11 px other lines) at every zoom. Border: the domain's colour darkened to 3:1 (domain mode), muted grey (layer mode).
- **Small cards.** What does not fit is dropped, in this order: the main tables (their heading first), the rows beyond those that fit (the last line reads "+ 2 layers" / "+ 2 domains"), the bars, the entity count and the word "tables", then the rows; the name is always kept. On the large project at 1280 × 800 the five business-domain cards show every row and at least 2 main tables; source systems show their rows only.
- **Pointer.** From half-way through the fade, the pointer acts on cards. Hover: the domain or layer tooltip, a row adds "Click: zoom on Raw vault" ("Clic : cadrer Raw vault") and a light tint with a pointer cursor; the card's border thickens. Click on a row: the camera frames that layer (or domain) band of the domain and lands on stars. Double-click on a card: zoom on the domain, landing on stars. Drag by the header: moves and pins the domain exactly as dragging its name. A click on a card outside its rows does nothing. Rows are canvas shortcuts, 14 px high (18 px on a coarse pointer), not controls: pinch, double-tap and search remain the touch path.
- **Filters** apply to every count, row, bar and main table; a domain with no passing table has no card. Folding and grouping changes recompute the cards; no count drops because of a fold.
- **Search and lineage.** Search lands past the card scale, on the table. A lineage seen from afar gives each card holding members a 2 px dark frame and its counts ("5 upstream · 2 downstream"), the start's card a double frame, the other cards at 45 %, and one arrow per pair of cards (dashed violet upstream, solid orange downstream, thicker for more links, with the count from 2 links up) between the cards.
- **Dependencies.** They run under the cards; their arrowhead sits in the gap between two domains. In layer mode with cards on, the rim arcs fade with the disc (M9.1, D-085); the card's rows are the legend.
- **Screen readers.** A hidden "Summary of the domains" ("Synthèse des domaines"; "of the layers" / "des couches" in layer mode) section lists, for each domain, its counts, one line per row ("Raw vault: 266 tables — Hub 60, Link 70, …") and its main tables, never cut; present at every scale, rebuilt on each change of filter, grouping or fold, not announced. The canvas stays pointer-driven; search, the Lineage button and Fit remain the keyboard paths.
- **Small screens.** No horizontal scroll at 360 px; the cards are smaller, so only the largest show rows; pinch zooms in to readable cards.
- **Image export** shows the cards, the cross-fade and the lineage marks as on screen, without the hover.

## Universe view — source systems and dependencies (M8.4, D-068 to D-071; "route" is the code word, the page says "dependency" since M9.1)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-12-m8.4-systems-routes.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** Tell the business domains from the technical systems that feed them, and read which domain depends on which, without zooming.
- **Source systems.** A domain made only of technical tables (sources, seeds, snapshots by default), or declared `system: true` on its domain rule, is a source system. In domains mode it is drawn neutral (grey halo, rim and name, 12 px) and placed in a grid on the left, level with the domains it feeds, about 22 px (at Fit) from the business domains; business domains are placed along the data flow, a domain mostly read by others left of its readers. Pinned domains stay where saved; **Reset this tab's layout** gives the new placement. Layer mode is unchanged. A project with no source system, or only source systems, looks as before.
- **Group card.** With summary cards on, at the summary scale two or more source systems are one white card with a grey border: "Source systems", "7 source systems · 504 tables" ("7 systèmes sources"), one row per system (label, kind bar when it fits, count), "+ N source systems" when rows do not fit. It cross-fades with the stars like any card. A click on a row zooms on that system; double-click zooms on the grid; a header drag moves and pins all. Zoomed in, each system is its own neutral disc.
- **Domains panel.** With source systems the list has two headings, "Business domains" and "Source systems" ("Domaines métier", "Systèmes sources"), each with its own **all · none** (distinct accessible names); otherwise one heading "Domains", as before.
- **Dependencies.** Drawn edge to edge, curved around the other domains, 1 to 8 px wide by weight, with an arrowhead at the reader's end on lineage dependencies (both ends when each reads the other, none for relations only). By default each domain shows its three strongest (ties kept); a source system shows at most its three heaviest, no tie (weight, then the other end's name) and none of weight 1; one that cannot be cleared of the other domains is not drawn; those of source systems are bundled through the group card at the summary scale. While some are hidden the meta line ends with "18 of 40 dependencies" ("18 dépendances sur 40") and drops its "N links" entry, so it stays on one line (cut with an ellipsis if still too long, the whole text in its tooltip); the **all (40)** toggle ("toutes (40)", accessible name "Show all 40 dependencies") on the Dependencies line (session only; disabled with its reason when nothing is hidden, in layer mode with nothing hidden, or while dependencies are off, M9.1, D-085) draws them all, and so does the spotlight for its domain. The domain tooltip sits 12 px below and right of the pointer, flipped left or above at the stage edges, and away from the hovered card when one of those places is clear of it.
- **Dependencies switch (M8.7, D-083; M9.1 wording and place, D-084, D-085).** A checkbox **Dependencies between domains** ("Dépendances entre domaines"; "between layers" / "entre couches" in layer mode), ticked by default (`universe.routes`), in **More display options** after the Links select, its **all (N)** toggle on its own line below. Unticked: no dependency or arrowhead at any zoom, cards on or off; no tooltip; hovering or focusing a domain dims nothing; the meta line drops "N of M dependencies" and keeps "N links" (its height is unchanged); the map and camera do not move. Lineage arrows and the text summary's dependency lists stay. The row is hidden when the page has no dependency. Announced in the live region; the choice is a viewer key (`dbtscope-routes:<title>`), never exported.
- **Hover and focus.** Over a domain, or on its Domains or Layers row (hover or keyboard focus), all its dependencies are drawn dark, its neighbours stay bright and the rest dims; nothing moves. Over a dependency, a tooltip gives its ends ("raw_crm → Vehicle"), "12 relations · 30 lineage links", who reads whom, and for a bundled route "From: crm 40 · erp 32 · + 3".
- **Lineage** keeps its dimming and its own arrows (now curved); the spotlight is off. **Export** shows curves, arrowheads, the group card and the density in force, without the spotlight or tooltips.
- **Screen readers.** `#u-summary` adds a "Source systems" block and each domain's "Dependencies" list (all of them, heaviest first, with direction).
- **Small screens.** Panel closed at start; the group card keeps header ("Sources", the short word, under 60 px of inner width; cut with an ellipsis below what it needs), count and one or two rows; the meta line wraps (never cut; a domain label whose centre is on the canvas is shifted inside it); no horizontal scroll at 360 px; 44 px rows and links on coarse pointers.
- **As built (M8.4).** Route ink `rgba(71,85,105,.6)` (the design's .5 left the thin business routes unreadable on the large project at Fit; the arrowhead ink is `.8`, the spotlight ink `rgba(31,41,51,.85)`). The group card is as wide as one system card and as high as its content (at most the grid's height), centred on the grid, so the gap from its border to the business cards is wider than the grid gap at Fit (about 150 px on the large project at 1280 × 800); the arrowheads still have their full tail. Routes clear a constellation's disc and card square plus 20 world px. The universe's count line wraps (D-082; its tooltip holds it whole) in a box whose min-height is that of the higher of its two forms, with or without "18 of 40 routes": crossing the scale where routes appear never changes the toolbar's height, so the stage is not resized. The Domains panel's all / none links are keyboard stops.

## Detail view — skeleton (M8.5, D-072 to D-076)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-12-m8.5-detail-skeleton.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** Read a large group (over 60 tables) as its entities and how they connect, and reach every table.
- **When.** No focus, more than 60 tables in the group's scope, at least one table whose parent (D-062, the same as the universe's) is in the scope, and the viewer's choice "folded" (default). Smaller groups and groups without parents are drawn as before; acme_retail's groups are.
- **Skeleton.** Core and connector tables, plus tables with no parent, laid out in several columns (as many as the drawing area's shape asks for; referenced tables left, referencing right). At the keys level a table is its header and, when it holds tables, one muted italic row "+ 3 tables drawn inside · Satellite" ("+ 3 tables incluses · Satellite"; "Satellite 3 · PIT 1" when several kinds; labels from the config, never pluralised). **Show: all columns** still draws every column. Relations of folded tables are not drawn. The table's tooltip lists the folded tables (12, then "+ N") and its column count ("12 columns · 3 keys").
- **Meta line.** It wraps, in the toolbar, to its own row when the controls leave less than 20 em, so no count is lost at any width (the toolbar grows, the stage keeps that size while zooming, and a drawing at Fit is contained in the room left; before the fix of 2026-11-15 it was one line cut with an ellipsis). "136 of 271 tables shown · 135 drawn inside · 214 relations · dashed outline = other domain" ("135 incluses"; "other group" when the page says Group); its tooltip splits by kind, "Hub 70 · Link 66 · drawn inside: Satellite 135". The scope count never drops because of the fold. Highlight lights a table whose folded tables match.
- **Control.** Detail panel, heading "Descriptive tables" ("Tables descriptives"), between Domain / Group and Around the focused table: one checkbox "Draw Satellite · PIT inside their table" with its count, as in the universe. Shown only when the group qualifies; disabled while a table is focused, the reason ("A focus draws every table") a visible line (M9.1). Own choice, kept in localStorage `dbtscope-detail-fold:<title>` only when it differs from the default, never exported. Unchecked: "271 tables: the drawing will be hard to read" ("271 tables : le schéma sera difficile à lire"), a status line under the row. A **Draw inside their table** ("Dessiner dans leur table") button under the warning does the same and leaves the focus on the checkbox. Changing it refits; screen readers hear "Descriptive tables folded: 136 tables drawn" or, when the warning does not appear, "Descriptive tables shown".
- **Focus.** A focused table never folds: everything around it is drawn in full. A connector (role `connector`, e.g. Link) crossed on the way costs no level, so at "direct links" a hub shows its satellites (those folded by name only too, D-080), its links and the hubs across them. A note under the depth select says it: "Passing through Link costs no level" ("Passer par Lien ne coûte aucun niveau"), shown whenever the scope has a connector kind (acme_retail too).
- **Arrangements (D-075, D-078).** A skeleton has its own arrangement (key part `|fold`), the shown drawing of a large group too (`|grid`); other keys are unchanged; Reset this tab's layout clears all.
- **Export.** SVG and PNG show the skeleton as on screen.
- **Small screens.** 360 px: no horizontal scroll, panel at 35 vh with the Entities block in view; Fit gives the shape, names are read by pinching in.
- **As built (M8.5).** The layout is a grid dealt by the page (D-074), not dot's own ranking: the measure on the large project's `vehicle` group (277 tables, 138 drawn) gave a strip of 1,236 × 6,984 pt with dot, and 2,253 × 1,368 pt (W / H 1.65, 8 columns in 4 pairs [hubs | links], names 10.7 px after three wheel notches at 1280 × 800, 0.3 s) with the grid. Neato only draws the relations; for more than 5,000 tables × relations they are straight lines, grey at half opacity (D-074). The shown 277 tables use the same grid (D-078). The detail toolbar keeps one row at 1280 px: `#d-meta` is one line cut with an ellipsis (its title holds it whole), `#d-focus` and `#d-q` shrink. At 360 px the Around-the-focused-table and Columns blocks stay open: the panel is closed at start, capped at 35 vh and scrolls inside itself, and a `<details>` would need a second markup for the wide layout. The `#d-fold` fieldset carries its own `margin-top:16px` (the group select above has no bottom margin), the warning `#d-fold-warn` is empty and zero high when there is nothing to say, and the depth note keeps `.hint` with a 6 px top margin. A name-only parent (a satellite folded by name with no relation, D-062) is not a neighbour of its parent: a focus on the hub does not draw it, a focus on the satellite shows it alone.

## Universe view — drill-down (M9.2, D-087 to D-090)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-17-m9.2-drill-down.md` § UI design. Supersedes: "No layer names on the canvas" (§ placement) for an entered domain only; the double-click "zoom on this domain / layer" (§ General, tooltips: now "Click: enter this domain / layer"); a click on a card outside its rows doing nothing (§ summary cards); the counts line in the toolbar (it moves to the top of Navigate, and stays the root crumb's `title`).

- **Levels.** Map, entered domain (layer), table, then the Detail tab. Page state only: never saved, exported or in the URL; every load opens on the map.
- **Map page** (more than `universe.map_above` tables, default 300): opens on named discs (name, count, composition bar), no star or table name from afar; small projects open as before.
- **Entering**: click or double-click a disc, a card, a ghost, a panel row's name, a search, or zoom into it. The domain's tables spread over the stage (300 ms tween, none under reduced motion), headers per layer (domain mode) or per domain (layer mode), "Raw vault 388", sticky. No link until a table is hovered or selected ("Hover a table to see its links" in the corner). The other domains are labelled **ghosts** at the stage edges (88 px left and right, 72 px top and bottom) with one dependency line each; click enters. No dragging inside; nothing saved.
- **Breadcrumb** `All domains › Vehicle › car_hub` (`All layers › …`) in the toolbar after ☰ Panel: `nav` "Location", every segment a button, the last `aria-current="location"`, one line that shrinks with ellipsis (middle segments first), never changes the toolbar's height; `title`s hold the counts. Ancestors are `--accent` links, the current segment `--fg` 600.
- **Esc** goes one level back: table, then domain, then nothing; popovers, the lineage graph, the search box and selects keep their own Esc first. In the Detail tab Esc follows the trail's last segment.
- **Panel rows** of Domains, Source systems (Layers in layer mode): the checkbox filters ("Show Vehicle"), the name is a button that enters ("Go to Vehicle"); the entered row has `aria-current` and a left accent bar. 44 px on touch.
- **Detail trail** `← All domains › Vehicle › car_hub`, every segment a button back to that level; opening the Detail tab shows the entered domain's group (domain mode, when groups are domains) and the trail.
- **Zoom** follows the location with hysteresis (enter when a disc fills 90 % of the stage's short side, leave below 0.6 of the spread's fit); one change per gesture.
- **Touch**: tap enters, pinch out leaves, the hint has a touch wording. 360 px: no horizontal scroll at any level; ghosts shrink and lose their labels.
- **Keyboard**: panel row names and the search box are the way in (canvas discs are not Tab stops); crumbs and Esc the way out; each change is announced in `#u-live`.
