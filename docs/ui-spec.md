# UI specification

User-visible behaviour of the generated page, kept true by the ui-designer and the documentation agent. Detailed designs (wireframes, exact styles) live in the design notes under `docs/architecture/`; this file states what the user sees and can do. User-facing wording: `docs/user-guide.md`.

## General

- One self-contained page, in English or French (`language`, D-016). Every string comes from the string tables of the scripts (`STRINGS` / `L` in `common.js` for page-wide strings, `T` for one view's own), in both languages.
- Light theme: background `#f5f6f8`, surface `#ffffff`, text `#1f2933`, muted `#5f6774` (5.3:1 on the background, M5.4), one accent `#2f6fb5`.
- **Text on colours reaches WCAG AA, 4.5:1 (D-037)**, whatever colours the config chooses. A table name on its kind colour (universe card, detail and flow header) is white when white reaches 4.5:1, else black: the built-in source and `other` colours, and the link, satellite and fact colours of the presets, take black; the others, the built-in seed red `#c62a36` included (D-042), stay white. A domain name on the universe is drawn in its colour, darkened just enough to reach 4.5:1 on the page background. The detail's column types and "+ N columns" row are the theme grey, darkened on the tints where it falls short. The universe card's column types are muted grey (were `#9aa3af`). Exported images show the same inks.
- **Colour marks reach 3:1 against the page (D-042, WCAG 1.4.11)**, whatever colours the config chooses. Every kind or domain colour drawn as a mark (universe star, card border and header band, domain circle and halo, domain composition bar; every filter, legend and title dot; detail table border and header; flow domain band and kind header) is checked against the page background `#f5f6f8`, hence against white. A colour that reaches 3:1 is drawn exactly as before. A lighter one (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#ffff00`, and the shipped `#94a3b8`, `#8a94a6`, `#c9a227`, `#a8924a`, `#d9822b`, `#4da3ff`) keeps its fill and gets a 1 px outline in the colour darkened toward black just enough to reach 3:1 (same hue); a line, a border or a bar of 6 px or less (card border, domain circle and halo, composition bar, flow domain band, detail table border) is drawn in that darkened colour. Domain circles keep their transparency. Exported images show the same marks.
- **Numbers follow the page language (D-039)**: "1,234" in English, "1 234" (narrow no-break space) in French, in every count, filter count, cap message and depth label.
- **French spacing (D-039)**: in French, a no-break space comes before `:` and a narrow no-break space before `?`, `!` and `;` ("Ciblé : …", "Image créée : …", "… de cet onglet ?"); English has none ("owner: x" in the domain tooltip). Names, descriptions and config labels are shown as written.
- Native controls (`button`, `select`, `input` + `datalist`, `details`). Visible focus.
- **Counts agree in number** (M5.2): "1 table", "2 tables", "1 column", "+ 1 column", "1 relation", "1 table brought back by the trajectory". English is singular for 1 only; French for 0 and 1 ("0 table · 0 lien · 0 domaine", "1 colonne"). Counts with no noun after them ("5 upstream", checkbox counts, the card's "+ N") have no noun to agree with. Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-04-m5.2-page-polish.md` § UI design.
- **Touch targets**: on coarse pointers (`pointer: coarse`), every control is at least 44 px high (buttons also 44 px wide): tabs, layout buttons, ☰, search and focus inputs, toolbar and export buttons, panel selects, checkbox and radio rows, the all / none links, the trajectory bar controls. Fine-pointer (desktop) rendering is unchanged.
- **No horizontal scroll at 360 px**, panel open or closed; checked at 360 and 1280 px. The toolbars, the tabs and the layout buttons wrap.
- The ☰ button of each toolbar opens and closes the side panel; it is named "Filters" ("Filtres") and exposes `aria-expanded`. Focus stays on it.
- **Narrow screens (D-035)**: a view that starts in a window 600 px wide or narrower opens with its panel closed (`aria-expanded="false"`), so the drawing fills the screen; the ☰ button, first in the toolbar, reopens it. At that width an open panel sits above the toolbar, full width, scrolling inside itself: at most 45 % of the screen height in the universe, 35 % in the detail (D-038; its toolbar wraps to several rows, so the diagram and a filter's effect stay in view). Nothing is saved and later resizing does not change the state. Wider windows open with the panel beside the drawing, as before.
- **Zoom and pan, the same in every view (M5.10, D-045)**: in the universe, the detail and the flow, the mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, and dragging the background pans, anywhere in the drawing area, over a table or not; a two-finger pinch zooms the drawing, never the page (touch screens, and trackpads: ctrl+wheel in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, Safari's own trackpad gesture events on macOS). One wheel notch is about 1.15×; the scale goes up to 12× real size (and down to 0.01×, or the fit if smaller). Double-click keeps each view's own action (focus in the detail; in the universe, open a table in the detail or zoom onto it or its domain), never the generic double-click zoom. A drag of more than 3 px is a pan, never a click: it does not reset the detail's highlight nor recentre the flow. In the detail, dragging a table still moves it (mouse); on touch a drag on a table pans. In the universe too, tables and domains move with the mouse only: on touch a one-finger drag pans and a pinch zooms wherever it starts, on a table or a domain name included. **Fit** contains and centres the whole drawing. The detail's hint reads "Wheel or pinch: zoom · drag the background to pan · …" ("Molette ou pincement : zoom · glisser le fond : déplacer la vue · …"); under a coarse pointer (touch), where a table cannot be moved, it reads "Pinch: zoom · drag: pan · tap a table: highlight its links" ("Pincement : zoom · glisser : déplacer la vue · toucher une table : surligner ses liens").

## Universe view — placement (M5.5, D-040)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-05-m5.5-universe-layout.md`.

- **Layer bands.** Inside each domain, tables sit in layer bands, left to right in the order of the panel's Layers list (in the example project: sources, bronze, silver, gold). Every table of a layer is strictly left of every table of the next layer. Inside a band, a table comes after the tables of its layer it reads, then near its neighbours of the earlier bands, then by name.
- **Tables without a layer.** With configured `layers`, a table that matches no rule ("Other") is placed by lineage, in the band after its latest parent (the first band when it has none). Without configured layers, a folder literally named `other` that sorts last is placed the same way.
- **Room.** Each table reserves what it shows: at names level its star and the name below it, on two lines when the name is longer than 14 characters (split after `__`, else after the `_` nearest the middle; label width estimated, so the shape is the same on every machine), at keys and all-fields levels its card. Reserved boxes never overlap. A band is one column, split into balanced columns when tall; the bands sit side by side and the circle is the smallest around them, with 16 px of room at names level and 24 px around cards.
- **Flat domains.** A circle cannot be narrower than its bands side by side: a domain with a few bands of few wide tables (acme_retail's Customer, or Product at the card levels) forms a wide row and its circle stays partly empty above and below.
- **Names at a distance.** Names keep their 11 px size at every zoom; from afar, an ordinary name that would cover another star or leave its domain's circle is not drawn (it comes back when zooming in). Trajectory and hovered names are always offered.
- **Star size by role (M8.1, D-058).** A star's radius is `min(cap, base + slope × √degree)` (degree: relations plus lineage links): core kinds 6 + 1.6√d, at most 16 px; connector and unclassified kinds 3 + 1.6√d, at most 14 px; descriptive and technical kinds 2 + 0.8√d, at most 6 px. Without roles, only sources, seeds and snapshots (technical) are small.
- **Name priority and names at Fit (M8.1).** Names compete by tier (core, then connector and unclassified, then descriptive and technical), then by degree and kind order as before. Below scale 0.6, when a core table is drawn, the only ordinary names offered are the 3 core tables with the most relations in each constellation (fewer when they collide); a constellation with no core table offers none. Without any core table drawn the rule is as before.
- **Types list order (M8.1).** Rows follow the role (core, connector, descriptive, technical, none), then the payload order. No role heading, no new string. The detail's list is unchanged.
- **Layer markers.** At constellation zoom (scale 0.6 or more), a faint 1 px line (`rgba(100,116,139,.10)`) separates consecutive bands of a domain, clipped to its circle; not drawn from afar, where routes speak. No layer names on the canvas.
- **Domain tooltip.** A line lists the domain's bands in order: "Layers: Sources → Bronze → Silver → Gold" in the example project ("Couches : …"); the catch-all band reads "Other" ("Autre").
- **Entities (M8.2, D-063).** A table whose parent passes the filters may be folded into it: only the drawn tables are placed and reserve room (an entity holding tables reserves 6 px more on its top and right for its badge, a card the rows of its folded tables and 4 px under them), while every count (meta line, domain and band counts, composition bars) is over the tables that pass the filters, folded included. Details: § Universe view — entities.
- **Reproducible.** The same project gives the same shapes at every generation and on every machine; unticking and ticking back a domain puts it back in place.
- **Saved arrangements (D-013).** Files and stored arrangements from before M5.5 load unchanged. A pinned domain stays where it was saved; a dragged table keeps its offset from its domain's centre (now the disc centre), so an old offset may sit outside the compact content: the halo grows to include it and computed tables under it are pushed off it. Tables never dragged take the new placement. Where a disc grew, two pinned domains may overlap: the pins win, and **Reset layout** gives the computed placement. Since M5.13 the layer grouping has its own arrangement next to these (D-048, below).

## Universe view — trajectory mode (M1, D-018)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-03-m1-trajectory-mode.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** From one table, show where its data comes from (upstream) and where it goes (downstream) along the `ref` / `source` lineage, without moving the universe.
- **Entering.** A single click on a table when zoomed in enough to see tables, or the **Trajectory** button next to the search box (disabled until the box holds an exact table name). Clicking another table moves the trajectory. A drag or a pan never enters it.
- **Bar.** Overlaid at the top of the canvas, in this order: "Trajectory of" + the start table; **Direction** (both, upstream, downstream; default both); **Depth** (all levels, 1, 2, 3 levels; default all); the counts ("5 upstream · 1 downstream"), which open a per-domain breakdown on click and show it on hover; "N hidden by the filters" when a filter changed in the mode hides trajectory tables; "N tables brought back by the trajectory" ("1 table brought back by the trajectory"); a legend (start, upstream, downstream); **Flow** (opens the flow view, below; disabled when the trajectory is empty); **Leave**. Direction and depth are kept for the session.
- **Empty state.** "No upstream or downstream table" (or "No upstream table" / "No downstream table" for one direction).
- **Drawing.** The start table has a double dark ring; upstream tables a dashed violet ring (`#6d28d9`), downstream tables a solid burnt-orange ring (`#c2410c`); trajectory lineage edges are drawn with arrows along the data flow at every zoom, dashed upstream, whatever **Links** shows. Everything else is dimmed (tables, names, links, routes at 20 %; domains without a trajectory table at 45 %). Trajectory names are placed first.
- **Tooltip.** "Start of the trajectory", "Upstream, level n" or "Downstream, level n"; the action line adds "Click: show its trajectory".
- **Filters.** Tables of the trajectory whose domain, layer or type is unticked are brought back (their boxes are ticked again and stay ticked after leaving). A box unticked during the mode is respected until the next trajectory change.
- **Fit** frames the trajectory while the mode is on.
- **Leaving.** **Leave**, `Esc` (first closes the open breakdown), or a click on the background. Focus returns to the Trajectory button (or the search box) when it was in the bar.
- **Screen readers.** A polite live region announces the start table and counts on each change, the brought-back count, and "Trajectory closed".
- Nothing about the trajectory is saved (D-013).

## Universe view — flow view (M5.3, D-036)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-04-m5.3-flow-view.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** See the current trajectory in data-flow order: upstream on the left, the start, downstream on the right, every arrow pointing right, without changing the universe.
- **Opening.** **Flow** ("Flux") in the trajectory bar, before **Leave**; title "Show this trajectory as a left-to-right flow". Same start, direction and depth as the trajectory. Disabled when the trajectory has no table for its direction. The flow covers the whole Universe tab (filters panel included); the universe underneath cannot be reached and does not change.
- **Header**, in order: **← Universe** ("Back to the universe (Esc)"); "Flow of" + the start as a button (kind dot + name, 2 px dark frame; title "Centre the view on the start table" / "Centrer la vue sur la table de départ"; M5.11): it centres the start in the area at the current scale, or 0.75 if smaller, and does nothing while laying out; **Direction** and **Depth** (they change the flow only, never the universe trajectory); counts ("24 tables · 22 upstream · 1 downstream", plural agreement, or the empty text "No downstream table"); **N domains** (a list: start / upstream / downstream marks, then each domain present with its colour and table count); the cap message when the flow is limited; **Fit** ("Show the whole flow"); **Export image** PNG / SVG.
- **Tables.** A box per table: the name on the kind colour (as in the universe and the detail), a domain colour band on the left edge, then the depth (`−2` upstream, `+1` downstream) and the layer. The start has a thick dark frame and reads "start" instead of a depth; around it, a dark ring (3 px, 6 px outside the frame, white inside) and a 10 px soft grey glow (the text colour at 14 %) keep the same screen size at every zoom, so the start is found at a glance even at Fit on 150 tables (M5.11, design note `docs/architecture/2026-11-05-m5.11-flow-start.md`). They lie under every table and arrow, and are exported as shown. The Domains list marks "start" with a double dark frame, as the universe marks it with a double ring. Columns follow the lineage, with the configured layers as a floor, so layers form bands where the lineage follows them.
- **Arrows.** Upstream dashed violet (`#6d28d9`), downstream solid burnt orange (`#c2410c`), as in the trajectory mode.
- **Initial view.** The whole flow if it is readable (scale 0.75 or more); otherwise scale 0.75 centred on the start. **Fit** always shows the whole flow. Wheel / pinch zoom, drag to pan; tables cannot be dragged.
- **Recentre.** A click (or Enter / Space on a focused table) redraws the flow from that table, with the flow's direction and depth; the header follows. The universe trajectory does not move: **← Universe** then **Flow** returns to its start.
- **Tooltip.** Name; domain · layer · type · N columns; "Upstream, level n" / "Downstream, level n" / "Start of the trajectory"; the description; "Click: show its flow" (not on the start). Not shown on touch.
- **Limit.** At most 150 tables. Above that the depth is reduced to the largest level that fits ("Depth reduced to 2 levels: the chosen depth has 412 tables, a flow draws at most 150"), or, when level 1 alone is too large, only the first 149 direct tables are drawn ("Only 149 of the 420 tables at level 1 are drawn (at most 150 per flow)").
- **States.** "Laying out…" while Graphviz runs (export disabled); "Render error: …" with the header still usable; a direction with no table draws the start alone.
- **Keyboard.** On open, focus is on **← Universe**. Tab order: header controls (**← Universe**, the start button, Direction, Depth, Domains, Fit, PNG, SVG), then the tables left to right. Enter or Space on the start button centres the start; no single-key shortcut. `Esc` closes the domain list first, then the flow; it never also leaves the trajectory. On close, focus returns to **Flow**.
- **Screen readers.** A polite live region announces "Flow of <table>: <counts>." on open and recentre (with the cap message), and "Flow closed". Each table is a button named by its name and level.
- **Small screens.** No horizontal scroll at 360 px; the header wraps; 44 px controls on coarse pointers.
- Nothing about the flow is saved (D-013).

## Universe view — constellations by layer (M5.13, D-048)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-05-m5.13-group-by-layers.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** Read the estate by tier ("what is in Gold, for which domains?") as well as by domain.
- **Switch.** First in the universe panel: **Constellations** ("Constellations"), two native radio buttons **Domains** / **Layers** ("Domaines" / "Couches"), each with the number of constellations it gives over the whole project. On a fine pointer the radio rows have the height and left edge of the checkbox rows (the radio takes the checkbox's default margin); on a coarse pointer the rows are 44 px. One Tab stop, arrow keys switch. Hidden, and the page stays in Domains, when the Layers list has fewer than two rows.
- **Default and memory.** The page opens in the viewer's last choice when one is stored, else in `universe.constellations` of `dbt-erd.yml` (default `domains`). The choice is stored in localStorage `dbtscope-constellations:<title>` only when it differs from the config default (choosing the default again removes it); it is never exported; without storage the switch works for the session.
- **Layer mode.** One constellation per layer, left to right in the flow order of the Layers list. Inside each, tables sit in domain bands in the order of the Domains panel, packed as in domain mode. The disc and its name are neutral (muted outline, text-colour name); the name, count line and composition bar are raised 8 px (`NAME_LIFT`), so the bar ends 4 px above the rim arcs; each band is tinted at 6 % in its domain colour (opaque, clipped to the disc) and marked by a 4 px arc of the disc's upper rim in its domain colour darkened to 3:1 (D-042), at every zoom; faint separators at scale 0.6 or more, as in domain mode. At scale 0.6 or more each band shows its domain name (11 px, its colour darkened to 4.5:1 on its tint), when it fits and does not collide. The Domains panel's dots are the legend. Meta line: "N tables · N links · N layers" ("couches").
- **Tooltip.** Over a layer constellation: its label, its table count, "Domains: Sales · Finance · …" (first 12, then "+ N"), over a band "Domain: Sales · 42 tables", and "Double-click: zoom on this constellation" ("Domaines : …", "Domaine : …", "Double-clic : cadrer cette constellation"). The domain-mode tooltip is unchanged.
- **Switching** is instant and framed by Fit (the trajectory when it is on); the hover and the search mark are cleared, as on any change of Links or Tables. Filters, search, the trajectory, the flow view, double-click and image export behave the same in both modes.
- **Saved arrangements.** Each grouping keeps its own (scopes `layers:names`, `layers:keys`, `layers:all` in the `universe` section, D-013 format unchanged), and, while the descriptive tables are folded, `layers:folded:names|keys|all` (M8.2, D-064); a drag in one never moves the other; **Reset layout** clears all.
- **Names without a label.** A domain, layer or kind whose rule sets `label: null` is shown by its key everywhere (filter rows, tooltips, canvas, flow view), through the one rule `common.js labelOf`. Before M5.13 such a layer showed nothing in the panel and "null" in the tooltip and the flow.
- **Hint.** "… drag a table, or a constellation by its name, to arrange them · … double-click a constellation to zoom in, a table to open its detail" ("… ou une constellation par son nom … double-clic sur une constellation pour la cadrer …"), in both modes.

## Top bar — layout buttons

- **Reset layout**, **Export layout**, **Import layout** (fr since M2: "Réinitialiser la disposition", "Exporter la disposition", "Importer une disposition") act on the manual arrangement of the page (D-013). They never export an image.

## Image export (M2, D-021)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-03-m2-export-offline.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** Put the current view into a slide, a document or a ticket, exactly as it is on screen.
- **Control.** At the right end of each tab's toolbar, after **Fit**: the muted label **Export image** ("Exporter l'image") and one button per format, **PNG** in the universe, **PNG** and **SVG** in the detail. A native button group named "Export image"; tooltips "Save the view as shown as a PNG image (high resolution)", "Save the visible diagram as a PNG image (high resolution)", "Save the visible diagram as an SVG image (vector, opens in any browser)".
- **What is exported.** The drawing as it is on screen: zoom, pan, filters, detail level, search mark, highlight, trajectory rings and dimming. Not the panels, toolbars, trajectory bar, tooltips or hover. No caption or legend is added. Background filled with the page background.
- **Formats.** Universe PNG and detail PNG at twice the screen resolution (lowered on very large screens to stay within browser limits); detail SVG cut to the visible area, styles inlined, opens alone in a browser.
- **File name.** `<page title>-universe.png`, `<page title>-detail.png`, `<page title>-detail.svg`, and for the flow view `<page title>-flow.png` / `-flow.svg` (M5.3); the title keeps ASCII letters, digits, `-` and `_` (accents dropped), `dbtscope` if nothing is left.
- **States.** Disabled while nothing is drawn (no table for the filters; detail laying out or in error). While an export runs, the clicked button ignores further clicks (`aria-disabled`, progress cursor) and keeps focus. Success: a polite live region in the top bar says "Image created: <file>" ("Image créée : <file>", no-break space). Failure: an alert "The image could not be created." ("L'image n'a pas pu être créée."), nothing is downloaded.
- **Keyboard.** In tab order after Fit; Enter or Space exports; no shortcut. Focus stays on the button.
- **Small screens.** The group wraps as one piece with the toolbar; no horizontal scroll at 360 px. 44 px targets on coarse pointers.

## Offline page (M2)

- Built with `--offline` or `offline: true`. The page looks and behaves exactly like the default one and shows nothing about it.

## Universe view — entities (M8.2, D-063)

Design: `docs/architecture/2026-11-12-m8.2-entities.md` § UI design.

- **Purpose.** On a large Data Vault, read the estate as its entities (hubs, links, dimensions, facts) and keep satellites, ref sats and PITs one step away.
- **Entity and mark.** A descriptive table with a parent (its core or connector table in the same domain and layer, computed by dbtscope from relations then names) is drawn inside that table instead of as its own star. The parent is an **entity**; when it holds at least one table its star carries a mark: from scale 0.6 a **badge**, a dark disc (`#1f2933`, white 1.5 px outline, 13.6:1 on the page) on the upper right of the star, 7 px with the count in white (99+ above 99); from afar (below 0.6) a thin dark ring (`#1f2933`, 1.25 px wide, 1.5 px off the star, tighter than every trajectory ring) so the kind colour of the star still shows. A card keeps a 4 px dot from afar. The star's size is unchanged. In both groupings, at every zoom and in image export; trajectory rings, the start's double ring and the search ring stay visible around it. On cards the badge sits on the top-right corner.
- **Tooltip.** An entity lists its folded tables, name and kind, 12 at most then "+ N" ("3 folded tables" / "3 tables repliées"). A table that has a parent but is drawn shows "Folds into: <parent>".
- **Cards.** At keys and all fields, folded tables are rows under the columns (name, kind label in grey at the right), 12 at most then "+ N".
- **Fold control.** Panel, after Constellations, heading "Entities" ("Entités"): one checkbox "Fold Satellite · PIT" ("Replier …", the descriptive kinds with a parent, from the roles) with the number of tables with a parent. Checked = folded; space toggles; 44 px on coarse pointers. Hidden when no table has a parent (the page is then as before). Default `universe.fold` (default true); the viewer's choice is kept in localStorage `dbtscope-fold:<title>` when it differs from the default, never exported. Changing it relayouts and fits, like the Constellations switch, and is announced to screen readers.
- **Counts never drop because of a fold.** The meta line counts every table that passes the filters, folded included, and adds "N entities" ("N entités") while folding is on: "1,419 tables · 428 entities · 2,621 links · 12 domains". Domain and layer counts, composition bars, band counts and the Types counts are the same folded or shown. Routes between constellations carry the links of folded tables; no link of a folded table is drawn inside a constellation.
- **Reach.** Searching a folded table opens its entity (one at a time), goes to the table, marks it and announces it ("car_unlinked_sat is folded into car_hub: shown"); a trajectory shows its tables, the start included, unfolded, and gives back the folded drawing when left (the camera stays anchored on the clicked table when it unfolds or folds members: no refit, the table stays under the pointer; Fit still frames the trajectory); double-click on an entity opens its detail as before.
- **Filters.** Unticking a kind or layer of folded tables lowers the entity's count and the meta line's tables as any filter does. Unticking the entity's own kind draws its folded tables as stars.
- **Saved arrangements (D-064).** The folded drawing has its own arrangement (scopes `folded:*` and `layers:folded:*`, D-013 format unchanged); an offset saved while shown applies again when shown; older files are arrangements of the shown drawing; **Reset layout** clears all.
