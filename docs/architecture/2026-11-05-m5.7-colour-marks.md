# M5.7 — Colour marks reach 3:1, a distinct seed colour (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE (the roadmap "Later" item of the M5.4 reviews, asked by the user on 2026-11-05; D-042 refines D-037)

## Context

Asked by the user on 2026-11-05: fix the two colour items of "Later" in `docs/roadmap.md` ("Colours, from the M5.4 reviews").

1. **Non-text contrast (WCAG 2.x, 1.4.11, 3:1).** D-037 made every *text* on or in a colour reach 4.5:1, but the *marks* drawn in a kind or domain colour (stars, card borders, dots, header bands, domain bands) are drawn in the raw colour. A near-white colour (`#ffffff`, `#fefefe`, `#f8fafc`) or a light one (`#ffff00`) makes the mark vanish on the page background `#f5f6f8` or on the white surfaces.
2. **Seed colour.** The built-in seed kind `#8b5cf6` (`domain/universe.py TYPE_KINDS`) is a violet next to the dimension / bridge / same-as-link violet `#8e5bb5` (acme example, both presets): ΔE2000 ≈ 9.5, and they take opposite inks (seed black, `#8e5bb5` white, D-037).

Current state: `common.js CONTRAST` (`ratio`, `textOn`, `ink`, `AA`) holds the WCAG math and the darkening toward black (`darken`, steps of 1/32). Every mark reads the payload colour directly (`KIND[k].color`, `DOM[d].color`, `k.color` in the detail).

A colour that reaches 3:1 on `#f5f6f8` is darker than it, so it reaches more than 3:1 on white (`1.05 / (L + 0.05) > 0.971 / (L + 0.05)`): one reference background, `THEME.bg`, covers the canvas, the cards, the panels, the trajectory bar and the Graphviz nodes.

## Chosen design

### 1. `CONTRAST.edge(color, bg)` and two small helpers (`common.js`)

- `CONTRAST.MARK = 3`, the non-text threshold, next to `AA`.
- `CONTRAST.edge(color, bg)`: `color` itself when `ratio(color, bg) ≥ MARK`, else `color` mixed toward black by the smallest step `j / 32` that reaches `MARK`, as `#rrggbb` (`#000000` if none; `color` unchanged if unparsable). It is the existing `darken`, given its threshold as a parameter: `darken(color, bg, min)`; `ink` = `darken(…, AA)`, `edge` = `darken(…, MARK)`, both memoised (`'edge|' + color + '|' + bg`). No second darkening (invariant 2). The hue stays, so a darkened edge still reads as its colour.
- `paintDot(el, color)`: the one colour dot of the page. Sets `el.style.background = color`, and `el.style.boxShadow = 'inset 0 0 0 1px ' + edge` when `edge !== color`, else clears it (`#u-tj-dot` and `#f-dot` are reused from table to table). Returns `el`. Used by `filterRow` and by every dot of the universe and the flow.
- `dotEdge(color)`: the attributes outlining a Graphviz table cell filled with `color`: `''` when `edge === color`, else ` border="1" color="<edge>"` (escaped). Used by the header cells of the detail and the flow. The developer checks with the vendored Viz.js (Graphviz 2.40) whether `sides` is honoured; all four sides is the expected form.
- Rule for every site: **a filled area keeps its colour and gets an outline in `edge`; a thin mark (a line, a border, a bar of 6 px or less) is drawn in `edge` itself.** Colours that pass give `edge === color`: no outline is added and every line keeps its colour, so their look is unchanged pixel for pixel.

Not changed: the domain names (already `CONTRAST.ink`, 4.5:1); the trajectory colours and neutral greys (not config colours); the flow node frame `#c3c9d2` (a neutral, pre-existing; the node is marked by its header, now outlined when needed). The domain circle and halo keep their fixed alphas: the 3:1 holds for the colour they are drawn from, so a near-white domain is drawn as visibly as a passing one; the domain itself is identified by its name. Raising the alphas would change every domain's look.

**Exports match the screen.** The universe PNG is painted by the same `draw`; the detail and flow SVG / PNG carry the DOT attributes. The dots are page controls, never exported.

### 3. The built-in seed colour becomes `#c62a36` (D-042)

- `domain/universe.py TYPE_KINDS["seed"].color`: `#8b5cf6` → `#c62a36` (a crimson red, hue ≈ 355°).
- White text on it 5.55:1 (`textOn` gives white, like `#8e5bb5`); 5.13:1 on the page background, so its edge and its domain ink are the colour itself.
- Distinct from every shipped colour (estimated CIEDE2000, the test recomputes): nearest `#d9622b` (≈ 17), `#c2477a` (≈ 17); far from every blue, green, teal, gold and violet. From the trajectory downstream orange `#c2410c` (a ring and a line, not a fill) ≈ 11.
- Why red: the shipped colours fill the blues, greens, teals, golds / oranges / browns, pinks, violets and greys. The free hues are red, yellow-lime, indigo, sky and fuchsia; only red reaches white text and stays ≥ 12 ΔE2000 from every shipped colour.
- **Payload change, on purpose:** the universe `kinds` entry of a project with seeds. No M3 reference payload holds a seed (the shop fixture has none): `payloads/*.json` stay byte-identical; `expected.json` is regenerated once for the asset change. The acme pages change (seeds `calendar`, `country_codes`, `currency_rates`…).
- README does not name the seed colour; `docs/ui-spec.md` § General ("the built-in source, seed and `other` colours… take black") becomes "source and `other`… take black; seed is white".

No data format, layout file or localStorage change (invariant 9). No config key. No error path: an unparsable colour cannot reach the page (D-041); `edge` returns it unchanged anyway.

### Responsibility map (`docs/architecture.md`)

Extend the `CONTRAST` row with `edge`, `MARK` ("a colour made to mark against the page, 3:1"); add `paintDot` ("a colour dot of the page, outlined when its colour is too light") and `dotEdge` ("the outline of a Graphviz cell filled with a config colour").

## Rejected options

- **An outline everywhere, whatever the colour**: changes the look of every passing colour.
- **Darkening every mark** (fills too): a near-white kind's star and header would no longer match its legend, and the name ink on the header would change.
- **Reaching 3:1 against white for the panels and against `--bg` for the canvas separately**: `--bg` is the stricter of the two for every colour that can pass; one reference is enough.
- **Opaque domain circles (3:1 on the composited stroke)**: changes every domain's look; the domain name already carries it.
- **A pixel test of the outlines**: 1 px canvas lines are anti-aliased over two pixels, so a darkest-pixel probe under-reads; the tests record the colours drawn instead.
- **Seed colours considered**: indigo `#4f46e5` (≈ 10 from `#7b52c4`, and the trajectory violet), sky `#0284c7` (≈ 8 from `#2f6fb5`), fuchsia (between `#a33fb5` and `#c2549d`), a dark yellow-lime (black text, next to `#5a8f1e` and the golds), changing `#8e5bb5` instead (a preset and example colour users copied, and still near the seed violet).
- **Darkening the built-in source and no-domain greys** to reach 3:1: a second payload change for what the edge already covers.
