# M9.2 — Drill-down: map landing, enter a domain, breadcrumb (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE. Everything here is the brief's decided scope (`docs/briefs/2026-11-17-visualization-optimization.md` § 3 rows 2 and 4, § 6); the breadcrumb was approved by the user on 2026-11-17 (brief § 3 closing paragraph, D-086). The supersessions below (part of D-081 on large projects, the M5.5 "no layer names on the canvas" rule, clauses of D-066 and D-086) are the ones § 6 asks for. One payload key and one config key are added (extension, backwards compatible); no layout format, layout scope or localStorage key changes; nothing about navigation is saved.

## Context

Request: give the universe an explicit drill-down: a large project opens on a map of its domains (or layers); clicking one enters it (a full-screen relayout of that constellation, layer headers, no table-to-table link until a table is hovered or selected, the other constellations as labelled ghosts at the edges); a breadcrumb `All domains › Vehicle › car_hub` replaces the count line, `Esc` steps one level back, and the same path carries to the Detail tab; free zoom keeps working and moves the location with hysteresis (roadmap M9.2; review findings 1, 2, 3 (group preselection), 8 (keyboard path)).

Current state (after M9.1):

- `universe.js` has one level: the whole universe, with a continuous free zoom. Double-click on a constellation zooms onto it (`zoomTo`), double-click on a table hands off to the Detail tab (`boot.js openInDetail`). The Domains / Layers / Source systems rows (`common.js filterRow`) are filter checkboxes only; hover and focus spotlight their routes (D-071).
- The toolbar holds `#u-meta`, the counts line (wraps, height kept by `setMeta`, D-082). `#u-q` + `mark(n)` find, ring and frame a table; `locate(id)` is the return from the Detail tab (D-086).
- `layoutConstellation(key, members)` packs a constellation's bands (`bandsOf`, `columns`, `packRow`) into the smallest enclosing circle (D-040); `layout()` runs every constellation (cached per constellation) and d3-force (seed 42).
- Summary cards are off by default (D-081); `summaryWeight(k)` is their one switch point and returns 0 while off, so a card-off page draws stars at every zoom. On the large project (1,419 tables, 12 domains) the Fit scale (about 0.18 at 1280 × 800) is below the cards band (D-065).
- `common.js crumbs(nav, items)` renders a trail (one `← Universe` item in the detail, `#d-crumbs`); `detail.focus(id, trail)`, `detail.focused()`, `boot.js backToUniverse(handOff)`.
- Esc: the lineage (trajectory) and the lineage graph (flow) each handle it; popovers close first (`dismissable` calls `preventDefault`, which the universe handler respects).
- Config `universe.*` keys are copied verbatim into `defaults.*` of the payload (`domain/universe.universe_data`).

## Chosen design

Layers touched: `domain/config.py`, `domain/payload.py`, `domain/universe.py` (one key), `render/assets/` (`page.html`, `page.css`, `common.js`, `universe.js`, `detail.js`, `boot.js`). `flow.js`, `storage/`, `cli.py`, `render/page.py`: unchanged.

### 1. The navigation model (D-088)

Three levels in the universe, one more beyond it in the Detail tab:

| Level | Name | What is drawn | Entered by | Left by (one level back) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **map** | every constellation, the layout of today (D-040, D-048, D-069, D-070) | page load; `Esc` or the root segment from level 1 | — |
| 1 | **entered constellation** (a domain in domain mode, a layer in layer mode) | that constellation's tables spread over the stage, band headers, ghosts | a click or double-click on its disc, its summary card or a card row, its panel row's name, a ghost, a search, the return from the Detail tab, or zooming into its disc (§ 5) | `Esc`, the root segment, zooming out (§ 5), a filter that empties it, the Organise-by switch |
| 2 | **table** | a selected table: the lineage start, or the table found by the search box / the return from the Detail tab (ring) | a click on a table (lineage, D-018 until M9.3), a search, the return from the Detail tab | `Esc`, the constellation segment, leaving the lineage, a background click (lineage, as today) |
| (3) | **detail** | the Detail tab after a hand-off | double-click on a table (D-086) | `Esc` or any trail segment in the detail |

State in `universe.js`: `nav = { inside: <constellation key> | null, table: <index> | null }`. `nav.table` replaces the "found" notion as the table level: `select(i)` sets it (search, `locate`, lineage enter), `unselect()` clears it (and leaves the lineage when it is its start). `found` (the ring) stays the drawing of a selected table that is not a lineage start. A table level without an entered constellation exists only at the map level of a page where stars are drawn (a small project's star click): the path is then `All domains › fct_orders`.

The levels are pure page state: never written to the layout, to localStorage, to the URL or to an export (D-013). Every page load opens at level 0.

**M9.3 room.** The table level is "a selected table", whatever selects it. M9.3's table panel becomes the table level's UI: single click calls `select(i)` and opens the panel instead of the lineage; the panel's Esc is the table level's step back (one Esc before "leave the domain"), and the lineage becomes a sub-state started from the panel. No change to `nav`, `path()` or the Esc chain is needed for that.

### 2. Level 0 — map landing on large projects (D-087)

- **Rule.** A page whose universe holds more tables than `universe.map_above` (`DATA.nodes.length > DATA.defaults.mapAbove`: the universe scope, before filters and fold) is a **map page** (`MAP`, a constant of `startUniverse`). Default threshold 300: the large project (1,419) is a map page, acme_retail and `shop` are not, so small projects keep today's opening exactly.
- **Drawing.** On a map page, at level 0, the stars give way to **named discs** from afar, by the cards' own rule (D-065): `summaryWeight(k)` returns the weight when `state.cards || MAP` (still 0 inside an entered constellation and on a non-map page with cards off). With cards on, the weight draws summary cards as today; with cards off it draws the discs (halo, rim, name, count, composition bar) at full alpha and fades only stars, table links and table names by `1 − weight`; routes as with cards off (individual, never bundled). One switch point stays `summaryWeight`; the disc-or-card choice reads `state.cards` in `paint` and `constellationAt` (the disc, not the card rectangle, is the hit target when cards are off).
- **Landing.** At level 0 on a map page, `fit()` (opening, Fit button, the root segment) frames at most at `kCards()`, so the page always opens with weight 1 (no stars, no table names) whatever the screen size; a viewer zooming in still sees stars fade in at the stars scale (free zoom, § 5).
- **Config.** `universe.map_above`: a whole number ≥ 0 (pydantic `StrictInt`, `ge=0`; a bool, a string or a negative number is one config line, e.g. `config <path>: universe.map_above: invalid value -1 (a number of tables, 0 or more); fix the file`), default 300, allowed in presets (`PresetUniverse` inherits it, D-030). `0` opens every project on the map; a number above the project's size keeps today's opening. Payload `UniverseDefaults.map_above`, serialised as `mapAbove` (as `hiddenKinds`), the new last key of the universe defaults. `universe_data` copies it, as it copies `cards` and `routes`.
- **Why the threshold is in the payload, not a computed boolean:** every other `defaults.*` value is the config value verbatim; the comparison needs only `DATA.nodes.length`, so it lives once, in the page, next to the drawing it switches.

### 3. Level 1 — the entered constellation (D-088, D-090)

**Layout: the stage-shaped spread.** `layoutConstellation(key, members, aspect)` gains an optional `aspect` (stage width / height, minus the ghost margin and the header strip). Without it: today's smallest enclosing circle, unchanged. With it: the same bands (`bandsOf`), the same band order and in-band order, the same `columns` / `packRow` scan of the height caps, but the objective is the largest fit scale of the row's bounding box in a box of that aspect instead of the smallest circle (`packRow` returns the bounding box of its corners as well). One packing, two objectives; reserved boxes still never overlap; deterministic. Result cached per `[by, key, detail, links, held, aspect rounded to 0.05]`.

- **World position.** The spread is centred on the constellation's map centre, so entering is a continuous move: the stars of the disc **tween** to their spread positions (300 ms, `d3.timer`, no tween under `prefers-reduced-motion`) while the camera frames the spread (`frame`, animated). Only the entered constellation's visible tables are drawn and picked (`tree` rebuilt over them).
- **What is drawn.** The viewer's choices hold (Show level, fold, Links, filters): entering changes no setting, so the panel stays true. The defaults (names, folded) give the brief's "stars and names" picture. The from-afar names rule (`fitNames`, below 0.6) is off inside: names compete by the existing order (`orderLabels`, `drawNames`) at every scale.
- **No table-to-table link** until a table is hovered or selected: inside, `paint` draws the links of the hovered table and of `nav.table` only (`C.hl`), in-constellation links star to star, links to another constellation as one line per ghost from the star to that ghost (width by count). The lineage draws its own edges as today (§ 7).
- **Band headers** (supersedes the M5.5 "No layer names on the canvas" rule for the entered level only, D-090): each band's name and count ("Raw vault 388") drawn in screen space above its column, centred on the band, 12 px 600, text colour in domain mode (layer names), the domain colour inked to 4.5:1 (`CONTRAST.ink`) in layer mode; a header **sticks** to the top of the stage while its band is in view and the header row has scrolled off; a faint full-height separator between bands (the colour of `drawBandMarkers`). The map keeps no layer names on its discs.
- **Ghosts.** Every other drawn constellation of the map is a **ghost**: a small labelled disc (radius 12 to 24 screen px by √tables, its colour at reduced alpha, neutral for source systems and layers; its name and count in 11 px) placed in screen space on a ring inset `NAV.GHOST_MARGIN` (64 px) from the stage edges, in the direction of its map centre seen from the entered constellation's map centre (so the viewer's own map arrangement orients them), slid along the ring to keep 8 px apart (sorted by angle, deterministic). Its dependency to the entered constellation, when the routes switch is on (D-083), is one line from the ghost's rim toward the spread's bounding box (clipped to the stage), width `lineWidth(c)`, arrowhead by direction (`headAt`), the counts of `routePairs` (every pair with the entered constellation, no `strongOf` density rule: there are at most a dozen). Hover: the constellation tooltip plus `routeWords`. Click or double-click: enter that constellation. An exported image shows the ghosts and their lines, since the export is the view as shown (D-021); tooltips and spotlight stay out.
- **Fit inside** frames the spread in the stage minus the ghost margin and the header strip (`NAV.HEADER`, 28 px; below the lineage bar when it shows); with a lineage, the in-constellation members (D-082's rule applied to the spread).
- **Dragging** is off inside (no `drag` subject while entered): the spread is computed, never saved (§ 8).

**Entering** (`enter(key, how)`): leaves an active lineage unless its start is in `key`; sets `nav.inside`; computes the spread (no `layout()`, no force run: the map is unchanged); tweens; renders the crumbs; announces in `#u-live` ("Vehicle: 299 tables in 4 layers" / layer mode "Raw vault: 828 tables in 5 domains"); `perf.enter` = compute time to the first drawn frame. A card row (`zoomOnRow`) enters and then frames that band in the spread. Entering another constellation from inside (ghost, panel row, search) switches directly.

**Leaving** (`leave(how)`): clears `nav.inside` and `nav.table`, restores the map positions (the map `P` is kept, not recomputed), tweens back, and frames: the whole map (Esc, root segment, filter, grouping change) or the left disc at `NAV.BACK` (zoom-out, § 5); announces "All domains" / "All layers".

### 4. Breadcrumb (D-089)

- **Universe toolbar.** `#u-meta` leaves the toolbar; in its place `<nav id="u-crumbs" class="crumbs" aria-label="Location">`, rendered by `common.js crumbs` from `universe.js path()`:
  - level 0: `All domains` (layer mode `All layers`);
  - level 1: `All domains › Vehicle`;
  - level 2: `All domains › Vehicle › car_hub` (or `All domains › fct_orders` at the map level of a star page).
  Every segment is a button. Ancestors go to their level (`go(step)`); the current (last) one carries `aria-current="location"` and frames its place (the map's Fit, the spread's Fit, the table). The separator `›` is `aria-hidden`. Each segment's `title` holds its counts: the root the whole counts line (what `#u-meta`'s `title` holds today), a constellation "299 tables (152 entities drawn) · 4 layers · 6 dependencies", a table its tooltip's meta line.
  - **One line, never wrapping**: segments shrink with an ellipsis (middle segments first; root and current keep at least 4 em), so entering or leaving never changes the toolbar's height and the stage is never resized by it (D-082's guarantee; a resize would refit).
  - If the keyboard focus was inside the crumbs when they re-render, it moves to the new current segment.
- **The counts line** moves to the top of the panel's **Navigate** section (`#u-meta`, same id, same `setMeta` and wrapping, D-082), and stays as the root segment's `title`.
- **`common.js crumbs` extended**, not duplicated: an item `{label, title, go, back, current}`; `current` with `go` is a button with `aria-current="location"`; `current` without `go` stays the plain span of M9.1.
- **Detail tab** (refines D-086). A hand-off (`boot.js openInDetail`) passes the universe path as a function of the detail's focus: `views.detail.focus(id, trailOf)` where `trailOf(focusId)` returns the crumbs items; `detail.focus` accepts an array (M9.1) or a function, re-evaluated on every focus change, so the last segment follows the detail's focus when it is a universe table (as `backToUniverse` already does). Items: `← All domains › Vehicle › car_hub`, every segment a button back to the Universe tab at that level (the root leaves to the map; the constellation segment enters it with no table; the table segment is `backToUniverse`, i.e. `locate` on the detail's current focus, M9.1 behaviour); none is `aria-current` (the detail's own place is its focus). The leading `←` (aria-hidden) says the trail leaves the tab.
- **Detail follows the universe location** (review finding 3): when the Detail tab is shown by its tab button and the universe location changed since the detail last followed it, `views.detail.follow(group, trailOf)` shows the trail (`← All domains › Vehicle`) and, in domain mode when `DOMAINS` (`detailDomains`) holds the entered key and the detail has no focus, selects that group. The viewer's own group choice in the detail stands until the universe location changes again. Layer mode: trail only (a layer is not a group).
- **Esc** (one level back):
  - Universe (`universe.js back()`, one keydown handler replacing the lineage-only one): ignored when not on the Universe tab, when `defaultPrevented` (popovers, the lineage graph), when the target is `#u-q` or a `select`, or when the lineage counts list is open (it closes it, as today). Else: table level → `unselect()` (leaves the lineage, clears the ring) → entered → `leave('esc')` → map: nothing.
  - Detail (`detail.js`): when its trail shows and the target is not an input or select and nothing prevented it, calls the last segment's `go` (back to the universe at the table).
  - Lineage graph overlay: its own Esc (D-036), unchanged and first.
- **Keyboard path** (review 8): map → constellation by its panel row's name button (§ 6) or the search box; constellation → table by the search box; back by `Esc` or the crumb buttons; detail → universe by `Esc` or the detail trail. Tab order of the universe toolbar: ☰ → crumbs → search → Lineage → ? → Fit → export.

### 5. Free zoom and hysteresis (D-088)

The location follows the viewer's zoom, evaluated **once per gesture**, on the d3 zoom `end` event that carries a `sourceEvent` (wheel idle, pinch release, drag end); the page's own transitions (`fit`, `frame`, `zoomTo`, `camera()`) never move the location. While a lineage is active, the zoom never enters or leaves (the lineage spans constellations; explicit entering still works).

- **Enter by zoom** (map level): the drawn constellation whose disc holds the stage centre and whose screen diameter is at least `NAV.ENTER` = 0.9 × min(stage width, height) is entered, anchored: the camera keeps its scale when that is at least the spread's Fit scale (else goes to it) and keeps the table nearest the stage centre at its screen point (`relayoutAnchored`'s rule).
- **Leave by zoom** (entered): a scale below `NAV.LEAVE` = 0.6 × the spread's Fit scale leaves to the map, which frames the left disc at a diameter of `NAV.BACK` = 0.6 × min(stage width, height).
- **No flapping:** after an enter the scale is ≥ the spread's Fit, about 3.7 wheel notches (1.15 each) above the leave threshold; after a leave the disc is at 0.6 of the stage, about 3 notches below the enter threshold. A pan never changes the level. `NAV` is one constant object; the browser test checks both margins.
- Small (non-map) pages: same rules; the map at Fit draws stars as today, and zooming into a disc until it fills the stage enters it.

### 6. Panel rows that go (D-089)

The rows of the list whose items are the current constellations (Domains and Source systems in domain mode, Layers in layer mode) get a **go** action: `filterRow({…, go})` (extended, one implementation) builds the row as the checkbox (the filter, its accessible name "Show Vehicle" / "Afficher Vehicle"), the colour dot, the name as a `<button>` that enters the constellation, and the count. Rows of the other lists keep the plain label (clicking the name toggles the box). The entered constellation's row carries `aria-current="location"`. Hover / focus spotlight (D-071) unchanged. The ui-designer settles the row's look (the name must read as a button without breaking the list's alignment; 44 px targets on coarse pointers).

### Formats and compatibility

- **Config** `universe.map_above` (new optional key; absent → 300): existing `dbt-erd.yml` files and presets stay valid; an older dbtscope ignores the key (lenient file, D-030's file rule) but refuses it in a preset (presets are closed): documented in the user guide.
- **Payload**: `defaults.mapAbove` is the new last key of the universe defaults; every page's payload changes by that key only. The M3 reference pages' `expected.json` and `payloads/*.json` are regenerated once, in the commit that adds the key (the brief's "byte-identical unless the phase says otherwise": this phase says otherwise, for this key).
- **Layout** `dbt-erd-layout` v1, its scopes and drawing keys, `dbt-erd-layout:<title>`, every `dbtscope-*:<title>` key: unchanged; no migration. Nothing navigational is stored or exported (D-013).
- **Behaviour changes for existing users**: a double-click on a constellation now enters it (was: zoom onto it); a click on a disc or on a card outside its rows enters it (was: nothing); the domain-panel name enters instead of toggling the filter (the checkbox still filters); a search enters the table's constellation; the counts line moved to the panel; on pages above the threshold the stars give way to named discs from afar with cards off.

### Error handling

Config: one line per invalid `map_above` (above), exit 1, through `errors.validation_message`. Page: no new failure path; entering a key that is no longer drawn (filtered out between a click and its handling, a stale detail trail) goes to the map; `go` of a table that is no longer in the universe scope goes to its constellation if any, else to the map; an empty constellation after a filter leaves with a live message.

## Rejected options

- **Entering by framing only (no relayout).** Decided against by the user (brief § 3 row 2).
- **Zoom-entering that only renames the breadcrumb** (two kinds of "inside Vehicle", one framed, one relaid). Two states behind one breadcrumb; the brief makes zooming in "equivalent to entering".
- **Entering in the middle of a wheel gesture.** The layout would change under the pointer while the wheel still scrolls; the gesture's end is the one moment the viewer is not steering.
- **A scale threshold only (no centre test) for zoom-entering.** A viewer zooming into the gap between two domains would be thrown into one of them.
- **No stars at all at the map level of a map page.** Removes the expert path of free zoom over several domains (brief § 4); the cross-fade rule keeps it and costs no new switch.
- **Cards on by default for large projects.** The user wants them off (D-081); the named discs give the map without them.
- **A boolean `defaults.map` computed in Python.** Every other default is the config value verbatim; the comparison needs only the node count the page already has.
- **`universe.map: auto | on | off`** or a `null` threshold for "never". Two concepts in one key; a number above the project's size already means never.
- **Forcing names and fold when entering.** The panel would lie about what is drawn; the defaults already give that picture.
- **Saving the spread's arrangement** (new layout scopes, as D-064). Up to 12 more scopes (grouping × fold × level) for a computed view that is re-entered in under a second; the map arrangement stays the saved one.
- **Ghosts in world space.** They would leave the screen when zooming inside; screen-fixed ghosts are always at the edges, as the brief asks.
- **One source-systems group ghost.** Hides which system feeds the entered domain; seven small ghosts fit the left edge at 800 px.
- **A lineage that leaves the entered constellation to show every member as a star.** Throws the viewer out of the place they chose; the ghosts carry the counts and the Lineage graph shows the whole chain.
- **The location in the URL hash** (shareable deep links). A new public format to keep stable (invariant 9) and a stored navigation state; out of scope.
- **The counts line as a tooltip only.** Invisible to touch and keyboard users; the panel keeps it readable, the root segment's title keeps it at hand.
- **A wrapping breadcrumb.** A height change resizes the stage, which refits (D-082).
- **A separate "Go" icon per panel row.** A second target per row; the name itself is the natural "go to" (review finding 1).
- **Detail trail `All domains › Vehicle › car_hub › Detail`.** The tab already says Detail; the trail is the way back.
- **Esc in the detail going back to the map.** One level back is the table the viewer came from (D-086's last `go`).

## UI design (ui-designer, 2026-11-17)

Task and decision. A viewer opening a 1,500-table page cold must (1) see which domains exist and how big and linked they are, (2) choose one and go in with one click, (3) at all times know where they are and get back in one step, (4) read one domain's tables at a readable size without 11 other domains competing. Everything below is the architect's decided scope (brief § 3, § 6; breadcrumb approved, D-086, D-089). NEEDS USER APPROVAL: none.

Deviations from the note, to confirm in review (none changes behaviour):
1. **Ghost margin is two numbers**, `NAV.GHOST_X` = 88 px (left and right) and `NAV.GHOST_Y` = 72 px (top and bottom), instead of one 64 px: a ghost's name has to fit beside its disc without entering the spread (the note names the margin as a knob). Below 600 px stage width both are 28 px and labels are dropped. The spread's Fit and the "stage minus margin" tests read these two numbers.
2. **One extra line of help** inside an entered constellation: `#u-inside-note`, a 12 px muted HTML line in the stage's bottom-left corner ("Hover a table to see its links"), because "no link until hovered" otherwise looks like a broken page. HTML, so never in an export. Hidden at the map level, while a lineage shows, and on coarse pointers (no hover; the touch hint says it).
3. **Canvas constellations are not Tab stops** (12 to 50 discs would add as many stops before the toolbar). Their keyboard equivalents are the panel rows' name buttons and the search box; the text alternative `#u-summary` stays.

## As built (2026-11-17)

Built as designed, with the ui-designer's three deviations (`NAV.GHOST_X` 88 / `NAV.GHOST_Y` 72, `#u-inside-note`, no canvas Tab stops). Differences and choices worth knowing:

- **Camera and stars move in one `d3.timer`** (`tweenTo`), not a d3 transition: the second press of a double-click on the canvas interrupts d3 transitions (d3-zoom's mouse-down), which left the camera half-way. A double-click right after an enter is ignored (`lastEnter`).
- **Esc in the search box does nothing**, as the plan says; after a search the focus stays in `#u-q`, so a keyboard user steps back with the crumb buttons or leaves the box first.
- **`PM` / `P`**: `layout()` keeps the map's positions in `PM`; an entered constellation's `P` is a copy with the spread's positions, so leaving restores the map without recomputing.
- **Large project, 1280 × 800** (headless Chromium, `tests/browser/test_drill_down_perf.py`, 3 runs each, cold run included): `perf.enter` at most 32 ms (layer mode, fold off, Raw vault 828 tables, at names), at most 20 ms in domain mode with fold on or off, at keys at most 24 ms; `perf.leave` at most 9 ms; a filter change inside 24 ms; frames inside Vehicle 3 ms median and 5 ms worst; the tween 19 frames in 300 ms. All under the 1 s budget by more than an order of magnitude.
- **Names inside Vehicle at the spread's Fit**: 45 of 153 drawn tables (29 %), under the 50 % target, and every drawn table is named only after more than 12 wheel notches (the target was 3): recorded, not a gate. The first knobs the plan names (the header strip and the ghost margin) cost at most 10 % of the height; the real limit is the reserved box, sized for a name at scale 1 while the spread fits at about 0.6. A follow-up could spread to a scale of at least 1 and let the stage scroll, which is a design change (open point).
- **Tests**: existing suites updated where behaviour changed (double-click and click on a domain enter, a search enters, the counts line is in the panel, a large project's weight is 1 at the landing); the lineage tests start the lineage from the page's own script (no blur of the search box, which is a search).
