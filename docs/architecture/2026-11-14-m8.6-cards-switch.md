# M8.6 — Summary cards can be switched off (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE. The user asked for it on 2026-11-14; it supersedes one rejected option of D-063, recorded as D-074. No ui-designer measurement needed beyond the review: the "off" drawing is the stars layer that already exists.

## Context

Request (user, 2026-11-14, after trying M8): "I don't much like the info tiles that appear in the universe view when zooming out; I want these tiles to be easy to remove. Otherwise the universe view is fine." Roadmap M8.6.

Current state: `universe.js summaryAlpha(k)` (D-063) is the card layer's opacity; every card-dependent path already reads it or `cardsOnAt(a)`: `paint` (`LA = 1 - a`, `placed = a > 0 ? cardLayout(k) : null`, disc halos of grouped systems at `1 - a/2`, `discLabel` and `bare`, `labelHits`), `routeLayout` / `routeSpecs` / `outlines` (`grouped = GROUPED.length > 0 && a > 0`, `groupShown(a)`, the obstacles are discs when `!cardsOnAt(a)`), `edgeOf(d, toward, a)` (disc edge at `a = 0`), `routeCounts(a)` (meta line), `fit` (`cardsAt(k)` is empty when `!cardsOnAt(summaryAlpha(k))`), `pickAt` / hover / double-click / drag (`cardsOn()`), the route tooltip and live line (`groupShown(summaryAlpha(t.k))`). At `a = 0` the frame is the stars frame (D-063: "the M8.2 frame, pixel for pixel", since M8.4 with curved routes from disc edges). There is no way to stay at `a = 0` when zoomed out.

## Chosen design

**One switch point.** `state.cards` (bool) and `summaryAlpha = k => (state.cards ? clamp(...) : 0)`. Every dependent above follows with no other change:

| Dependent | Cards off (a = 0 at every zoom) |
|---|---|
| Card layer, `cardLayout`, `summaryHits` / `summaryAll` | never called / empty; `summariesDrawn()` empty at every zoom |
| Stars layer (stars, table links, badges as arcs from afar, orbits, trajectory arrows, names) | drawn at `LA = 1` at every zoom; below `OVERVIEW_K` names follow M8.1 (`OVERVIEW_NAMES` core names), D-040 rules unchanged |
| Disc labels (`discLabel`) | every constellation's name, counts line and composition bar above its disc (`bare` is false, no `avoid` pass); `labelHits` are these labels: drag by the name as before M8.3 |
| Source systems | no group card; each drawn as its own neutral constellation (halo at full strength), still stacked left of the business domains (placement is in `layout()`, unchanged) |
| Routes | individual routes (`grouped` false, never bundled), ends on disc edges, obstacles are discs; density, **All routes**, focus unchanged |
| Meta line | `routeCounts(0)`: the individual routes. Large project at Fit: `· 20 of 45 routes` (was `9 of 15` with the group card; the 45 / 20 of D-067 at the stars scale) |
| Fit | `cardsAt` empty: frames the discs with `FIT_PAD` (D-073), as at the stars scale |
| Picking, hover, double-click | star interactions (`cardsOn()` false); tables picked from `FAR` (0.5) as before M8.3 |
| Trajectory from afar | stars with dimming and arrows; no card frames |
| Export | `image()` paints the same `paint`: no card (export shows the screen, D-021) |
| `CELL`, `summaryScale`, `starsScale`, `SUMMARIES` | still computed by `summarise()` (the disc label's bar and the text summary read `SUMMARIES`); `SUMMARY_CELL` / `SUMMARY_FADE` have no effect |

Two adjustments beside the switch:

- **Zoom floors.** `zoomOnConstellation` / `zoomOnRow` (double-click on a disc, the text summary's buttons) raise their scale to `starsScale * 1.05` only to land past the cards: through one helper `starsFloor()` = `state.cards ? starsScale * 1.05 : 0`, so with cards off a double-click frames the constellation as before M8.3 (`0.92 · min(w, h) / (2r + 60)`). The search keeps `max(t.k, 1.5, starsFloor())`.
- **Hooks.** `geometry().summary` gains `on` (`state.cards`); `geometry().routes[].bundle` is `null` while cards are off (no route is bundled). `GROUPED` is kept (the text summary's "Source systems" Zoom frames them; `systems.grouped` unchanged).

**The text summary `#u-sum` stays**, unchanged: it is the accessible list of constellations built from `SUMMARIES`, never from the canvas cards (confirmed: `renderSummary` reads `SUMMARIES`, `zoomOnConstellation`, `zoomOnRow`). Its heading keeps the word "Summary" / « Synthèse ».

**Control.** `<label id="u-cards-row" class="check"><input type="checkbox" id="u-cards"> <span id="u-cards-t"></span></label>` in `page.html` right after `fieldset#u-fold` (before the Links heading): the two "how constellations are drawn" choices sit together. Always shown (any page shows cards when zoomed out). Native checkbox: one Tab stop, Space toggles, native focus ring, 44 px under `pointer:coarse` (the `.check` rule of **All routes**). Ticked = cards on. `title` = `T.cardsTitle`. On change: `state.cards`, `CARDS.set`, hint refreshed, `overCard = null`, tooltip hidden, live region `T.cardsOn` / `T.cardsOff`, `draw()`. **No layout** (`layoutRuns` unchanged) and **no camera move** (the Fit button gives the Fit of the new state; toggling at a Fit camera never jumps). Caches: `cardCache` is not read while off; `routeLayout`'s cache misses because its `cl` changes (object ↔ null); no key change needed.

**Hint.** `hintText()` = `T.hint` + fold clause + (`state.cards ? T.hintCards : ''`) + `T.hintRoutes`.

**Strings (`T`, en / fr):**

```js
cards: 'Summary cards', cardsTitle: 'From afar, draw each constellation as a card: its counts, layers and main tables',
cardsOn: 'Summary cards shown when zoomed out', cardsOff: 'Summary cards hidden: stars at every zoom',
// fr
cards: 'Cartes de synthèse', cardsTitle: 'De loin, dessiner chaque constellation comme une carte : ses effectifs, ses couches et ses tables principales',
cardsOn: 'Cartes de synthèse affichées de loin', cardsOff: 'Cartes de synthèse masquées : des étoiles à tout zoom',
```

**Viewer memory.** `boot.js`: `CARDS = memoryOf('dbtscope-cards')` (key `dbtscope-cards:<title>`, words `shown` / `hidden`), passed as the last parameter: `startUniverse(DATA, openInDetail, detailIds, LAYOUT, EXPORT, openFlow, FOLD, GROUPING, CARDS)`. `state.cards = (CARDS.get(['shown', 'hidden']) ?? word(DATA.defaults.cards)) === 'shown'`; `CARDS.set(word(on), word(DATA.defaults.cards))` writes only when it differs from the page default. Never in the layout, never exported; universe only (the detail has no cards). Format `dbt-erd-layout` v1 and its key untouched (D-013).

**Config and payload.** `domain/config.UniverseSettings.cards: StrictBool = True` (after `fold`; the docstring's key list gains `cards: true  # summary cards when zoomed out (D-074)`); allowed in presets (`PresetUniverse` inherits it). `domain/payload.UniverseDefaults.cards: bool = True`, the new last key (after `fold`). `domain/universe.universe_data` passes `cards=universe.cards`. A non-bool is the existing strict-bool one-line config error. No CLI option, no summary row.

**Payload diff (one regeneration).** Each of the 7 reference payloads (`tests/fixtures/m3-reference-pages/payloads/*.json`) differs only by `"fold":<b>}` → `"fold":<b>,"cards":true}` in `universe.defaults` (+13 bytes each); the pages (`*.html`, `expected.json` hashes and sizes) are regenerated once, with the asset changes; the user guide's quoted page sizes follow (`test_docs`).

**Performance.** With cards off at Fit on the large project the page draws 933 stars (1,374 shown) from afar, as before M8.3 and as at the stars scale today (measured at M8.4: frame 9.5 ms at stars, routes 2.7 ms). Budgets, cards off on the large page and the 1,500-table payload: page open ≤ 3 s, a filter change ≤ 1 s, a frame at Fit (`drawMs()`) ≤ 100 ms, `routesMs()` ≤ 5 ms on the large page and ≤ 30 ms on the 1,500-table payload (M8.4's budget for its 210 routes), toggling ≤ 100 ms with no layout run.

Error handling: nothing new fails; without localStorage the choice lasts for the session (`memoryOf`).

## Rejected options

- **A config key only** (no control): the user wants the cards "easy to remove" as a viewer.
- **Switching `starsScale` / `SUMMARY_CELL` to 0**: spreads the condition over the scale constants; the zoom floors would then collapse silently. One flag read by `summaryAlpha` (and `starsFloor`) is the switch.
- **Keeping the group card (or bundled routes) with cards off**: the group card is a summary card; the user wants none.
- **Clearing `GROUPED` when off**: needs a layout run to toggle and removes the text summary's "Source systems" Zoom.
- **A three-valued choice (auto / always / never) or a toolbar button**: one checkbox in the panel, next to the fold, is the page's pattern for drawing choices; cards are never forced at the stars scale.
- **Refitting on toggle**: moves the camera under the user; Fit is one click.
- **Hiding `#u-sum` when cards are off**: it is the keyboard and screen-reader path to every constellation, independent of the canvas.

## Built (developer's record)

- **Routes budget.** The 1,500-table payload keeps M8.4's `routesMs` ≤ 30 ms (measured 7 to 13 ms with its 210 routes); the large project keeps ≤ 5 ms. The first draft of this note said "≤ 5 ms on both": the 210-route hairball never made 5 ms, with the cards or without.
- **Labels with the cards off (review loop, `tests/browser/test_cards_off_labels.py`).** At Fit (1280 x 800, links both) the seven source systems were stacked so tightly that their labels (name, counts, bar: 46 px, constant screen size) printed over the neighbouring discs (5 labels on a disc, 22 route / label crossings). Kept, in the preferred order: **(a)** the collide radius of the constellations (`roomOf`) and the stack of source systems keep, between two discs, the height of a label plus a corridor for a route (`LABEL_PX` = 46 + 30 screen px, so a route can still thread between a disc and the label below it) and the lane between two columns of systems is at least `CHANNEL_PX` = 24 px (it was 30 world px, 5 px on screen at Fit). The label's world size depends on the scale: `layout()` places once with no room, takes the scale Fit would give on a 1000 x 700 reference stage (`refScale`, the `fitScale` that `frameScale` also uses; the stage's real size is never read, so a layout is deterministic and the same positions serve both states of the cards), and places again with the room. One pass was enough (the scale falls from 0.160 to 0.147, the room was sized for 0.158: no label on a disc). Pins and saved arrangements win as before. **(b)** with the cards off the routes treat each constellation's label as a soft outline (`labelRect`, kind `label` in `routesDrawn()`): a route is first laid out against the discs only (so the 0 blocked / 0 crossings guarantee is the one of M8.4), and laid out again with the labels, within a budget of 80 curves per layout (`ROUTE.LABEL_BUILDS`), only when it crosses one; the new route is kept when it clears the discs and crosses fewer label samples. (Route / label crossings 22 to 14 at Fit; the rest are routes threading the stack of source systems, which no detour within the fixed budget clears. A first version with the labels as hard obstacles left 7 routes `blocked` by discs and doubled `routesMs`.) **(c)** the D-073 rule also applies to every disc label with the cards off: a label whose usual place is taken moves round its disc and shrinks, never dropped, and is also kept off every other constellation's disc (`onDisc`); it matters on a stage smaller than the reference (1024 x 640: one label pair overlapped without it).
- **Measures** (large project, cards off, Fit 1280 x 800): label on a disc 5 to 0, label on a label 0 to 0, Fit scale 0.160 to 0.147 (cards on: 0.159 to 0.147, 6 cards at level `rows`, 0 crossings, business domains in flow order, systems left of them), `routesMs` 1 to 4 ms. acme_retail's golden fixture is regenerated (its business domains settle on another arrangement because the room is larger than the old 30 px + 15 %; Fit scale 0.461 to 0.474, see its `notes.md`).
