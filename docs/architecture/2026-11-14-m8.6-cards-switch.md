# M8.6 — Summary cards are optional, off by default (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE (explicit user decision of 2026-11-14, recorded as D-081; it supersedes clauses of D-065, see § Impacts)

## Context

Request: the user tried M8 and finds the summary cards that appear when zooming out unwelcome ("I want these tiles to be easy to remove… off by default"). The summary cards (D-065 to D-067, and the group card of D-069) become an option, **off by default**: a checkbox in the universe panel, a per-viewer memory, and an optional config key so that a project can open with them. Roadmap M8.6.

Current state (`render/assets/universe.js`): one function decides how much the cards are drawn, `summaryWeight(k)` (0 at `s = k · refR ≥ 3 px`, 1 at `s ≤ 2 px`). Everything card-related reads it, directly or through `tablesLive()` (`summaryWeight(t.k) < 0.5`): `paint` (`a`, `cardsOn`, `starsOn`, `fade`, `routesOn = k < 0.6 || cardsOn`, `cardPlans`, `drawSummary`, `drawTrajectoryArrows`, the labels' drag hits `a < 0.5`), `routeView(a, …)` (individual set at `1 − a`, bundled set at `a`, current set `a ≥ 0.5`), `routeLine` (disc / card outline interpolated by `a`), outlines, the pointer (`pick`, `constellationAt`, `rowAt`, hover, double-click, click), the hooks `geometry().routes` and `summary`. Viewer choices go through `common.js viewerMemory(name, values)` (`dbtscope-constellations`, `dbtscope-fold`, `dbtscope-detail-fold`; D-048, D-063, D-073).

## Chosen design

### 1. Config and payload (domain)

- `config.UniverseSettings.cards: StrictBool = False`, after `fold`; docstring line `cards: false  # from afar, draw each constellation as a summary card (D-065, D-081)`. Allowed in presets with no change (`PresetUniverse` subclasses it). A non-boolean is the one-line error `universe.fold` already gives (`config <path>: universe.cards: …; fix the file`, exit 1).
- `payload.UniverseDefaults.cards: bool = False`, **last key** of the universe defaults (after `fold`); `universe.universe_data` passes `cards=universe.cards`. No logic in Python; no CLI change; no built-in preset sets it.
- **Payload diff**: every page's universe defaults end `…,"fold":true,"cards":false}` instead of `…,"fold":true}` (14 bytes). The reference pages (`tests/fixtures/m3-reference-pages/`, all cases) are regenerated once, in the asset commit; old and new payloads are equal once `"cards"` is removed (checked before regenerating, said in the commit message and in `notes.md`); the pages also change by `universe.js`, `page.html` and `page.css`.
- `dbt-erd.yml` gains one optional key; `dbt-erd-layout.json` (format `dbt-erd-layout` v1), its localStorage key and every layout scope are untouched. No migration: an existing file without the key gets `false`, which is the one user-visible change (pages that opened on cards now open on stars), asked by the user.

### 2. The page: one switch point

- `state.cards = cardsMemory.get() ? cardsMemory.get() === 'on' : DATA.defaults.cards`, with `const cardsMemory = viewerMemory('dbtscope-cards', ['on', 'off'])` beside `byMemory` / `foldMemory`. Key `dbtscope-cards:<title>`, written only when it differs from `DATA.defaults.cards` (else removed, by `viewerMemory.set`), never in the layout object, never exported, kept by **Reset layout** (as the other viewer choices).
- **The switch point is `summaryWeight`**: `summaryWeight = k => (state.cards ? <today's formula> : 0)`. `refR`, `kStars()`, `kCards()` keep their values (computed by `layout()`, reported by `geometry().summary`). With cards off, every dependent reads weight 0, i.e. the M8.3 rule's own "stars" end, at every zoom:

| Dependent | Cards off |
|---|---|
| `paint`: stars, halos, links, table names, constellation labels (name, count, composition bar) | drawn at full alpha at every zoom (`fade` = 1, `starsOn`), as before M8.3; names from afar by `fitNames` below 0.6 (D-058) |
| `drawSummary`, `cardPlans`, `summaryHits`, `cardInfo` | not computed, not drawn; `summaryHits()` empty |
| Group card of the source systems (D-069) | not drawn: each system is its own neutral disc with its label, in its grid place (placement unchanged) |
| Routes | `routesOn` = `k < 0.6` (before M8.3: routes from afar, table links when close); the individual set only (bundled set at alpha 0, never current, `geometry().routes` has no `bundled: true`); the spotlight never resolves to `GROUP`; density, `strongOf`, "N of M routes", **Show all routes**, hover / focus spotlight unchanged |
| `routeLine` | clipped at the disc (a = 0) |
| Obstacles (`obstacleOf`) | unchanged (disc + card square + `MARGIN`): paths are the same in both states, so toggling never invalidates `routeCache` |
| Pointer | `tablesLive()` true: stars are picked, clicked and double-clicked at every zoom from `FAR` up as before; `constellationAt` uses discs; labels are drag handles at every zoom |
| Trajectory from afar | table-level trajectory edges at every zoom (D-018 as before M8.3); no card frames, no aggregated arrows |
| `fit`, `frame`, `zoomTo` | unchanged; the floors `kStars() · 1.05` of the constellation double-click and of search stay (they land on readable stars, a reason that does not depend on cards) |
| `#u-summary` (text summary) | unchanged and always present: it is the canvas's text alternative at every scale (D-066), including the source-systems block and the routes |
| `geometry()` | `summary.weight` 0, `summary.cards` []; new `summary.on` (`state.cards`) |
| Export | `image()` repaints through `draw`: the screen as shown, so no card when off |

- **Toggling** runs no layout and never moves the camera: `state.cards` changes, `cardsMemory.set(state.cards ? 'on' : 'off', DATA.defaults.cards ? 'on' : 'off')`, `hoverCard = hoverRow = null`, `draw()`. Ticking at a scale below `kCards` shows the cards at once (that is the zoom the viewer chose); unticking there shows the stars as small dots. Nothing is unavoidable here.
- **Ticked = today exactly**: every frame, hit and hook equals the M8.5 page with the same camera.

### 3. Control, strings, accessibility

- Markup (`page.html`), right after `#u-detail`: `<label id="u-cards-row"><input type="checkbox" id="u-cards-box"><span id="u-cards-label"></span></label>`, always shown (any page can show cards by zooming out), styled as `#u-routes-row` (`page.css`: one rule if needed for its top margin). It is a native checkbox: Tab, Space, the label clickable; 44 px under `pointer: coarse` through the existing `.panel label` rule (D-035). `title` on the row.
- Strings (`T` of `universe.js`; French UI word per the glossary, "fiche de synthèse", D-027, not "carte"):

| Key | en | fr |
|---|---|---|
| `cards` | Summary cards | Fiches de synthèse |
| `cardsTitle` | From afar, draw each constellation as a card: its counts by layer and its main tables | De loin, dessiner chaque constellation comme une fiche : ses effectifs par couche et ses tables principales |
| `cardsLiveOn` | Summary cards on: they replace the stars from afar | Fiches de synthèse activées : elles remplacent les étoiles de loin |
| `cardsLiveOff` | Summary cards off: stars at every zoom | Fiches de synthèse désactivées : des étoiles à tous les zooms |

  The change is announced in the existing live region `#u-live` (as the fold). The hint (`T.hint`) names no card and stays.

### 4. Labels at Fit with cards off (large project)

Computed from the M8.4 placement (Fit 0.187, systems' r ≈ 340 world px, stacked at `2 · (1.15 r + 30)`): the vertical gap between two stacked system discs is about 30 screen px and a constellation label reaches 34 px above its rim (name at −22, 12 px font), so a system's label touches at most the bottom 3 to 5 px of the upper system's halo, near its lowest point; labels never overlap each other (each sits in its own column, its rows a disc apart); business domains keep the collision radius under which their labels were readable before M8.3. **Readable; no placement change.** The developer measures it at Fit, 1280 × 800 and 360 px, both groupings (label rectangles from a hook over `taken`): labels pairwise disjoint, none over another constellation's disc by more than 6 px. Only if that fails: the smallest fix is that a constellation label which is not `free` (business labels first, then systems in grid order) is skipped, its name staying in the tooltip and `#u-summary`; it changes nothing with cards on at Fit (labels are not drawn at weight 1).

### 5. Responsibilities

Reused: `viewerMemory`, `summaryWeight` (the one switch point), `$u('live')`, `.panel label`. New: `cardsMemory`, `state.cards`, the `#u-cards-*` row. Responsibility map row (`docs/architecture.md`): "Whether summary cards are drawn: the viewer's switch, default from `universe.cards` (page, D-081)" → `universe.js state.cards`, `cardsMemory`, read only by `summaryWeight`. Glossary row of **summary card**: add "optional, off by default (D-081)".

## Rejected options

- Hiding cards by a high threshold or a scale floor: still a card when zooming far out; not "easy to remove".
- Keeping the group card when cards are off: it is a summary card (D-069); systems as discs read as before M8.3.
- Changing obstacles or placement with the switch: the routes would recompute and move on toggle; ticked would no longer be today's page.
- The choice in the exported layout, or a new layout scope: D-048, D-063 rule it out; a view preference is per viewer.
- Omitting `cards` from the payload when false: D-048's reason (logic in `payload.py`).
- Dropping the `kStars` landing floors when off: two rules for one gesture; the floor is about readable stars.
- "Cartes de synthèse" in French: one word per concept, the glossary's French UI word is "fiche de synthèse" (D-027).

## Test plan

Every use of `large_page` in `test_summary_cards.py` and `test_systems_routes.py` moves to `large_cards_page`; their synthetic builders (`hostile_payload`, `other_payload`, `sized_payload` in `test_summary_cards.py`, also used by `test_contrast.py`'s card section; the payloads at the lines building `"defaults"` in `test_systems_routes.py`) set `"cards": True`; `write_example_page` and the acme / shop part of the threshold test build with `CARDS_ON` (acme's `dbt-erd.yml` has no `universe:` section; the shop through `build_page(CARDS_ON)`). Left as they are (they pass with weight 0): `test_roles.py` (its camera step to `kStars`), `test_entities.py`, `test_universe_layout.py`, `test_constellations.py`, the example-page text-summary tests of `test_summary_cards.py` (they now also prove the summary stays without cards).

New, `tests/browser/test_cards_switch.py`:
- default large page (1280 × 800 and 360 × 740): box unticked; `summary.on` false, weight 0, no card and `summaryHits()` empty at Fit, at `kCards / 2` and at the minimum of `ZOOM_EXTENT`; `drawnNames()` non-empty at Fit; constellation labels drawn; no `@systems` outline, no bundled route; the 6 constellation labels' rectangles pairwise disjoint and within § 4's bound.
- ticking at Fit: cards drawn (weight 1, 6 cards), camera transform and `layoutRuns` unchanged, `localStorage['dbtscope-cards:<title>'] === 'on'`, `#u-live` says `cardsLiveOn`; unticking removes the key (default reached) and draws stars; reload with the key: opens with cards, box ticked.
- `universe.cards: true` in `dbt-erd.yml` and in a preset file: opens with cards, box ticked, no key stored; unticking stores `off`; reload keeps it off.
- the key `dbtscope-cards:<title>` never appears in an exported layout file (export after toggling).
- keyboard: Tab reaches the box, Space toggles it; under `touch` its row is ≥ 44 px.
- en / fr label, title and live strings (` ` before `:`).
- toggling with a trajectory on: no aggregated arrows when off, table edges drawn; image export equals the screen when off.
- budgets with cards off: large project page open ≤ 3 s, a Types filter change ≤ 1 s, `perf.draw` ≤ 50 ms at Fit, `perf.routes` ≤ 30 ms on the first frame at Fit (individual routes, as D-071's stars-scale measure, with its retry); 1,500-table payload: open ≤ 3 s, filter change ≤ 1 s, `perf.draw` ≤ 100 ms at Fit.

Edge cases: toggle at mid-crossfade; toggle while hovering a card (no stale hover); toggle in layer mode; a page with no core table; storage unavailable (session only).

## Built

Built as designed (D-081), with the points below.

- **Payload.** Every reference page's universe defaults end `…,"fold":true,"cards":false}`; the ten cases were compared with their old payloads before regenerating (equal once `,"cards":false` is removed, one occurrence each). `page.css` is unchanged (the new row sits in the panel like `#u-routes-row`, no rule needed). Page sizes quoted by the user guide: 291 to 293 kB and 294 to 296 kB (the two example summaries; +2 kB).
- **Labels at Fit with cards off (§ 4), measured** (hook `labelRects()`, the label rectangles of the last frame, against each other and against the other constellations' discs, large project, `tests/browser/test_cards_switch.py`):

  | Screen, grouping | labels drawn | label pairs overlapping | worst reach into another disc |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1280 × 800, domains (12) | 12 of 12 | 0 | 6.3 px (`claim` over the disc of `auction`) |
  | 1280 × 800, layers (4) | 4 of 4 | 0 | 0 px |
  | 360 × 740, domains | 9 of 12 | 0 | 25.3 px (`claim` over the disc of `auction`) |
  | 360 × 740, layers | 3 of 4 | 0 | 9.4 px (`products` over the disc of `vault`) |

  At 1280 the prediction holds: no label overlaps another, and a label grazes a neighbouring disc by 6.3 px (a few pixels over the 6 px bound, the label rectangle being at least 88 px wide), so the placement stays. At 360 px, before the fix, six pairs of system labels overlapped (`raw_crm` / `raw_dms`…): the note's smallest fix was applied, a constellation label that is not free is skipped (business first, then the systems in grid order; its name stays in the tooltip and `#u-summary`). It runs for every state of the box and every zoom: with the cards ticked, labels are drawn from the crossfade up (while the stars layer shows), where the skip rule applies too, where they do not meet. The 25 px reach of a business label over its neighbour's disc at 360 px is not covered by that fix (two business domains side by side, as before M8.3); it is recorded here, the test bounds it (7.5 px at 1280, 27 px at 360), and a placement change is left to the user.
- **New hook** `labelRects()` on the universe handle (screen-pixel rectangles of the constellation labels), in place of reading `taken` from outside; documented in `render/CLAUDE.md`.
- **Tests.** `test_cards_switch.py` (27 tests), unit additions in `test_config.py`, `test_payload.py`, `test_universe.py`, `test_page.py`, `test_cli.py`; the card tests of `test_summary_cards.py` and `test_systems_routes.py` run on `large_cards_page` / `CARDS_ON` with their assertions unchanged (their payload builders and the acme / shop pages set `cards` on). Each new behaviour was proved to fail once by a brief mutation (always on, no memory write, no label skip, `bool` instead of `StrictBool`, no `cards=` in `universe_data`, no markup).
