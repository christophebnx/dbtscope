# M8.4 — Source systems apart, readable routes (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE (the user asked for the whole of M8 to be built without stopping; every point the brief leaves open is settled below and recorded in D-065, D-066 and D-067). The before / after screenshots are approved by the user at the close of M8 (brief § 10); that does not block this phase.

## Context

Request: mark the domains made only of plumbing as **source systems** (decided in Python, overridable per domain rule), set them apart upstream in the universe (neutral, stacked on the left, one group card at overview, their own heading in the Domains panel), place business domains along the data flow, and make **routes** readable: curved from outline to outline around the other constellations, only the strongest by default, all of a constellation's on hover or keyboard focus, with a tooltip and a control to show them all. Roadmap M8.4, brief `docs/briefs/2026-11-12-m8-synthesis.md` § 8 (findings 3 and 4 of § 2; constraints § 4; § 3 "Out": a relation-driven placement of the raw vault and the flow between layers stay out). It takes the roadmap "Later" item on curved routes and neighbours lit on hover (the shareable link stays "Later").

Current state of the code (after M8.3):

- `domain/universe.py`: `domain_of` (dbt group > `meta.domain` > first `domains` rule > `group_by`), `_domains` (payload order: size descending, "No domain" last; label and colour from the rule **with the same key**, whatever resolved the domain), `UniverseDomain(key, label, color, owner)`. `domains` rules are plain `config.Rule`s (a `system` key would be ignored today).
- `cli._summary`: `universe:` row `1,374 tables in 12 domains, 892 relations, 1,360 lineage links`, then `fold:`.
- `render/assets/universe.js`: `layout()` aggregates `agg` (distinct table pairs per constellation pair, from `edges`, which follow the Links selector) and `flow` (net lineage per pair) into `routes` (`source`, `target`, `c`, `dir`: the sign of the net flow when it exceeds 20 % of `c`); d3-force (seed 42) places every constellation with collide, link (by `c`), `forceX(d.rank * W)` (domain mode: the mean layer position of its tables, `flowX` 0.06), `forceY(0)`, charge, then 100 ticks of collide only; pins (`fx`/`fy`) win. `paint()` draws routes below `OVERVIEW_K` (0.6) as straight segments between `edgeOf` ends (centre at `a = 0`, card edge at `a = 1`), width `(1 + log2 c) · 1.6` screen px, arrowhead at 55 %. Drag end pins **every** constellation of the scope. M8.3 left the seam `summaryRect(d)` / `edgeOf(d, toward, a)` and the hook `geometry().routes`.
- Large project at Fit (1280 × 800): 12 discs, 12 cards (`a = 1`); routes straight from card edge to card edge, crossing other cards and discs (finding 3); the seven `raw_*` systems mixed among the business domains (finding 4).

## Chosen design

### 1. Source systems: the rule (Python, D-065)

**One function, `domain/universe.source_systems(nodes, kinds, config) -> frozenset[str]`** (pure; called by `universe_data` once the nodes and kinds are built; unit-tested in `tests/unit/test_universe.py`). A domain key is a source system when:

1. **Override first.** A `domains` rule with that `key` sets `system: true` or `system: false`: that is the answer. The rule applies **by key, whatever resolved the domain** (dbt group, `meta.domain`, a rule's `match`, `group_by`), exactly as a rule's `label` and `color` already do in `_domains`: one lookup, `configured[key]`, no second mechanism. So a domain coming from a dbt group or a schema is overridden by writing `{key: raw_crm, match: [], system: false}` (an empty `match` labels or flags without capturing tables). Documented in the config reference.
2. **"No domain" (key `""`) is never a source system**, override included: it is the catch-all of tables nobody placed, not a system.
3. **Automatic**, when no override applies: every table of the domain (in the universe scope: `universe.select` / `exclude`, **not** the page filters) has a kind whose role is `technical` (built-in source, seed, snapshot included), **and the config gives a role to at least one kind** (`any(k.role for k in config.kinds)`, preset or file).
4. **At least one business domain remains.** If the automatic rule (3) would make every non-empty domain a source system, it makes none (there would be nothing to set them apart from). Overrides (1) still apply after this guard.

**Why the gate in (3), and what a no-preset page looks like.** Since M8.1 sources, seeds and snapshots are `technical` on every page, so without the gate a domain made only of sources (the `raw` schema of the `shop` fixture, the `raw` domain of `layers_domains`) would become a source system on a page with no preset: the brief says "with no technical role in the page (no preset), nothing changes". Built-in roles are a default dbtscope applies to resource types; they say nothing about the project's conventions. A project that gives roles to its own kinds (a preset, or `role:` in its `kinds`) has told dbtscope how to read its tables, and then a domain of only technical tables is plumbing. The gate is "any role", not "a technical role": a config that declares `hub: core` but no staging kind still has sources-only domains that are plumbing. **A no-preset page with a sources-only domain**: that domain stays an ordinary domain (`system: false`), coloured from the palette, in the Domains list among the others, with its own summary card; it changes only as every domain does in this phase (routes; placement along the flow, § 3, where a domain read by others and reading none naturally sits on the left).

**Filters never change the flag**: Python decides once, over the universe scope. A source system whose tables are all filtered out disappears like any domain.

**Config** (`domain/config.py`): `DomainRule(Rule)` with `system: StrictBool | None = None` (`null` or absent: automatic). `Config.domains: DomainRuleList`. A non-bool value is a one-line config error through `validation_message`, as `universe.fold` today (`config dbt-erd.yml: domains[2].system: …; fix the file`). Presets: `PresetDomainRule(DomainRule)` with `PRESET_SETTINGS`; `Preset.domains: PresetDomainRuleList` (the built-in presets have no `domains`; a preset file may set `system`). `layers` rules stay `Rule`: a `system` key there is ignored in `dbt-erd.yml`, an unknown key in a preset (as `role`, D-058). Lists still replace lists: no inheritance of `system` from a preset's domain rule (presets carry no domains in practice; one rule, D-058's `_inherit_roles` is about kinds only). `dbt-erd.yml` only gains an optional key; a config written before M8.4 is valid and means "automatic".

**Payload** (`domain/payload.py`): `UniverseDomain.system: bool = False`, **the last key** of a domain (after `owner`). `False` is written for every non-system domain (never omitted: D-048 rejected omitting defaults).

**CLI** (`cli._summary`): the `universe:` row says `1,374 tables in 12 domains (7 source systems), 892 relations, 1,360 lineage links`; the parenthesis only when at least one domain is a source system (`_count(n, 'source system')`). Other rows unchanged. Refines D-029.

**Example** (`examples/acme_retail/dbt-erd.yml`): its domains mix sources, bronze, silver and gold, so none is automatic, except **Shared**, whose only table is the `calendar` seed (technical), read by `dim_date` in Sales. It is a shared business reference, not an upstream system: the example's rule becomes `{key: shared, …, match: ["calendar"], system: false}`, which also documents the override. acme_retail therefore has **no source system**; its page changes only by the placement along the flow (§ 3) and the routes (§ 5–7). `tests/fixtures/acme_retail/expected.json` gains `"source_systems": 0`; the large project's `expected.json` gains `"source_systems": ["raw_auction_house", "raw_billing", "raw_claims", "raw_crm", "raw_dealer", "raw_telematics", "raw_web"]` and the summary line.

### 2. The page reads the flag (D-066)

`universe.js`: `SYSTEM = new Set(DATA.domains.filter(d => d.system).map(d => d.key))`; the page never computes it. **`DOMAIN_ORDER`** = business domains in payload order, then source systems in payload order: the one order of domains in the page, used by the Domains panel, `DOM_POS` (layer-mode band order, so D-048's "in the order of the Domains panel" stays true) and `BY.layers.rowList` (layer cards' rows). With no source system it equals `DATA.domains` (pages without systems unchanged).

### 3. Placement in domain mode (D-066)

- **Business domains** are placed by d3-force with seed 42 as today, **without the source systems in the simulation**, with one change: the x target. `BY.domains.rank` (mean layer position) is replaced by a **domain flow rank**, computed in `layout()` by `common.js flowRanks` (reused, invariant 2) on the graph of the business constellations: members their keys; edges, every route between two of them whose `dir` is not 0, from the upstream end to the reader; `layerOf`, the layer position of the constellation's median visible table (`null` when that is the catch-all `other`). A domain read by others sits left of them; a domain with no cross-domain lineage keeps a floor from its layers, as before. Cycles go to `back` (ignored). `forceX(W · rank)` with `W` the mean business radius × 2.4 and **`flowX` 0.1** (was 0.06; measured by the developer on both projects: the acceptance test of § Test plan decides, the value is recorded in `docs/progress.md`). Link, collide, y, charge and the settle pass are unchanged. In layer mode nothing changes (`rank` = layer position, `flowX` 0.3).
- **Source systems** are placed **after** the simulation, deterministically, by **`stackSystems()`**: in columns left of the business domains' bounding box (pinned business domains included). Column height cap `H` = the business bounding box height (with no business domain: `√Σ(2R)²`, `R` the collide radius `r + 30 + 0.15 r`); number of columns `c = ceil(Σ 2R / H)`; the systems are sorted by **barycentre y** (the mean y, weighted by route `c`, of the business domains they have routes to; the business box's centre when none; then payload order), dealt round-robin into the columns (column 0, nearest the business domains, first), each column stacked by a 1-D sweep that keeps each disc as close to its barycentre as the spacing `R_i + R_j` allows; column 0's centre x = business box left − `max R` − 30 − its own `max R`, the next columns step left by `2 · max R + 30`. Sources stay next to the business domains that read them, routes stay short. No randomness, O(n log n).
- **Pins win.** A pinned business domain is fixed in the simulation as today; a pinned system stays at its pin, and an unpinned system's sweep treats a pinned disc lying in its column's x range as a fixed interval. **Reset layout** clears the pins and gives the new placement.
- **Old arrangements load unchanged.** A drag pins every constellation of its scope (`saved[G.pins]` holds them all), so a layout saved before M8.4 has every domain that was visible then pinned: they load where they were. Only domains without a pin (new since, or filtered out when the layout was saved) take the new placement, around the pinned ones, as any unpinned domain does today. The format, the scopes and the localStorage key are untouched (D-013, D-048).
- **"Smaller"**: the disc of a source system keeps its packing (D-040: its reserved boxes are already those of technical stars, 0.75 × since D-058); shrinking the world geometry would break the star-cell rule (D-063) and the zoom onto its stars. It reads smaller by its treatment: neutral halo, a 13 px name above the disc (business: 13 to 20 px as today) and, at overview, one group card for all (§ 4).
- **Neutral treatment** (domain mode): halo, ring and card border in `NEUTRAL` (`#8a94a6`, drawn through `CONTRAST.edge`, D-042), name ink `C.muted` (5.3:1, D-037); the stars keep their kind colours; the Domains panel dot keeps the domain's colour (so a system can still be told apart). **Layer mode**: systems keep their domain colours in their bands (bands are how a layer shows its domains; a neutral tint would make the seven bands of Sources identical); only the band order changes (`DOMAIN_ORDER`).

### 4. The source-system group card (D-066)

- **When**: domain mode, cards drawn (`a > 0`), **at least two visible source systems**. With one, it has its own card (neutral border). In layer mode, none.
- **What**: one item of `cardLayout` keyed `'\u0000systems'` (no domain key can collide with it), anchored on the centre of the systems' bounding box (screen), `r` = half its diagonal (the "stays on its constellation" bound of D-063). Content: header "Source systems" (`T.systems`, neutral ink), counts line `7 systems · 504 tables` (`plural(m, T.systemsN)` · `plural(n, L.tables)`; never reduced by filters other than what they hide), then one **row per visible system** (label, count, bar by kind as the layer rows, `drawBar`), in stack order (top to bottom), at most `GROUP_ROWS` = 8, then `+ N` (a muted line). No main tables. Its ladder stops at `name` (it never becomes a bare pill: it carries seven names).
- **Interactions**: hover → tooltip "Source systems" with the list (first 8, `+ N`) and `T.rowZoom`; a click on a row → `zoomOnConstellation(system)` (zooming in shows each system as its own constellation: the group card fades with the cards, the systems' discs and stars appear); double-click → frames the systems' bounding box at least at `starsScale · 1.05`; dragging the header moves **every visible system together** and pins all constellations, as any drag (mouse only, D-045). Trajectory from afar: the group card is framed by the union of its members' sides and its trajectory line sums theirs (D-064's rules).
- `summaryRect(d)` of a grouped system returns the group card's rectangle; `edgeOf` follows (§ 5).
- **Text summary** `#u-sum`: business constellations first, left to right; then a heading `h4` "Source systems: 7 · 504 tables" with a **Zoom** button (frames the systems), then one section per system, top to bottom. Layer mode unchanged.

### 5. Route geometry (D-067)

**Outline of a constellation**, the one place being `summaryRect` / `edgeOf` (extended, not duplicated): its **disc** while stars are on (`a < 0.5`), its **drawn card** while cards are on (`a ≥ 0.5`; a grouped system: the group card; a card at level `count` / `frame`, not drawn: its disc). **`edgeOf(d, toward, a)`** returns `lerp(discEdge, cardEdge, a)`, where `discEdge` is the point of the disc's circle toward `toward` (was the centre at `a = 0`) and `cardEdge` M8.3's ray exit: continuous in `k`, so routes never jump.

**`routePath(route, obstacles)`** (new; one responsibility: the drawn path of one route), in screen px, deterministic:

1. Ends `A = edgeOf(from, next, a)`, `B = edgeOf(to, prev, a)` (`next` / `prev`: the first / last waypoint, else the other end's centre).
2. **Obstacles**: the outline of every drawn constellation except the two ends (a bundled route: except every member of the group), inflated by `ROUTE_MARGIN` = 6 screen px. Broad phase: only obstacles meeting the chord's box inflated by the largest detour so far.
3. **Unobstructed first**: a quadratic curve bowed by `ROUTE_BOW` = 8 % of the chord, to the right of lower key → higher key (two routes never coincide; deterministic). If its samples clear every obstacle, done.
4. **Else, waypoints**: from the straight chord, up to `ROUTE_PASSES` = 6 passes: take the first obstacle the current curve enters (in order along the chord); add a waypoint at its centre + `(R + margin)` along the chord's normal, on the side opposite the obstacle's centre (if the centre is on the chord: the side with fewer obstacle centres within `2R`, then the right side); if that waypoint lies inside another obstacle, try the other side; keep the side with less penetration. At most `ROUTE_WAYPOINTS` = 4. The curve is a centripetal Catmull–Rom through `A`, the waypoints, `B`, drawn as cubic Béziers.
5. **Check** = `ROUTE_SAMPLES` = 24 samples per Bézier segment; a sample inside an obstacle (distance to a disc centre < `r·k`, or inside a card rectangle) is a **crossing**, except samples inside an end's own outline.
6. **When avoidance is impossible** (a pinned overlap, an end inside another outline, 4 waypoints not enough): draw the candidate with the least total penetration and mark the route `blocked` (hook). Never loops, never throws.

**Cache**: the drawn paths are computed in screen offsets from the camera's translation, as `cardLayout` is (a pan reuses them), and cached by `k`, `layoutRuns`, `dragRev`, the card cache, the route set shown (strongest / all / focus), the trajectory. **Cost**: O(routes × passes × samples × nearby obstacles); budget `perf.routes` ≤ 5 ms per frame on the large project with every route, ≤ 30 ms on the 1,500-table payload with every route, measured by the hook `routesMs()`.

**Drawing**: width `(1 + log2 c) · 1.6` screen px as today; colour `C.route`; a route **carrying lineage with a direction** (`dir ≠ 0`) gets an **arrowhead at the reader's end** (the downstream end), placed on the curve just outside the reader's outline and aligned with the curve's end tangent; a route with lineage but no net direction has none. With **Links** on `both`, one line per pair (never two parallel lines): a route **carrying no lineage** (relations only) is **dashed** (6 · 3 screen px), any other solid; the tooltip gives the split. With `relations` only, all routes are relation routes (dashed); with `lineage`, all solid. (Dash and colour values: ui-designer.)

**Bundling through the group card**: while the group card is drawn, routes with a grouped system at one end and a business constellation at the other are summed per business constellation into one **bundled route** (`c`, relations, lineage summed; `dir` from the summed net flow, same 20 % rule), ending on the group card; routes between two systems are not drawn. Cross-fade with `a` as D-063: the bundled set at alpha `a`, the individual system routes at `1 − a`; routes between business constellations at full alpha. Drawn below `OVERVIEW_K` as today.

### 6. Default density: the strongest routes (D-067)

- **Weight** of a route: `c`, the distinct table pairs it carries under the Links selector (today's `agg`: a relation and a lineage edge between the same two tables count once); folded tables are carried by their entity, which is in the same constellation (D-062), so nothing changes there. `layout()` also counts per route `rel` (relations) and `lin` (lineage links) for the tooltip, from the visible tables, Links selector applied.
- **Strongest** (`strongRoutes()`): for each constellation, its routes sorted by weight; it keeps its `STRONG_ROUTES` = **2** heaviest (3 in the first draft; measured, see UI design), **ties included** (every route as heavy as its third). A route is drawn when it is among the strongest of **either** end. Computed on the individual graph and, for the group card, on the bundled graph (the group is one constellation there). O(R log R), in `layout()` (filters change it), the bundled selection when cards are first drawn after a layout.
- **The meta line** says it, only while routes are drawn (`k < OVERVIEW_K`) and some are left out: `… · 12 domains · 18 of 31 routes` (en `${num(shown)} of ${plural(total, T.routesN)}`, fr proposal `18 routes sur 31`; wording to the ui-designer). `total` and `shown` count the routes of the current set (bundled while the group card is on).
- **Control**: a checkbox **"All routes"** (`#u-routes`, fr « Toutes les routes »), in the panel under **Links**; default off; **no config key and no viewer memory** (exploration, not a lasting choice; the payload stays as it is); hidden when the strongest already are all routes. On: every route drawn, the meta clause gone.

### 7. Hover, keyboard focus, tooltip (D-067)

- **Focus of a constellation** (`routeFocus`: a constellation key or the group): set by the mouse over a disc or a card (not over a table, not in trajectory mode), and by **keyboard focus** inside its row of the **Domains panel** (`focusin` on the filter row's checkbox) or its section of the text summary `#u-sum` (`focusin` on the `.sum-c`); cleared on `mouseleave` / `focusout`. Effect, while routes are drawn: **all its routes** are drawn (strongest or not), its neighbours (the other ends) at full alpha, every other constellation (halo, card, stars, label) at `DIM_CTX` and every other route at `ROUTE_DIM` = 0.15. One redraw, no layout. Below the route scale it is visible as soon as the camera zooms out; at `k ≥ OVERVIEW_K` nothing changes (table links speak).
- **Text equivalent**: each `.sum-c` of the summary gains a line "Routes: reads Raw CRM (36), Raw dealer (12) · read by Auction (8) · relations with Account (5)" (the constellation's routes, all of them, heaviest first; wording to the ui-designer): the canvas is silent (D-064).
- **Route tooltip**: hit-testing on the drawn samples of the last screen frame (distance to the polyline ≤ `max(6, width / 2 + 3)` screen px); precedence: table, card part, route, disc. Content: `Raw CRM → Vehicle` (upstream → reader; a route without direction reads `Vehicle – Account`), `36 lineage links · 4 relations` (`plural`, en / fr), and the direction in words, `Vehicle reads Raw CRM` (none without a direction); a bundled route also lists its systems (first 5, `+ N`). Not in trajectory mode.
- **Trajectory mode** keeps its own edges and dimming: routes stay drawn at `DIM`, strongest only (or all with the control), no focus, no route tooltip.
- **Export** shows what is on screen: the route set (strongest or all), the bundling and the cross-fade; hover **and keyboard focus** are pointer-like state and are left out, as hover is today (D-021, D-064).

### 10. Test hooks

- `geometry()`: each constellation gains `system` (bool); top level gains `systems: { keys, grouped }` and `routes` gains, per route, `rel`, `lin`, `strong` (in the strongest set), `bundle` (the group key or null); `flowRank` per business constellation.
- `routesDrawn()` (new, like `summariesDrawn`, kept out of `geometry()`): for each route drawn by the last screen frame, `{ source, target, bundled, c, rel, lin, dir, alpha, dashed, blocked, arrow: [x, y] | null, samples: [[x, y], …] }` in screen px, plus `obstacles: [{ key, kind: 'disc' | 'card', x, y, r | w, h }]` (the outlines the frame used).

### Error handling

Config: a non-bool `system` is a one-line config error (exit 1, as D-041 / D-058). Nothing else can fail: a blocked route is still drawn; the stack and ranks never throw on cycles (`flowRanks` puts them in `back`). No file, no format change.

## Rejected options

- **Source systems by name** (`raw_*`, `src_*`) or by the `source` resource type alone: Python decides from roles (brief § 4), nothing Data Vault–specific.
- **The automatic rule on every page** (built-in roles count): a no-preset page would change, against the brief; **gating on "a technical role declared"** rather than "any role": a config with `hub: core` and no staging kind would never detect its sources-only domains.
- **Overrides only for rule-declared domains**: a second lookup for domains from dbt groups or `group_by`; rules already label and colour any domain by key.
- **"No domain" as a source system**: it is a catch-all, not a system.
- **The flag in the browser, or depending on filters**: Python decides once (brief § 4).
- **Systems sorted last in the payload**: changes the payload order of every page with systems; the page orders them (`DOMAIN_ORDER`).
- **Systems in the force simulation with strong x / y targets**: collisions push them out of their column and the order is not guaranteed; the stack after the simulation is exact and cheaper. **Fixing them with `fx`/`fy` before the simulation**: they would not avoid pinned business domains.
- **Shrinking the systems' discs**: breaks D-040's room and D-063's star cell. **Neutral bands in layer mode**: the seven bands of the Sources layer would be identical.
- **A group card in layer mode**: the systems are bands of two layers there, not constellations.
- **Visibility-graph shortest paths or edge bundling (force-directed, hierarchical)**: heavier, less predictable; at most a few dozen routes and obstacles need a few waypoints. **Straight routes with transparency**: crossings stay.
- **Two lines per pair (relations and lineage)** when Links is `both`: doubles the hairball; one line, dashed for relations only, with the split in the tooltip.
- **A fixed number of routes for the page** or **a weight threshold**: a small constellation would lose all its routes; per constellation, union of both ends, keeps every constellation connected.
- **A config key `universe.routes` or a viewer memory** for the control: no user asked; keeps the payload diff to the flag; revisit on request.
- **Keyboard focus on the canvas** (roving focus over discs): the canvas is not a keyboard widget (D-064); the Domains rows and the summary already are.
