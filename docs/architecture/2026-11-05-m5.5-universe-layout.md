# M5.5 — Universe layout (design note)

Status: DONE (built, tested and reviewed on 2026-11-05). Roadmap M5.5, asked by the user on 2026-11-05. The payload, the layout file format, the localStorage key and the config are unchanged, and saved arrangements still load (§ 6). Decision D-040 is recorded in `docs/decisions.md`. It refines D-014 (the inner placement of a domain is no longer a d3-force simulation; the domains still are) and the "local simulation" option of brief § 7.

## Context

The request: on `examples/acme_retail`, the tables of a domain are placed in no order (sources, staging, vault and marts mixed). The circles are large and mostly empty, with tables crowded in the centre and names overlapping. At the keys and all-fields levels, cards overlap. The user wants:

1. tables placed along the data flow, layers from left to right;
2. circles sized to their content;
3. no overlapping cards.

Filters, search, trajectory, flow, exports, semantic zoom, the fixed seed, performance (brief § 11) and saved arrangements must keep working.

Current state (`render/assets/universe.js`):

- `layoutDomain(members)` runs one d3-force simulation per domain, cached by `[domain, detail, links, members]`. Its forces:
  - a weak `forceX` toward a per-layer target `tx` (strength 0.12);
  - `forceY(0)`;
  - links (strength 0.2) and a charge;
  - circle collisions of radius `sqrt(w·h)·0.6 + 2`.
- Then `separate(ns)` runs: at most 40 sweeps of a rectangular push.
- Why it fails:
  - **Order is lost.** Link and collision forces outweigh the layer force, so layers mix.
  - **Labels overlap.** A name box is about 130 × 26 world px, but it collides as a circle of radius about 35, so labels overlap sideways. The names box is also centred on the star, while the label is drawn *below* the star (`y = sy + r + 2`, 14 px high), so part of the label is outside the reserved box.
  - **Cards overlap.** 40 sweeps do not settle dense clusters at the card levels.
  - **Circles are too large.** `R` is the distance from the bounding-box centre to the farthest corner, plus 30. A wide, sparse simulation gives a large, mostly empty disc.
- `layout()` adds saved offsets: `REL = saved.nodes[id] || [x − cx, y − cy]`, an offset from the domain centre. The domain simulation places discs of radius `max(R, reach)`, with domain pins `saved.domains` as `fx` / `fy`. `P[i]` is then domain centre + offset.
- Every table has a layer in the payload. With configured `layers`, tables that match no rule get the catch-all key `other` (`classify.OTHER`), always appended **last** to `DATA.layers`. Without configured layers, layers come from folders and are ordered by `universe.flow_rank`.
- `flow.js flowRanks(members, edges, layerOf)` is the pure column helper of the flow view. It puts lineage first, uses layers as a floor, and is cycle-safe.

## Chosen design

Only the page changes: `common.js`, `universe.js` and `flow.js`. `domain/`, `storage/`, `render/page.py`, the CLI and the payload stay as they are.

### 1. Columns along the data flow: `flowRanks` moves to `common.js` and accepts "no layer"

- `flowRanks` moves unchanged from `flow.js` to `common.js`, next to `trajectory`, since two views now use it. `flow.js` calls the global as before. The `render/CLAUDE.md` exception "a view never declares a global, except `flowRanks`" goes away.
- One extension: `layerOf(i)` may return `null` (no layer). Such a table gets no floor (base 0) and counts in no layer's span. The flow always passes a number (`LAYER[..].pos`), so its ranks are unchanged and the `flowRanks` checks of `test_flow.py` stay green.
- The universe calls it once per domain, with:
  - `members`: the visible tables of the domain;
  - `edges`: the domain's lineage edges `[parent, child]` (from `DATA.lineage`, whatever the Links selector) that keep layers apart, i.e. both ends in the same layer, or the child without a layer. An edge from a later layer back to an earlier one is ignored for placement, so layer bands stay strict;
  - `layerOf`: the position of the table's layer in `DATA.layers`, or `null` for **a table without a layer**. That is a table whose layer key is `other` when `other` is the last of `DATA.layers` (the catch-all of configured layers; no payload change, § 7).

### 2. Inside a domain: layer bands packed into a disc (`layoutDomain`, rewritten, deterministic)

No force simulation and no randomness inside a domain. Steps:

1. **Bands.**
   - A table with a layer goes to its layer's band.
   - A table without a layer goes to the band whose column range `[floor, floor + span)` holds its rank, or the last band when the rank is beyond every range, so it sits after its latest parent.
   - A domain with no layered table has one band.
   - Bands are ordered by layer position, left to right.
2. **Order inside a band.**
   - Sort by the `flowRanks` rank (in-layer lineage depth: a model sits after the models it reads in its own layer).
   - Then by barycentre: the mean normalised order index (0–1) of its neighbours in earlier bands, over lineage and the drawn links. Bands are processed left to right; a table with no such neighbour gets 0.5.
   - Then by name, then by `unique_id`, so the order is total.
3. **Reserved boxes.** `box(n)` gains `oy`, the vertical offset of the reserved box centre from the table's point `P` (the star centre):
   - **names:** `w = max(6 + 6.2 × name.length, 2r + 4)`. This is an estimate of the 11 px label in its bold form; it is never measured, so the placement does not depend on the fonts installed. The height runs from the star top to the label bottom: `h = 2r + 18`, `oy = 7`, with `r = starR(n)`.
   - **keys / all:** the card as today, `oy = 0`.
4. **Disc packing** `packDisc(items, R)`, where `items` are the bands' ordered boxes:
   - Fill vertical strips from `x = −R` to the right. A strip takes the next items of the current band while their stacked height fits the disc chord over the strip's x-extent: `2·sqrt(R² − max(x0², x1²))`, or `2R` when the strip spans `x = 0`. The strip width is the widest item.
   - A strip that cannot take even one item moves right by 4 px and tries again.
   - A new band always starts a new strip.
   - Gaps: `PAD` between stacked boxes (6 at names, 10 at the card levels), `STRIP_GAP` between strips of a band (10), `BAND_GAP` between bands (28, room for the layer marker).
   - Each strip is centred on `y = 0`.
   - It fails when an item would end beyond `x = R`.
5. **Smallest disc.**
   - `lo = max(sqrt(Σ (w+PAD)(h+PAD) / π), max hypot(w, h) / 2)`.
   - `hi` starts at `1.5 · lo` and doubles until the packing succeeds (at most 30 doublings).
   - 18 bisection steps follow, keeping the smallest `R` that packs. The final packing is at that `R`.
   - The domain centre is the disc centre `(0, 0)`.
   - The radius kept for the domain is `max corner distance + MARGIN (30)`, the same margin as today's `R` and `reach`.
6. **Result** (cached as today, same key): `{ ns: [{i, x, y}], R, rank, bands: [{layer, x0, x1}] }`.
   - Offsets are relative to the disc centre: `cx = cy = 0`, so `REL = [x, y]`.
   - `bands` holds the x-range of each band, for the layer markers (§ 4).

What this guarantees:

- **Layer order.** Every table of layer *L* (not dragged) lies strictly left of every table of layer *L + 1* in the same domain.
- **No overlap.** No two reserved boxes overlap, at any level. This is true by construction, so the rectangular pass of brief § 9 is no longer needed for computed positions (§ 3).
- **Fit.** The disc is the smallest the strip packing allows.
- **Reproducibility.**
  - The same input gives the same positions bit for bit, on any machine: total orders, estimated widths, no `Math.random`.
  - The domain simulation keeps `d3.randomLcg(42)`.

### 3. Rectangular de-overlap: only around dragged tables (`separate`, extended)

- `separate(rects, pinned)` stays the one rectangular pass. It is now called in `layout()`, per domain, **only when the domain holds saved table offsets**.
- Pinned (dragged) tables never move. A computed table that overlaps one is pushed away by the whole overlap; two computed tables share the push as today.
- Sorting ties break on the table index, so the pass is deterministic.
- It runs at most 200 sweeps and stops at the first sweep with no move.
- The rects are the reserved boxes (with `oy`).
- The domain radius then grows through `reach`, as today, which now adds `oy` too.
- **Across domains:** domains collide as discs of radius `≥ reach` of all their tables (unchanged), so tables of different domains do not overlap. If the browser test (§ Test plan) finds a cross-domain pair, the developer adds a deterministic circle de-overlap of unpinned domains after the 100 collision-only ticks. It must not move pinned domains.

### 4. Layer markers (light, no text on the canvas)

- At `k ≥ 0.6` (the constellation and detail zooms), in each domain with two bands or more, a 1-px line is drawn between consecutive bands, in the middle of the band gap. Its colour is `C.cross`, and it is clipped to the disc (chord).
  - It is drawn after the halo and before links and tables.
  - It is not drawn from afar (`k < 0.6`), where routes speak.
- The domain tooltip gains one line: `Layers: Sources → Staging → Raw vault → Marts` (fr `Couches : …`), the labels of the domain's bands in order. It uses `T.layers`, `L.colon` and `esc`, and the catch-all band takes its payload label ("Other" / "Autre").
- No layer names on the canvas: they would compete with table names. The panel's Layers list already holds them in flow order.
- The ui-designer may change the marker's colour and dash; the zoom threshold and "no canvas text" are part of this design.

### 5. Consumers of the reserved box

`B[i]` gains `oy`. Every use of `B` as a rectangle around `P` adds it:

- `fit` (trajectory framing);
- `inView` in `paint`;
- `reach`;
- `separate`.

`place(id)` returns the reserved rectangle (`oy = 0` at the card levels, so `test_contrast.py` is unaffected). `pickAt` keeps the star-distance hit at names and the card rectangle at the card levels.

`starR`, `drawStar`, `drawCard`, `drawNames` (label below the star, `free` check kept), routes, edges, trajectory, search, `reveal`, `relayout`, `image()` and the flow do not change.

### 6. Saved arrangements (invariant 9, D-013)

The format and the key are unchanged:

- `dbt-erd-layout.json` (format `dbt-erd-layout`, version 1);
- `universe.<detail>.domains` (absolute domain centres) and `universe.<detail>.nodes` (a dragged table's offset from its domain's centre);
- the localStorage key `dbt-erd-layout:<title>`.

Every existing file and stored arrangement loads. Dragged positions win:

- **A pinned domain** stays exactly where it was saved (absolute world position, as today).
- **A dragged table** keeps its saved offset from its domain's centre. The meaning of the offset is unchanged ("from the domain's centre"); the centre is now the packing disc's centre instead of the old bounding-box centre.
  - Under M5.5, an old offset was taken in an older, larger circle, so the table can now sit outside the compact content. The halo grows to include it (`reach`), and computed tables under it are pushed off by § 3.
  - Nothing is lost or rewritten. The file is only rewritten when the user drags again (as today), and **Reset layout** gives the new computed placement.
- **Tables never dragged** take the new computed placement. Today only dragged tables are saved, so an old arrangement mixes the user's moves with the new bands.
- **Pinned domains with new radii.** Discs are mostly smaller, so pinned domains do not collide. Where a disc grows (rare, at the card levels), two pinned domains may overlap: pins win (the user placed them), and **Reset layout** resolves it. This is documented in the ui-spec.

No migration and no approval needed: no data is lost, and the files stay readable both ways (an M5.5 file opens in an older page with the same meaning).

### 7. Payload

No change. The layer order is `DATA.layers`. Lineage is `DATA.lineage`. The catch-all is recognised as key `other` in last position:

- Without configured layers, a folder literally named `other` that sorts last would be placed by lineage instead of as a band. That is harmless, and documented.

The reference payloads stay byte-identical (`git diff --exit-code tests/fixtures/m3-reference-pages/payloads/`); only page hashes and sizes in `expected.json` change.

### 8. Performance

- The packing is O(n log n) per domain, with about 50 trial packings of O(n). That is far cheaper than today's per-domain simulations (≤ 120 ticks with collisions and charge).
- The domain simulation is unchanged.
- The per-domain cache is unchanged, so a domain filter recomputes only changed domains.
- Expected: well under the budgets (open < 3 s, filter change < 1 s), measured by the new perf test.

### 9. Test hook

`views.universe.geometry()` is read-only, for the browser tests, and listed with the other hooks in `render/CLAUDE.md`. It returns:

- `layoutMs`: the time of the last `layout()`, from `perf.layout`;
- `tables`: `[{id, domain, layer, x, y, w, h, oy}]` in world coordinates, with `x, y = P`;
- `domains`: `[{key, x, y, r, bands: [{layer, x0, x1}]}]`, with bands in world coordinates.

### Responsibility map (`docs/architecture.md`)

| Responsibility | Where | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Columns along the data flow: lineage, layers as a floor, tables without a layer by lineage (page) | `render/assets/common.js flowRanks` | moved from `flow.js`, `null` layer added; row reworded |
| Placement of a domain's tables: layer bands packed into the smallest disc (page) | `render/assets/universe.js layoutDomain`, `packDisc` | new |
| Rectangular de-overlap around dragged tables (page) | `render/assets/universe.js separate` | extended (pinned) |

Glossary: add **layer band**, the strip of a domain where the tables of one layer are placed (universe). It is not "column" (the flow's word) and not "lane".

## Rejected options

- **Stronger forces** (a strong per-layer `forceX`, rectangular collision, then `separate`). Overlaps and order still depend on tuning. It runs the most expensive part of the layout, and its circles stay loose. Layers mix wherever links pull.
- **`flowRanks` lineage-first columns as bands** (as in the flow). A backward lineage edge would put a table outside its layer band, so "layers left to right" would not hold. The flow keeps lineage-first: a different view with a different aim.
- **One strip per in-layer lineage depth.** A long chain inside a layer (the 1,500-table payload has ten per layer) becomes a thin horizontal line and a mostly empty disc. The depth stays a sort key inside the band.
- **Measuring labels with `measureText`.** The placement would depend on the installed fonts, so the same page would have different shapes on two machines and saved offsets would land differently.
- **Elliptic or rectangular domains.** Halos, routes, collisions, hit tests and framing all assume discs.
- **Layer names drawn in each domain.** They add clutter next to table names. The tooltip line and the panel list hold them.
- **A payload flag for "no layer"** (or the layer order). It is a payload change for information the page already has.
- **Rewriting old saved offsets** (rescaling them to the new disc). It changes the user's file meaning silently. Offsets keep their meaning, and **Reset layout** exists.
- **Keeping the 40-sweep `separate` on every domain.** It is not needed after a packing that cannot overlap, and it costs time on 1,500 tables.

## Test plan

Default suite:

- `test_page.py`:
  - the rendered page holds `function flowRanks(` exactly once;
  - `flow.js` no longer declares it;
  - the `<script` parity checks stay green.
- `test_reference_pages.py` is green after one regeneration, with payloads byte-identical.

Browser suite: a new `tests/browser/test_universe_layout.py`. Geometry comes from `geometry()`; checks are computed in Python. "Big" is the 1,500-table payload of `test_trajectory_perf.py` (`universe_payload`). "acme" is `example_page`. Each check runs at the three detail levels unless said otherwise.

1. **Flow order.**
   - In every domain, for consecutive layers present (in `DATA.layers` order, the catch-all excluded), the max right edge of layer *L* is < the min left edge of layer *L + 1*. This implies that the mean x increases.
   - Runs on acme and big.
   - With a shop config whose `layers` leave some models unmatched (`other`): each `other` table with an in-domain parent is in the band of that parent or a later one; one without parents is in the first band.
   - `flowRanks` with a `null` layer, checked by pure evaluation: no floor, ranks after parents, and the spans of the other layers are unchanged.
2. **No overlap.**
   - No pair of reserved boxes (`x ± w/2`, `y + oy ± h/2`) overlaps, across the whole universe, at names, keys and all, on acme and big. Tolerance 0.
3. **Names readable.**
   - At names level, frame each acme domain at `k = 1` (camera set through the zoom).
   - Record `fillText` (as `test_contrast.py` does) and count the names drawn among the tables whose star is in view: all of them, allowing up to 2 % for font metrics.
   - The drawn label rectangles do not overlap.
4. **Circles fit their content.**
   - For each domain with ≥ 10 tables: `Σ w·h / (π r²) ≥ 0.25` at names and `≥ 0.35` at keys and all, on acme and big.
   - Each box lies inside its disc: corner distance `≤ r`.
   - The developer records the measured minima in the test docstring.
5. **Determinism.**
   - Two fresh loads of the same page give identical `geometry()` (exact equality) at each level.
   - Unticking a domain and ticking it back gives the same positions.
6. **Saved arrangement.**
   - Import a layout with one pinned domain and one dragged table offset (the `OLD_LAYOUT` shape of `test_page_polish.py`).
   - The domain centre equals the pin. The table's `P` equals centre + offset. At keys level, no computed box overlaps the dragged one. The disc contains it.
   - `test_layout_exported_before_m5_2_imports_and_exports_unchanged` stays green. A localStorage arrangement gives the same result as the imported file.
7. **Performance** (big, visible tab, median of 3):
   - from navigation start to the end of the first layout `< 3000 ms`;
   - a detail change (`#u-detail`) to keys and to all `< 1000 ms`;
   - unticking a domain `< 1000 ms`;
   - `geometry().layoutMs < 1000` at each level.
   - `test_trajectory_perf.py` and `test_flow_perf.py` stay green.
8. **Markers and tooltip.**
   - At `k ≥ 0.6`, an acme domain with 4 bands draws 3 separators: recorded `lineTo` calls inside the disc, or a pixel check in the band gap.
   - At `k < 0.6` it draws none.
   - The domain tooltip holds the layers line in en and fr (`Couches : …`).
9. **Unchanged behaviour.**
   - The whole existing browser suite is green: trajectory, flow, export pixel comparison, search, filters, contrast, polish.
   - No console error.

## Revision after review loop 1 (2026-11-05)

The first build followed § 2 to the letter and missed ask 2 ("circles sized to their content"): on acme_retail the discs filled 0.12–0.15, Marketing came out larger than before M5.5 at the same Fit, and the test thresholds had been lowered instead of escalated. Two causes: names reserved one wide line each, and strips filled from `x = −R` at a bisected `R` leave the disc's edges empty. § 2 steps 3 to 5, § 3 and § 5 are replaced as follows; everything else holds.

- **Names on two lines (§ 2.3).** At names level, a name longer than 14 characters is drawn on two lines, split after the `__` nearest the middle, else after the `_` nearest the middle (a name without `_` stays on one line). `nameLines(name)` is the one helper, used by `box` and `drawNames`. The box: `w = max(6 + 6.2 × longest line, 2r + 4)`, `h = 2r + 4 + 14 × lines`, `oy = 7 × lines`.
- **Packing in one row (§ 2.4–2.5).** `packDisc` and the bisection are replaced by `packRow(bands, H, PAD)`:
  - each band is one column, or, when taller than its height cap `H[j]`, the fewest columns of height ≤ `H[j]`, balanced (`columns`: the smallest cap that keeps that number of columns, so no lone short column reads as an extra layer);
  - bands are laid out left to right from `x = −W/2`, each column centred on `y = 0`, `BAND_GAP` 20 (was 28), `STRIP_GAP` 8, `PAD` 6 at every level (was 10 at the card levels);
  - the smallest enclosing circle of the box corners (`d3.packEnclose` of the vendored d3, deterministic: its shuffle uses a fixed-seed generator) is moved to `(0, 0)`;
  - `layoutDomain` scans a common cap `H` over 25 geometric steps from the tallest box to the tallest band, keeps the smallest circle, then cuts each band to the circle's chord at its x-range while the circle shrinks (at most 6 rounds).
  - The radius is the farthest box corner + `margin()`: 16 at names, 24 at the card levels (was a fixed 30). `holds(dx, dy, box)` is the one helper for "the radius that holds this box", used by `layoutDomain` and `reach`.
- **Names at a distance (§ 5).** Names stay 11 px on screen while boxes are sized in world px, so below scale 1 a name is wider than its box. `drawNames` now skips an ordinary name whose backdrop would cover another star or leave its domain's circle (trajectory and hovered names still always compete; names never overlap each other, as before).
- **Barycentre.** Neighbours are a set, so a lineage edge also drawn as a link counts once.

### Acceptance (replaces test plan 4)

No left-to-right placement can make a circle narrower than its bands side by side: `r ≥ max(W/2, √(Σ w·h / π)) + margin`, with `W` the sum of each band's widest box plus the gaps. acme_retail's domains have four bands of two to eight wide boxes: at the card levels they are flat, and their fill bound is below the note's 0.25 / 0.35 (Product at keys: 0.134 at most). So the test asks, for every domain of 10 tables or more, a fill ≥ 0.25 at names and ≥ 0.30 (acme) / 0.35 (1,500 tables) at keys and all, **or** a radius within 15 % of that lower bound.

| acme Marketing (22) | 0.351 / 0.200 / 0.251 | 1.06 / 1.00 / 1.07 |
| acme Logistics (21) | 0.355 / 0.244 / 0.291 | 1.03 / 1.02 / 1.09 |
| acme Sales (17) | 0.295 / 0.215 / 0.257 | 1.06 / 1.02 / 1.09 |
| acme Finance (15) | 0.295 / 0.189 / 0.222 | 1.02 / 1.01 / 1.05 |
| acme Product (11) | 0.237 / 0.134 / 0.170 | 1.01 / 1.00 / 1.02 |
| acme Customer (8) | 0.151 / 0.101 / 0.143 | (one row) |
| 1,500 tables, minimum | 0.278 / 0.361 / 0.358 | — |

Against the layout before M5.5 (world radius): every acme domain is smaller at names (Marketing 317 → 231, Sales 299 → 209). At the card levels, where the old layout overlapped cards, Logistics and Sales are smaller at keys and all, and every domain but Product is smaller at all; at keys, Product (261 → 294), Finance (290 → 303), Customer (270 → 276) and Marketing (359 → 361) grow, Product (all) too (311 → 317). They sit at their lower bound: their old circles were smaller only because cards overlapped.
