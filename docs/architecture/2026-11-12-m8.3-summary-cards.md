# M8.3 — Summary cards at overview (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE

Every change below is foreseen by `docs/briefs/2026-11-12-m8-synthesis.md` § 4 and § 7 and by `docs/roadmap.md` M8.3. Nothing is computed in Python: no payload key, no config key, no layout scope, no CLI change; `dbt-erd.yml`, `dbt-erd-layout.json` and the localStorage keys are untouched, so no released format changes and no migration. No decision is superseded: D-018, D-040, D-048 and D-058 are refined (§ Impacts). The open points the brief leaves to the architect and the ui-designer (threshold, footprint, bar scale, number of main tables, text alternative, trajectory from afar) are decided below with their reason; none is a choice between options with no documented preference. The user's approval of the new screenshots (done-when 4) is collected at the M8 close, as for M8.1 and M8.2 (brief § 10): it is an open point in `docs/progress.md`, not a stop.

## Context

Request: at overview scale, draw each constellation of the universe as a **summary card** (name, table and entity counts, one row per layer, or per domain in layer mode, with a bar split by kind, and its main core tables) instead of as stars, with a continuous transition while zooming and no new control, so that a 1,400-table project opens on a readable structure while a small project opens on stars as today. Roadmap M8.3; brief § 7 is the spec (finding 1).

Current state (`render/assets/universe.js`):

- `layout()` computes the three sets of D-063 (`pass`, `FOLDNOW`, `visible`), per-constellation `local` layouts (cached), `constellations` (`k`, `x`, `y`, `r`, `n` over passing tables, `bands` with counts, `mix` by kind), `routes` (`c`, `dir`), and `P` / `B` / `R[]`.
- `paint()` draws, in world space: halos (or layer-mode band tints and rim arcs), band markers (k ≥ 0.6), routes (k < 0.6, straight centre to centre, arrowhead at 55 %), table links (hidden below 0.35), stars or table cards (keys / all fields above 0.35); then in screen space: each constellation's name, count and composition bar above its disc (`labelHits`, the drag handle), band names (layer mode, k ≥ 0.6), table names (`orderLabels`, `fitNames` below 0.6).
- Semantic zoom thresholds already in use: 0.35 (table links, table cards), `FAR` = 0.5 (tables can be clicked, dragged, double-clicked), 0.6 (routes vs links, names at Fit, badges, band markers and names).
- Interactions: hover tooltip (`pick` for tables at any scale, `domainAt` for discs; domain tooltip or `layerTip`), double-click (a table at k ≥ `FAR`, else `zoomTo` the disc at `0.92·min(w, h) / (2r + 60)`), drag by name (`labelHits`), click (trajectory at k ≥ `FAR`), search (`zoomTo` at max(k, 1.5)), trajectory dimming (`DIM`, `DIM_CTX`) and `drawTrajectoryEdges` at every zoom; `image()` repaints through the same `draw`.
- The page already has all that a card needs: kinds and roles (`KIND`, `ROLE_ORDER`), `DATA.relations` and `RELDEG` (relations per table, used by `fitNames`), domains and layers with labels and colours, `PARENT` / `HELD`, `trajectoryCounts`.
- Measures on the large project (M8.2): page open 0.45 s, a filter change 52 to 70 ms, page 894,827 bytes. Estimated Fit scale at 1280 × 800: about 0.15 on the large project (979 stars drawn folded, 12 discs), about 0.4 on acme_retail (82 stars, 7 discs); the developer measures both first (§ 7).

## Chosen design

### 1. Nothing in Python

Every number on a card is a count over the passing tables (filters are applied in the page), and the ranking of the main tables needs only the relations per table, which the page already computes for the names at Fit (`RELDEG`, D-058). A payload key would duplicate what the page computes and could not follow the filters. So `domain/`, `render/page.py`, `cli.py` and the payload are unchanged; the reference pages change by their script bytes only (§ Impacts).

**One ranking of the main tables (invariant 2).** `fitNames` becomes a view of a new `mainTables()`: per constellation, its drawn core tables sorted by relations (`RELDEG`), then links (`DEG`), name, id (today's order), the first `MAIN_MAX` = 5 kept; cached per layout as `fitNames` is today. Names at Fit take the first `FIT_NAMES` (3) of each list; a card lists up to `MAIN_MAX`. A core table is never folded (only descriptive tables have a parent), so drawn core = passing core.

### 2. When a card is drawn: one rule on the drawn size of stars, a crossfade, no camera change

- **Reference radius** `refR`, computed once per layout: the median world radius (`R[]`) of the drawn stars of the **highest tier present** among them (large if any core table is drawn, else medium, else small). It stands for "the stars a reader looks for": on a role-aware page the core stars, on a page without roles the ordinary ones. Not the median of all stars: on acme_retail most drawn stars are small-tier (staging, sources) and are already at the 2.5 px floor at Fit, while its core stars are 3 to 5 px.
- **Drawn size** `s = k · refR` (screen px), `k` the zoom scale.
- **Weight** of the cards, `summary = clamp(log(S_STARS / s) / log(S_STARS / S_CARDS), 0, 1)`: 0 at `s ≥ S_STARS` (stars only), 1 at `s ≤ S_CARDS` (cards only), linear in log k in between. Values: `S_STARS = 3`, `S_CARDS = 2` px (ui-designer may retune them in the UI design section, keeping the shape). Reason: below a 2 px radius the reference star is a dot under the 2.5 px floor of `drawStar`, so stars stop shrinking with the world while their reserved boxes do, and neighbouring stars merge; the band from 3 to 2 px is a zoom ratio of 1.5, about three wheel notches (1.15 each, D-045), long enough to read as a transition, short enough not to show two drawings for long.
- Equivalent scales, exposed to the tests: `kStars = S_STARS / refR` (stars fully drawn from there up), `kCards = S_CARDS / refR`.
- **Continuous both ways, no jump.** The weight is a pure function of `k` and of the layout; the switch never moves the camera, never relayouts and has no state, so zooming in and out over the band gives the same frames in reverse (no hysteresis needed). A transition animated by `zoomTo` or `fit` is drawn frame by frame through the same rule.
- **One rule for the whole universe**, not per constellation: every constellation shares `k`, and a per-constellation reference would turn a sources-only disc (small stars) into a card while its business neighbours stay stars, which reads as two maps. A relayout (filter, grouping, fold, detail level) recomputes `refR`, and the following Fit applies it.
- **Effect.** Large project at Fit (k ≈ 0.15, core `refR` ≈ 10): `s` ≈ 1.5 px, cards only. acme_retail at Fit (k ≈ 0.4, core `refR` ≈ 9): `s` ≈ 3.6 px, stars only, as today. The same rule shows cards on acme if the viewer zooms out past Fit, or on a narrow phone screen where its Fit scale drops (its stars would be merged dots there): one rule, recorded in D-065. The brief's "on a small project cards never show" holds at Fit on the default viewport, which the acceptance test pins (§ 7).
- **Detail level.** At keys and all fields the rule is the same (it reads star radii, not reserved boxes); constellations are larger there, so Fit is lower and cards show sooner. Where `kStars` is above 0.35 (a page without roles, small stars), table cards and summary cards crossfade together: continuous, accepted.

### 3. The card (geometry, content, drawing)

**Footprint.** A rounded rectangle centred on the constellation's centre `(d.x, d.y)`, width and height `1.8 · d.r`, corner radius `0.4 · d.r` (world units, scaled by `k`). Every point lies within `1.11 · d.r` of the centre, inside the collision radius of the force layout (`1.15 · d.r + 30`), so two cards never overlap where two discs did not, and the card covers the disc whose stars fade in when zooming: zooming in lands on the stars where the card was. The card has no layout of its own: it moves with its constellation (drag, pins, Reset), so D-013, D-040, D-048 and D-064 are untouched.

**Content** (computed in `layout()`, over passing tables, by a new `summaryOf(d)`; drawn in screen pixels, top-aligned, fixed font sizes whatever the zoom, as names):

1. **Header.** The constellation's name (`constellationLabel`): in domain mode in the domain colour made readable (`CONTRAST.ink`, D-037), in layer mode in `C.text` (as today's labels). Second line, muted: `plural(n, L.tables)`, then `· plural(entities, T.entitiesN)` while the fold is on (`folding()`, as the meta line, D-063): entities = drawn core and connector tables of the constellation.
2. **Rows.** Domain mode: one row per layer present, keyed by the tables' own `layer` (the catch-all "Other" layer, placed by lineage in someone else's band, has its own row), in the order of `DATA.layers` (flow order). Layer mode: one row per domain present, in the order of the Domains panel (`DOM_POS`), with the domain's colour dot. A row shows the label (`labelOf`), its table count (`num`, folded tables included: a fold never reduces a count) and a **kind bar**: 6 px high, segments in the order of the Types list (`ROLE_ORDER`, then payload order, so a kind sits at the same place on every row), each in `CONTRAST.edge(kind colour)` (a bar of 6 px or less is drawn in the edge colour, D-042), its length proportional to the row's count over the card's largest row (the number says the size, the bar the composition; the largest row fills the width so small kinds stay visible).
3. **Main tables.** Muted heading "Main tables" ("Tables principales"), then the first `MAIN_MAX` (5) of `mainTables()` for that constellation, one name per line in `C.text` with a dot in its kind colour (`CONTRAST.edge`, 3:1). Absent when the constellation has no drawn core table (source systems, Staging in layer mode), so a card never names an arbitrary table (D-058).
4. **Trajectory** (only while the mode is on, § 5).

**Room.** Content is laid out top-down inside the card's screen rectangle minus a padding, and what does not fit is dropped from the bottom, by priority: header name (always, ellipsised to the width), count line, rows (cut with a last muted row "+ N layers" / "+ N domains", `plural`), then main tables (fewer than 5, down to none). Texts wider than the card are ellipsised (`measureText`). A card narrower than its name keeps only the header. On the large project at Fit, at 1280 × 800, every business-domain card shows its header, all its rows and at least 3 main tables (acceptance, § 7); the ui-designer tunes sizes to meet it.

**Drawing.** `drawSummary(d, a)` in screen space after the world drawing, at alpha `a = summary` (times `DIM_CTX` for a constellation holding no trajectory table, as today): white surface (`C.card`), 1.5 px border in the domain's edge colour (domain mode, D-042) or `C.muted` (layer mode), header band tinted as today's halo; in layer mode the rim arcs of D-048 stay drawn around it in world space (the domain colours of the rows are their legend). Fonts: name 600 13 px, other lines 11 to 12 px; contrasts: `C.text` and `C.muted` on white (≥ 4.5:1), the name through `CONTRAST.ink`.

**Crossfade.** `paint()` draws, in order: halos (layer mode: tints and arcs), band markers (k ≥ 0.6), routes, table links and tables (stars or table cards, badges, rings) at alpha `1 − a`; then, in screen space, constellation labels above the discs (name, count, composition bar) at `1 − a`, band names, table names at `1 − a`, and the summary cards at `a`, last, so they cover what fades. At `a = 1` tables, links and names are not drawn at all (`drawnNames()` is then empty), which makes the overview frame cheaper than today's; at `a = 0` no card is computed for drawing. Routes stay under the cards. Their arrowhead moves from 55 % of the centre-to-centre segment to the middle of the gap between the two discs' rims (`routeLine(a, b)`, one helper used by the routes and by the trajectory arrows of § 5), so a card never hides it; M8.4 replaces the straight line in that one helper.

### 4. Interactions

The pointer acts on cards when `a ≥ 0.5` and on stars below; `tablesLive()` = `a < 0.5`.

- **Hover.** Over a card (`constellationAt(ev)`: the card's rectangle when `a ≥ 0.5`, the disc otherwise, replacing `domainAt`): the existing domain tooltip or `layerTip`, unchanged, plus over a row the action line "Click: zoom on Raw vault" ("Clic : cadrer Raw vault"). Table tooltips only when `tablesLive()` (no tooltip of a star that is not drawn).
- **Double-click on a card** zooms on the constellation, centred on it, at `max(frameK(d), kStars · 1.05)` where `frameK(d)` is today's `0.92·min(w, h) / (2r + 60)`: it always lands on stars, even where the disc is wider than the screen (narrow screens, very large constellations). Table double-click is unchanged above `FAR` when `tablesLive()`.
- **Drag by name.** The card's header rectangle is pushed into `labelHits` when `a ≥ 0.5` (the label above the disc otherwise): dragging a card moves and pins its constellation exactly as dragging its name does today, in the current scope.
- **Click on a row** zooms on its band: domain mode, the layer band of that key in `d.bands` (`bandSpans`: x from `s0` to `s1`, y over the disc's chord there); layer mode, the domain band; a row with no band (the catch-all layer placed by lineage in other bands) zooms on the whole constellation. Framed by `frame(…, animate)`, at a scale clamped to at least `kStars · 1.05`. Row hit rectangles are kept per frame in `summaryHits` (screen space, as `labelHits`), the cursor is a pointer over them. A click elsewhere on a card does nothing; outside any card and label, it leaves the trajectory as today.
- **Search, trajectory button, Fit, filters, panels**: unchanged entry points. Search goes to max(k, 1.5), past the card scale, through the crossfade.
- **Touch**: pinch and pan as today (D-045); a tap on a row zooms on it, a double-tap on a card frames it where the browser fires `dblclick`. Rows are canvas affordances (18 px high), not controls: the 44 px rule for controls (D-035) does not apply to them, as it does not to stars; pinch remains the primary way on touch.

### 5. Filters, search and trajectory

- **Filters** apply to every count, row, bar and main table (computed from `pass` and `visible` in `layout()`); a constellation with no passing table has no card (as no disc today). `refR` follows the drawn stars.
- **Search** is unchanged (§ 4). The search mark is drawn on the star once the camera arrives.
- **Trajectory from afar.** Each card holding trajectory members gets a line under its header, "5 upstream · 2 downstream" (`trajectoryCounts(direction, up, down)`, the shared wording, over the members in that constellation, start excluded), and a 2 px `C.text` frame; the card holding the start gets the start's double frame (two 1.5 px frames 3 px apart, as the start's double ring). Cards holding no member are dimmed to `DIM_CTX`, as discs are today. The table-level trajectory edges (D-018: drawn at every zoom) are drawn at `1 − a`; at `a` they are drawn aggregated: one arrow per ordered pair of constellations and side (upstream dashed `C.up`, downstream solid `C.down`), width `(1 + log2 c) · 1.2` px, through `routeLine`; edges inside one constellation are summarised by its counts. Computed when the trajectory or the layout changes, not per frame.

### 6. Keyboard and screen readers: a text summary of the constellations

- A new `<section class="sr-only" id="u-summary" aria-labelledby="u-summary-h">` inside `#u-stage` after the canvas (so it is `inert` with the stage while the flow view is open), heading `<h2 id="u-summary-h">` "Summary of the constellations" ("Synthèse des constellations"). For each constellation, in flow order of the drawing (`rank`, then name): an `<h3>` with its name, a `<p>` with its counts ("299 tables · 152 entities"), a `<ul>` with one `<li>` per row ("Raw vault: 266 tables — Hub 60, Link 70, Satellite 120, PIT 6, View 4"; layer mode "Vehicle: …"), and a `<p>` "Main tables: car_hub, vehicle_hub, …" (all of `MAIN_MAX`, not cut by room).
- Built with DOM calls and `textContent` (names and labels are untrusted, render/CLAUDE.md), rebuilt by `layout()` (filters, grouping, fold, detail level), never per frame; not a live region (a filter change would read the whole map). Always present, at every scale: it is the canvas's text alternative, not only the cards'.
- Sighted keyboard users: the canvas stays pointer-driven, as today (noted in M8.2); the summary adds no focusable element (a focusable control inside an `sr-only` box would fail visible focus, WCAG 2.4.7). Keyboard paths to the stars remain search, the trajectory button and Fit.

### 7. Small screens, export, performance

- **Small screens.** No DOM change outside the `sr-only` section: nothing new can scroll horizontally at 360 px. Cards follow the rule; at 360 px the room rule of § 3 drops rows and main tables first, never the header.
- **Image export.** `image()` repaints through `draw` at the current `k`, so it shows the cards, the crossfade and the trajectory marks exactly as on screen; hover is left out as today. `summaryHits` and `labelHits` are updated only on screen paints.
- **Performance budgets**, large project, browser (`large_page`), 1280 × 800:
  - page open to first frame ≤ 3 s (brief; M8.2: 0.45 s);
  - a filter change ≤ 1 s, at Fit (cards) and at `kStars` (stars);
  - the summary computation (`summaryOf` for every constellation, the `#u-summary` rebuild) ≤ 30 ms per layout (`perf.summary`);
  - a frame (`perf.draw`) ≤ 50 ms at Fit (cards only) and ≤ 100 ms in the middle of the crossfade (both drawings);
  - the existing trajectory, flow, fold and search budgets unchanged.
  The 1,500-table synthetic payload (no roles) keeps its budgets; it now opens on cards (its `refR` is medium-tier), which its existing Fit-scale tests must take into account (§ Test plan).

- Reused: `layout()`'s sets and counts, `bandSpans`, `rimHalf`, `frame`, `zoomTo`, `labelHits`, `constellationLabel`, `fitNames`' ranking (moved into `mainTables`), `ROLE_ORDER`, `CONTRAST.edge` / `ink`, `plural`, `num`, `labelOf`, `trajectoryCounts`, `DIM_CTX`, `draw` / `image`.
- Errors: none new. A page with no drawn star (everything filtered out) shows `L.empty` as today and no card.
- Test hook: `geometry()` gains `summary: { weight, refR, kStars, kCards, cards: [{ key, x, y, w, h (world), n, entities, rows: [{ key, n, kinds: [[kind, n]] }], main: [ids], traj: { up, down, start } }] }`; the handle gains `summaryHits()` (screen rectangles of the rows drawn in the last screen frame, with their constellation and row key), for the click tests only.
- Responsibility map, new or changed rows: "When the universe draws summary cards: weight from the drawn size of the reference star (page, D-065)" → `universe.js summaryWeight`, `refR`, `S_STARS`, `S_CARDS`; "Content of a summary card: rows per layer or domain, kind split, counts, trajectory counts (page, D-066)" → `summaryOf`; "Main tables of a constellation, ranked by relations (page; summary cards and names at Fit)" → `mainTables` (`MAIN_MAX`; the D-058 row now reads `starR`, `TIER`, `orderLabels`, `FIT_NAMES`, `fitNames` over `mainTables`); "Draw a summary card and its row hit regions (page)" → `drawSummary`, `summaryHits`; "The constellation under the pointer, disc or summary card (page)" → `constellationAt`; "Straight route between two constellations, arrow in the gap (page; routes and trajectory arrows, reworked by M8.4)" → `routeLine`; "Text summary of the constellations for screen readers (page, D-066)" → `renderSummaryText`, `#u-summary`.
- Glossary (D-027), new rows:
  - **summary card** (`drawSummary`, `summaryOf`, `#u-summary`): a constellation drawn at overview scale as its counts, rows and main tables instead of its stars (M8.3, D-065). Not tile, panel, badge. French UI: fiche de synthèse. A table drawn at keys or all fields stays a **card**; say "summary card" whenever both could be meant.
  - **main table** (`mainTables`, `MAIN_MAX`): a core table of a constellation among those with the most relations; listed on its summary card, named at Fit. French UI: table principale.
  - **summary weight** (`summaryWeight`): how much the summary cards are drawn at the current scale, from 0 (stars) to 1 (cards). Internal word; not in the UI.

## Rejected options

- **A Python ranking or summary in the payload** (`main_tables` per domain, counts per layer): cannot follow the page's filters, duplicates `RELDEG` and the page's counts (invariant 2), and grows every payload.
- **A fixed scale threshold** (cards below k = 0.3): ignores what makes stars unreadable (their drawn size); a page without roles has smaller stars and would switch later than it should.
- **The median radius of all drawn stars** as reference: small-tier stars are already floored at acme's Fit, so acme would open on cards (done-when 2).
- **A per-constellation threshold**: source-system discs (small stars) would turn into cards while business discs stay stars at the same zoom: two readings at once.
- **A density rule** (screen area per star): sound, but the brief asks for a rule on the drawn size of stars, and at names level the packing makes spacing proportional to the reserved boxes, so it would give the same switch with a less explainable constant.
- **A hard switch, or a switch with hysteresis**: a visible jump at one notch, and state that the export and the tests would have to reproduce.
- **A camera jump** (snapping to `kStars` when the wheel crosses the band): the brief forbids it.
- **A fixed-size card in screen pixels**: the card would not match the stars it stands for, and zooming in would not land on them.
- **The card as a disc** (content in the inscribed rectangle, 1.6r × 1.2r): about half the room of the chosen rounded rectangle, which on the large project leaves no room for the main tables of the smaller domains.
- **Bars proportional to the constellation's total, or to the largest row of the universe**: small kinds vanish into 1 px segments; the number already gives the absolute size.
- **Mix sorted by count per row** (today's composition bar): a kind would move from row to row; Types order makes rows comparable.
- **A config key or a panel control for the cards** (`universe.summary`): the brief asks for no new mandatory control; an optional one would be a permanent `dbt-erd.yml` surface (invariant 9) for a behaviour the viewer controls by zooming.
- **A visible summary panel or a new tab**: the brief places the cards inside the universe; a panel would repeat the canvas for sighted users. The text alternative is `sr-only`.
- **Focusable rows or buttons in the text summary**: invisible focus (WCAG 2.4.7); the canvas's keyboard access is a separate, unscheduled subject.
- **Hiding trajectory edges at card scale, or drawing them as today over cards**: the first loses where the trajectory goes, the second draws hundreds of lines converging on hidden points.
- **Drawing routes above the cards**: lines across the cards' text. M8.4 makes routes go edge to edge.

## Impacts

Decisions to append to `docs/decisions.md` (documentation step, with these texts):

- **D-065 — At overview scale a constellation is drawn as a summary card, by one rule on the drawn size of stars.** The reference radius is the median radius of the drawn stars of the highest tier present; with `s` = scale × that radius, the cards' weight is 0 from `s` = 3 px up, 1 from 2 px down, linear in log scale between (about three wheel notches); stars, links and names fade by `1 − weight`, cards by the weight; the camera never moves for it and nothing is stored. The card is a rounded rectangle 1.8 r wide and high, corner radius 0.4 r, centred on the constellation (inside its collision radius), moved with it; no layout, payload or config change. The pointer acts on cards from weight 0.5. Pages open on cards when Fit is below the band (the large project) and on stars when it is above (acme_retail, at 1280 × 800); a viewer who zooms out past Fit, or a narrow screen, may see cards on a small project too. Refines D-058 (names at Fit apply where stars are drawn), D-040 (no layout change; table names fade with their stars) and the zoom levels of `docs/brief-universe-view.md` § 4.
- **D-066 — A summary card shows counts, rows by layer or domain with a kind bar, and the main tables; it can be read as text.** Content of § 3 (header, entities while folded, rows in flow order or Domains order, 6 px bars in Types order scaled to the card's largest row, up to 5 main tables ranked as the names at Fit, `mainTables`, room-based truncation); interactions of § 4 (hover tooltip unchanged plus the row line, double-click lands on stars at least at the stars scale, drag by the header as by the name, click on a row frames its band); trajectory from afar (§ 5: counts and frames on cards, aggregated arrows at card scale); the route arrowhead in the gap between discs; the `sr-only` "Summary of the constellations" section rebuilt on each layout. Refines D-018 (trajectory edges at every zoom are aggregated between cards at card scale) and D-063 (the entity count also appears on cards).

## UI design

Task: at Fit on a 1,400-table project, answer without zooming "which domain (or layer) is big, how is it made, what are its main tables, where do I zoom?". Decisions served: which constellation to zoom into; which layer or domain band to open; "is this the one I look for?". A card is a reading surface with one action (zoom), not a control panel; nothing is added to the toolbar, the panel or the page. Brief § 7 and § 2 to § 6 above stay the spec; this section fixes the pixels, the wording and the room rules. Measured on the large project (1,419 tables, folded, domains) with the page's own `geometry()` before writing: Fit scale 0.181 at 1280 × 800 and 0.091 at 360 × 740; reference star radii 3 to 12 px world (core up to 12), so `s` ≈ 1.5 to 2 px at Fit, cards only.

- **Main names**: ellipsised at the inner width, each on one line, the dot at the padding, the name at +11 px.

No shadow, no icon, no decoration.

### What is dropped when the card is small (priority, first kept to last kept)

Decided in one function, top-down, so the result is deterministic from width and height only:

1. Header name (always; 11 px font then ellipsis; a card narrower than 24 px shows no text, only its frame).
2. Count line: full text; if it does not fit the width, the number only ("72"); entities are the first part dropped ("299 tables" then "299").
3. Rows: all of them when they fit. Row width rules, tried in order: bar >= 24 px; else the bar is dropped and label + count stay (inner width >= 56 px); else no row. Rows that do not fit in height: as many as fit minus one, then a muted last row "+ 2 layers" ("+ 2 domains" in layer mode, `plural`), a row whose count is not shown counts in that "+ N".
4. Main block: the separator and the names that fit after the rows, never displacing a row. With the heading when at least 3 names fit under it; else without the heading and as many names as fit (down to 1); none when 0 fit. Absent when the constellation has no drawn core table.
5. Trajectory line (when the mode is on) takes the place right under the count line before the rows (it is information about the current task, so it outranks the rows' fitting but not the header or count); rows lose a row of room.

Priority is for height; the width rules apply to every line separately. Results on the large project at 1280 x 800 (arithmetic: 5 + 15 + 12 + 3 + 14 x rows + 3 + 12 x names + 5): vehicle, auction, account, activity show the header, count, every row and the main block (3 to 5 names; heading on vehicle, auction, account); claim (121 px) shows header, count, 3 rows and 3 names (no heading); the seven source systems (about 112 px, 2 rows, no core table) show header, count and 2 rows, with empty space below. **Acceptance replaces the architect's "at least 3 main tables on every business-domain card"**: every business-domain card shows its header, its count and all its rows, and at least 2 main tables; the cards 130 px or higher show at least 3 (4 of the 5); the developer reports the measure. The tests derive it from the drawn-lines hook, not from a constant.
At 360 x 740 (cards 56 to 104 px): vehicle and auction (>= 92 px) show header, count and rows (2 or 3, the rest as "+ N layers"); account (83) header, count and 2 rows without bars ("Raw vault 266"); the others header and count (number only when narrow). Never horizontal scroll: the canvas is the stage.

### Domain card and layer card

Same anatomy. Domain mode: name in its colour, border in its edge colour, rows = layers (flow order, no dot). Layer mode: name `C.text`, border `C.muted`, rows = domains (Domains-panel order, colour dot), the world-space rim arcs of D-048 stay around the card as its legend. Rows in layer mode can be many (12 domains): the room rule keeps as many as fit then "+ N domains", and the main block is usually dropped: the layer card answers "which domains are here", the domain card "which layers and which tables". The sr summary always lists every row.

### Cross-fade with the stars

- Weight `a` per § 2 (S_STARS = 3, S_CARDS = 2 kept: a ratio of 1.5 is three wheel notches, enough to read as a transition). Stars, links, names, the constellation labels above the discs and the disc halos draw at `1 - a`; the card (surface, border, content) draws at `a`, last. Card content has no alpha of its own: one `globalAlpha = a` over the whole card, so text and surface fade together and stay readable: the surface is opaque white at `a`, and under it the stars are at `1 - a`, so at `a = 0.5` the text is at 50 % over a half-faded star field (the transition, brief and architect accept it). Nothing is drawn for `a < 0.02` and the card is the only layer for `a > 0.98`.
- Name duplication: at mid-fade the label above the disc (`1 - a`) and the card header (`a`) show the same name at different places; their alphas add up to 1, so only one reads at a time, and both are the domain's name in the same ink. Accepted; the label is not moved.
- Pointer: cards act from `a >= 0.5` (§ 4). Cursor: `grab`-style default of the canvas, `pointer` over a row, `move` over a header (drag), as the names today.
- No animation of its own: the cross-fade follows the wheel, the pinch and `zoomTo`; with `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing changes (there is no time-based animation to reduce; `zoomTo` already follows the page rule, M5.10).

### Trajectory on cards

- A card holding members: a 2 px `C.text` frame replaces the 1.5 px border (inset, same outer size), and a line right under the count line: `trajectoryCounts(direction, up, down)` over members of that constellation, start excluded ("5 upstream · 2 downstream", 11 px 600 `C.text`, 12 px; one direction only: "5 upstream"). Text, not colour, carries the meaning (1.4.1); the colour marks live on the arrows.
- The start's card: two 1.5 px `C.text` frames 3 px apart (the outer one 3 px outside the card, as the start's double ring); its counts line is like any member's. No extra string.
- Aggregated arrows (§ 5): colours `C.up` (7.10:1 on white, dashed) and `C.down` (5.18:1, solid), width `(1 + log2 c) x 1.2` px, count `c` written at the arrow's middle in 11 px 600 `C.text` on a white 2 px halo only when `c >= 2` (a single link reads without a number); arrowhead in the gap between rims (`routeLine`). Drawn under the cards.
- Row clicks and double-click work as without the mode.

### Row click: target and feedback

- Target: the whole row (inner width x row height, 14 px fine pointer, 18 px under `pointer: coarse`), not only the label. Rows are 14 px high, below the 24 px of WCAG 2.5.8 and the 44 px of D-035: a deliberate exception because the row is a shortcut for a gesture that exists otherwise (wheel, pinch, double-click or double-tap on the card, search), is not a control, and the card has no room for more (a 44 px row would drop 2 rows and all names on the 121 px card). Under a coarse pointer the 18 px rows show only where the card is large; pinch stays the primary touch path (recorded for the M8 close, D-035 untouched).
- Feedback: before the click the hover tint, the pointer cursor and the tooltip line; on the click `frame(...)` animates the camera to the band (the existing `zoomTo` animation); the card cross-fades to stars as the weight falls. No selection state remains on the row. A row whose band does not exist (the catch-all layer) frames the constellation: the tooltip line then reads "Click: zoom on Other" too, since the user wants "show me those", and the result still contains them.
- A click on a card outside its rows does nothing and does not leave the trajectory (so a stray click on a card never loses the mode); a click on the page background leaves it as today.

### Text summary for screen readers (`#u-summary`)

```html
<section class="sr-only" id="u-summary" aria-labelledby="u-summary-h">
  <h2 id="u-summary-h">Summary of the constellations</h2>
  <h3>Vehicle</h3>
  <p>299 tables · 152 entities</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Raw vault: 266 tables — Hub 60, Link 70, Satellite 120, PIT 6, View 10</li>
    <li>Business vault: 23 tables — Link 3, Satellite 20</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Main tables: vehicle_hub, car_hub, vehicle_x_car_link</p>
  ...
</section>
```
- Order: flow order of the drawing (`rank`, then name). One `<h3>` per constellation, then the count `<p>`, a `<ul>` of rows, a `<p>` of main tables (absent when none; never cut by room, up to 5). Row text: `label` + `L.colon` + `plural(n, L.tables)` + ` — ` + `kind label count` pairs joined by ", " in Types order (kind with zero omitted); French: "Raw vault : 266 tables — Hub 60, ...". The main-table paragraph: `mainH` + `L.colon` + names joined by ", ".
- Heading levels continue the page's outline (`h2` in the tab, `h3` per constellation). Rebuilt by `layout()` with `textContent`, no live region. It is inert with the stage while the flow is open and never receives focus.
- It is the canvas's text alternative at every scale, not only the cards': the section is present with 1 constellation, with stars, and after a filter change.
