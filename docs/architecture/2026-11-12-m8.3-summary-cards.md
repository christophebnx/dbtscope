# M8.3 — Summary cards at overview (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE (the user asked for the whole of M8 to be built without stopping; every point the brief leaves open is settled below and recorded in D-063 and D-064). The user approves the screenshots at the close of M8 (brief § 10); that does not block this phase.

## Context

Request: at overview scale, draw each constellation of the universe as a **summary card** (its name, table and entity counts, one row per layer with a bar split by kind, its main core tables) instead of stars, with a continuous transition to the stars while zooming, so that a 1,374-table project opens on its structure and a 95-table project still opens on its stars. Roadmap M8.3, brief `docs/briefs/2026-11-12-m8-synthesis.md` § 7 (constraints § 4; M8.4, § 8, builds on the cards: source systems become one group card, routes go from card edge to card edge). It answers finding 1 (at Fit a domain is a grid of dots of one size) and the § 2 questions of `docs/brief-universe-view.md` about sizes and composition.

Current state of the code (after M8.2):

- `render/assets/universe.js` (about 1,280 lines, one closure `startUniverse`): `layout()` computes `visible` (filters; every count), `FOLDED` / `foldedSet` / `drawn` (D-062), the reserved boxes `B` (D-040: names level, star and label; keys / all levels, the **table card**), `MAIN` (rank of each core table in its constellation, from `mainTables`, D-058), `constellations` (`k`, `x`, `y`, `r`, `n` = visible tables, `bands` with world spans and `n`, `mix` = kind counts, folded included), `routes` (one per pair of constellations, `c`, `dir`), then d3-force places the discs (`forceCollide(r + 30 + 0.15 r)`, seed 42, pins win).
- `paint()` draws, at every zoom: disc halos (domain mode) or domain bands (layer mode); routes below scale 0.6, centre to centre with an arrowhead at 55 %; table links; stars (`drawStar`: the drawn radius is floored at 2.5 screen px) or table cards (`drawCard`, keys / all, `k > 0.35`); badges, orbits; then in screen space each constellation's name, count line and 80 px composition bar above its disc (`labelHits`, the drag handle of a constellation), band names, table names (`orderLabels`, `drawNames`; below `OVERVIEW_K` = 0.6 only the first `OVERVIEW_NAMES` core names when the page has a core kind).
- Interactions: `pick` / `pickAt` (tables, only at `k >= FAR` = 0.5), `domainAt` (the disc under the pointer: tooltip, double-click zoom), `labelAt` (a constellation's name: drag, mouse only, D-045), `zoomTo`, `frame`, `fit`; the `ResizeObserver` refits on every size change.
- Hooks: `geometry()` (`by`, `layoutMs`, `tables`, `fold`, `folded`, `constellations`), `drawnNames()`, `camera()`, `place()`, `placeMs()`, `trajectoryState()`.
- There is no measured Fit scale in the docs: `tests/browser/test_roles.py` only asserts that both acme_retail and the large project open below 0.6.

## Chosen design

**No Python change. The payload is unchanged** (`tests/fixtures/m3-reference-pages/payloads/*.json` stay byte-identical); only the page assets change, so the reference pages (`*.html`, `expected.json` hashes and sizes) are regenerated once, and the page sizes quoted by the user guide are updated in the same commit. Everything a card shows is derived in the browser from what Python already decided: `nodes` (`domain`, `layer`, `kind`, `parent`), `kinds` (`role`, `color`, `label`), `layers` (flow order), `domains` (order, colour), `relations` (ranking of the main tables, as `mainTables` already does). The browser decides only *when* (it depends on the viewport and the zoom) and *how* it is drawn.

### 1. When: one page-wide rule on the room of a star (D-063)

- **Star cell.** `CELL` = the median, over the drawn tables, of `√(w · h)` of their reserved box `B` (D-040), in world px. Computed at the end of `layout()`, so it follows the filters, the grouping, the fold and the detail level, and never changes while zooming.
- **The rule.** A star "can be told apart" when its cell drawn on screen, `k · CELL`, is larger than `SUMMARY_CELL` = **14 screen px**. Two scales follow, constant between two layouts:
  - `summaryScale = SUMMARY_CELL / CELL`: at `k <= summaryScale` every constellation is a summary card, fully;
  - `starsScale = summaryScale · SUMMARY_FADE` (`SUMMARY_FADE` = **1.3**): at `k >= starsScale` every constellation is stars, as before M8.3.
- **Continuous transition, both ways: a cross-fade.** `summaryAlpha(k) = clamp(log(starsScale / k) / log(SUMMARY_FADE), 0, 1)`: 1 at and below `summaryScale`, 0 at and above `starsScale`, linear in `log k` between. The card layer is drawn at alpha `a`, everything the stars layer draws (table links, stars or table cards, badges, orbits, trajectory arrows, table names, the constellation name and bar above the disc) at `1 − a`. It is a pure function of `k`: zooming in and out pass through the same images at the same scales, no hysteresis, no timer, no animation (nothing for `prefers-reduced-motion`). At `a = 1` the stars layer is skipped entirely (cheaper frames at Fit); at `a = 0` the card layer is skipped and the frame is the M8.2 frame, pixel for pixel.
- **Global, not per constellation.** One threshold for the page: at a given zoom every constellation is in the same state. The cell is a per-table measure, so a small constellation next to a huge one has stars of the same room at the same zoom: when the huge one cannot be read, neither can the small one. A per-constellation rule would mix cards and stars at one zoom for a few percent of difference in median box, and make the map read as inconsistent.
- **Why the room and not the radius.** `drawStar` floors the drawn radius at 2.5 screen px, so the drawn disc of a star never shrinks at overview: what makes stars indistinguishable is the room between them, which the reserved box measures. The box-based cell also makes the rule independent of the detail level (at keys / all the boxes are table cards: the universe is larger, the Fit scale smaller, the cell on screen about the same). At Fit, `k · CELL` is about `√(drawing area / drawn tables)`: in effect a density rule, so it separates projects by size, robustly.
- **What the user sees.** acme_retail (95 tables, 82 drawn folded) at 1280 × 800: stars at Fit, as today; cards only when zooming out well below Fit. The large project (1,374 tables, 933 drawn folded) at 1280 × 800: 12 cards at Fit; stars after a few wheel notches or one double-click. The expected cell at Fit differs by about `√(933 / 82)` ≈ 3.4× between the two projects; the rule needs only 1.3 × 1.1² ≈ 1.6×.
- **Calibration (developer, first step, measured and recorded in `docs/progress.md`).** At 1280 × 800, scale 1, panel open: `c_acme = k_fit · CELL` on acme_retail and `c_large` on the large project (folded, the default). The constants hold when `c_large <= SUMMARY_CELL / 1.1` and `c_acme >= SUMMARY_CELL · SUMMARY_FADE · 1.1`. If not, set `SUMMARY_CELL = √(c_acme · c_large / SUMMARY_FADE)` (centred in log space) rounded to 0.5 px, and if the ratio `c_acme / c_large` is below 1.3 × 1.21, set `SUMMARY_FADE = max(1.15, c_acme / (1.21 · c_large))`; record the measured values and the constants. The two Done-when tests (§ Test plan) are the acceptance.
- **Measured (ui-designer, 2026-11-13, headless Chromium, panel open at 1280, closed at 360; scripts and screenshots in the session scratchpad `m8.3/`).** `CELL` = 59.3 world px on the large project (933 drawn tables, folded, names level) and 53.2 on acme_retail (82 drawn). Fit scale `k_fit` and `c = k_fit · CELL`:

  | Page | `k_fit` | `c` (screen px) | State at Fit |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | large, 1280 × 800 | 0.159 | 9.45 | cards, `a` = 1 (`summaryScale` 0.236, `starsScale` 0.307: 3 notches to start fading, 5 to reach stars) |
  | large, 360 × 800 | 0.095 | 5.66 | cards |
  | acme_retail, 1280 × 800 | 0.449 | 23.9 | stars, `a` = 0 (`summaryScale` 0.263, `starsScale` 0.342) |
  | acme_retail, 360 × 800 | 0.255 | 13.57 | cards, `a` = 1, 3 % under `SUMMARY_CELL` (a knife edge: any slightly larger window gives a partial fade, no jump) |

  The constants hold: `c_large` 9.45 <= 14 / 1.1 = 12.7 (margin 1.48) and `c_acme` 23.9 >= 14 · 1.3 · 1.1 = 20.0 (margin 1.31). **`SUMMARY_CELL` = 14 and `SUMMARY_FADE` = 1.3 are confirmed; the fallback formulas are not used.** Resetting them to open acme at 360 px on stars would need `SUMMARY_CELL <= 10.4`, which leaves the large project at 1280 only 10 % from the fade: not worth it, and 6 cards with text are more useful than 82 dots of 2.5 px on a phone.
- **360 px.** The Fit scale is about 0.6 of the 1280 px one on the large project (0.095 against 0.159: the stage is 360 px wide, panel closed), 0.57 on acme: the large project opens on cards, acme_retail on cards too (table above). The cards degrade by content (§ 3, UI design): see the outcome there.
- **Window resize.** Nothing is stored: the existing `ResizeObserver` refits, the new Fit scale gives the new state. `summaryScale` depends on the layout only, never on the viewport.
- **No new tab, no new control.** The state follows the zoom; the text summary (§ 6) is an optional, closed-by-default panel section.

### 2. Content of a card (D-064)

Computed by **`summarise()`**, called at the end of `layout()` (after `MAIN`), into `SUMMARIES: Map<constellation key, summary>`; O(visible tables), no layout; its time is `perf.cards`.

- **Name**, `constellationLabel(k)`: in domain mode in its domain colour made readable on the card's white (`CONTRAST.ink(color, C.card)`, D-037), in layer mode in `C.text` (neutral, as D-048).
- **Counts line**: `plural(n, L.tables)`, `n` = visible tables of the constellation (folded included: never reduced, brief § 4), then, **while the fold is on** (`state.fold`, the meta line's own rule, D-062) **and the constellation holds at least one entity**, ` · ` + `plural(entities, T.entitiesN)`, entities = its visible `core` and `connector` tables (ui-designer: a source system has none, and "0 entities" on seven cards is noise; the clause is left out at 0). A page that does not fold shows tables only, as its meta line.
- **Rows**, one per **row key present** among the constellation's visible tables:
  - domain mode: one row per layer, in flow order (`DATA.layers` order, the catch-all `other` last as in that list);
  - layer mode: one row per domain, in the Domains panel order (`DATA.domains`), with a dot in the domain colour (D-042 outline).
  - **Each table counts in its own row** (its own `layer`, or its own `domain`), folded tables included. The band `n` of `layoutConstellation` is not reused: in domain mode it counts a folded table in its parent's band (the M8.2 reviewer's point), which a card must not do.
  - Each row: label (`labelOf`), count (`num`), and a **bar split by kind**: segments in the **Types-list order** (role order, then payload order: the order of the panel, so colours read in the legend's order and line up from row to row), each in `CONTRAST.edge(kind colour, C.bg)` (a bar of 6 px or less is drawn in the edge colour, D-042), separated by 1 px of the card's white. Bar length = the row's count over the **largest row of the card**, times the bar's room (the rows of a card compare at a glance; the counts compare cards).
  - The Types-list order is today computed inline in `refreshPanel`; it becomes one helper, **`kindOrder`** (a sorted copy of `DATA.kinds`), used by `refreshPanel` and the cards (invariant 2).
- **Main tables**: up to `SUMMARY_NAMES` = 5 table names, each with its kind dot, as many as the card's room allows (§ 3). They are **the first names of the overview order of the constellation**: the order `orderLabels` already applies to ordinary names below `OVERVIEW_K` (D-058), extracted into one helper **`overviewOrder(members)`** used by both (invariant 2):
  - on a page with a core kind: the constellation's core tables by `MAIN` rank (`mainTables`: most relations, then links, name, id; folded tables are never core, so `MAIN` over drawn tables is `MAIN` over visible ones);
  - on a page without a core kind (no preset, no `role`): its tables by role order (`NAME_ORDER`) then `labelScore`, as names at overview are offered there today;
  - a constellation **without a core table on a page that has core kinds** (a source system: sources and staging only) lists **no** table: listing technical tables by score is the arbitrary name finding 1 complained about. Its rows and counts say what it holds; M8.4 groups such constellations anyway.
- **Trajectory** (only while the mode is on): a line `start · 5 upstream · 2 downstream` (`L.lgStart` when the card holds the start, then `trajectoryCounts(direction, up, down)` of `common.js`, counted over the trajectory's tables in this constellation), and a frame by side (§ 4). A card holding none of the trajectory is drawn at `DIM_CTX`, as its disc is today.
- **Filters** apply to everything above (counts, rows, bars, entities, names): `summarise` reads `visible`, `vis`, `MAIN`. A constellation with no visible table has no card (it has no disc either).
- **Not on cards**: badges, orbits, search ring, hover (they belong to the stars layer). A search or a trajectory start at card scale is reached by the camera (§ 4).

### 3. Geometry (D-063; revised by the ui-designer after measuring, see D-065 to record)

**Measured, and why the inscribed rectangle is dropped.** At 1280 × 800 Fit on the large project the 12 discs measure 110 (vehicle), 96 (auction), 82 (account), 61 (activity), 58 (claim) and 60, 57, 52, 51, 50, 45, 45 screen px (the seven source systems) in radius; at 360 × 800, 66 to 27. The inscribed rectangle (1.6 r × 1.2 r) is 92 × 69 px for claim and 72 × 54 px for the smallest source system, against a business card that needs 139 × 136 px (name 16, counts 14, two rows of 22, three main tables of 14, paddings; the counts line "290 tables · 149 entities" alone is 123 px at 11 px) and a source card 112 × 90 px. At most vehicle and auction would fit: the requirement (name, counts, layer rows and three main tables on every business card; name, count and two rows on every source card) fails on at least 10 of 12. Cards sized from their content and centred on their discs overlap on 6 or 7 pairs (raw_crm and raw_web are 92 px apart horizontally and 66 px vertically); shrinking overlapping pairs to fit leaves 5 to 7 cards below the requirement. The discs cannot move (no force change) and Fit is height-limited, so the room has to come from **moving the cards, not the discs**: a card may leave its disc.

- **Card size from content.** A card has a ladder of **levels**, from the most to the least content: `rows` (header, one row per layer or domain, then 3 to 5 main tables; sub-level `rows` without tables), `bar`, `name`, `count`, `frame` (sizes in the UI design section). Its screen size is that of its content at its level, constant in screen px (text is at a constant screen size), width clamped to 112 to 176 px. It does not depend on the disc, except the number of main tables (3 at least, up to 5 as the disc's screen height allows).
- **Placement: anchor, then push apart, in screen space, per frame.** The anchor of a card is its disc's centre on screen. `cardLayout(k)` (new, the one place that places cards; pure function of the layout, the camera and the filters) puts every card on its anchor, then **separates overlapping cards** with a fixed relaxation: 40 iterations; at each, every overlapping pair (rectangles with a 2 px gap) is pushed apart along the line between their centres by exactly the distance that clears the nearer axis (the push is continuous in `k`: it is 0 when the cards just touch), the lighter card (smaller area) moving more; displacements are summed per card and applied at 0.6. Deterministic: same inputs, same rectangles; no random seed, no timer, no state kept between frames.
- **A card stays on its constellation.** The distance between a card's centre and its anchor is at most the disc's screen radius `r · k` (the centre stays inside the disc). When the relaxation leaves a card beyond that or still overlapping, **the worst offender (largest shift over `r`, overlaps weighing 10) drops one level** and the relaxation restarts, at most 30 rounds (then every remaining offender goes to `frame`). Measured on the large project: 1 round at 1280 × 800 (all 12 cards at level `rows`; the five business cards with 4, 3, 5, 3, 5 main tables; largest shift 14 px, raw_crm), 25 rounds and 5 ms at 360 × 800; on acme_retail at 360 × 800, 7 rounds and 5 ms. Cost: O(rounds · 40 · n²/2) with n the number of constellations (12 here): 5 ms measured at worst; budget in § 7.
- **What does not change.** d3-force, the pins, the saved arrangements, their format (D-013, D-048) and **Fit** (which frames the discs): the camera at opening is the camera of M8.2. A card is not a disc: dragging a constellation by its card's name moves the disc, and the card follows its anchor.
- **`summaryRect(d)` and `edgeOf(d, toward, a)` (the M8.4 seam) keep their names**: `summaryRect(d)` returns the world rectangle of the card as placed by the last `cardLayout` (screen rectangle divided by `k`, translated), the one place that says where a constellation's outline is; `edgeOf` as in § 4.
- **Zooming in lands on the stars where the card was**: the card's anchor is the disc's centre, and the push shrinks to 0 as the discs spread with the zoom (centre distances about double between Fit and `starsScale` on the large project, where the 112 px cards of the closest pair, raw_crm and raw_web, no longer touch). Between, the card is at most one disc radius from where its stars appear, inside the disc.
- **Text at constant screen size**, laid out in screen space at each frame (`ctx.measureText`, synchronous, so the export draws the same): name 13 px `600`; every other line 11 px (`FONT`). A label or name wider than its room is cut with `…` (the full text is in the tooltip and in the text summary); counts are never cut (the counts line has two forms, § UI design). Ink: `C.text`, `C.muted`, domain names through `CONTRAST.ink` (D-037).
- **Content by room, top to bottom**, so what is dropped is always the least important: name; counts line; trajectory line (when on); rows (all or none); main tables. A card drops a level when its room is short (`rows` without tables, then `bar`, then `name`, `count`, `frame`: the level drawn is reported by the hook).

### 4. Interactions (D-064)

Card interactions are on while `a >= 0.5` ("cards are on"), star interactions while `a < 0.5`; the switch is at `√(summaryScale · starsScale)`, between the two.

- **Hit-testing.** The last screen frame records, in screen px, each card's rect, its name line (header) and its rows (`summaryHits`, as `labelHits`). While cards are on, `pick` returns no table (the stars are not drawn), and `labelHits` holds the card headers instead of the names above the discs, so the **drag code is unchanged**: dragging a card **by its name** moves the constellation and pins it as today (mouse only, D-045; on touch a drag pans and a pinch zooms).
- **Hover**: the existing tooltip (`domainAt`: the card rectangle first, then the disc, since a card may overlap a neighbour's disc, § 3): domain tooltip, or layer tooltip whose band line is the **row under the pointer** (not the band under the pointer's x, which means nothing on a card). The action line gains `T.rowZoom` while cards are on: "Double-click: zoom on this domain · click a row: zoom on it".
- **Double-click** on a card (anywhere in its rectangle or its disc): `zoomOnConstellation(d)`, the existing double-click target `0.92 · min(w, h) / (2r + 60)`, raised to at least `starsScale · 1.05` so it always lands on stars (a very large constellation is then partly off screen). The existing domain double-click uses the same function.
- **Click on a row**: `zoomOnRow(d, key)`: frames the band of that key (`bandSpans(d)`: world x from `s0` to `s1`, the disc's height there), at least at `starsScale · 1.05`. A row whose band is not drawn (domain mode, every table of that layer folded into an entity of another layer's band) zooms on the constellation. A click elsewhere on a card does nothing (it neither leaves the trajectory nor clears the search, as a click on a disc today).
- **Cursor**: `grab` over a card's name (as over a constellation name today), `pointer` over a row, default elsewhere.
- **Search** goes to the table as today, at `max(t.k, 1.5, starsScale · 1.05)`: past the card scale. A folded table is shown on its orbit as M8.2 does.
- **Trajectory seen from afar.** A card holding trajectory tables: a double dark frame when it holds the start (as the start star's double ring), else a dashed violet 2.5 px frame (upstream only), a solid orange one (downstream only), or both (violet dashed outside, orange inside 3 px in) — the trajectory colours and dashes of D-018; plus its trajectory line (§ 2). The other cards are at `DIM_CTX`. **Fit** in the mode frames the trajectory's tables as today (`kMax` 2): when that scale is below `summaryScale`, the trajectory is read on the cards. The trajectory bar, its counts and the flow view are unchanged.
- **Routes** (drawn below scale 0.6, as today, behind the cards): each end moves from the constellation's centre toward the edge of its card by the card alpha: `edgeOf(d, toward, a)` = the disc centre plus `a` times the vector to the point where the ray from the card's centre (which may be pushed off the disc's centre, § 3) toward the other constellation's centre leaves `summaryRect(d)`. At `a = 0` the route is today's (acme at Fit unchanged); at `a = 1` it runs from card edge to card edge, so its arrowhead (55 % of the segment) is never under a card. **The M8.4 seam**: `summaryRect(d)` and `edgeOf` are the one place that says where a constellation's outline is; M8.4's curved routes and the source-system group card build on them.
- **Image export** (`image()` → `draw(target, scale)`): the cards are painted by the same helpers, in the same synchronous call, at the same `k`: the export shows the cards, the cross-fade, the trajectory frames as on screen (hover left out, as today).

### 5. Code layout

- **Everything stays in `universe.js`**, in a new section `// ---- summary cards` (estimate +260 lines, about 1,550 in all). The cards read and write the closure's state at every step (`k`, `constellations`, `visible`, `vis`, `MAIN`, `FOLDED`, `traj`, `ctx`, `T`, `labelHits`, the zoom); a separate asset would have to be a global (`render/CLAUDE.md`: only `common.js` declares globals; "a view never declares a global") taking a dozen parameters, and `page.py`'s asset list and the reference pages' script blocks would change. No helper is page-wide, so nothing moves to `common.js`. Splitting `universe.js` needs a rule for view modules first: noted under "Later" in the roadmap.
- New in `universe.js` (one responsibility each):
  - constants `SUMMARY_CELL` (14), `SUMMARY_FADE` (1.3), `SUMMARY_NAMES` (5);
  - `kindOrder` (the Types-list order, extracted from `refreshPanel`), `overviewOrder(members)` (extracted from `orderLabels`);
  - `summarise()` → `SUMMARIES`, `CELL`, `summaryScale`, `starsScale` (in `layout()`);
  - `summaryAlpha(k)`; `cardLayout(k)` (levels, sizes, anchors, the push, § 3: one call per frame while `a > 0`, its result cached by `k`, the layout revision and the trajectory); `summaryRect(d)`; `edgeOf(d, toward, a)`; `motionMs(ms)` (0 under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, else `ms`: used by `zoomTo`, `frame` and the new zooms);
  - `drawSummary(d, a, k)` (one card; returns its hits and the level drawn) and the card pass in `paint()`;
  - `summaryAt(x, y)` (the card part under a screen point: header, row, card);
  - `zoomOnConstellation(d)`, `zoomOnRow(d, key)` (used by the canvas, the double-click and the text summary);
  - `renderSummary()` (the text summary, § 6).
- Changed: `layout()` (calls `summarise`), `paint()` (two layers and their alphas; routes through `edgeOf`; the stars layer skipped at `a = 1`, the card layer at `a = 0`), `orderLabels` (through `overviewOrder`), `refreshPanel` (through `kindOrder`), `pick` (no table while cards are on), the mousemove tooltip and cursor, the double-click and click handlers, the search zoom, `enterTrajectory` / `leaveTrajectory` (refresh the text summary when open), `hintText` (one clause), the hooks.

### 6. Keyboard and screen readers: the summary as text (D-064)

- **Where**: a real panel section, not a visually hidden list (a focusable control that cannot be seen fails focus visibility, WCAG 2.4.7). At the end of the universe panel, after the Domains list and before the hint: a native **`<details id="u-sum">`**, closed by default, `<summary id="u-sum-h">` reading "Summary: 12 domains" / « Synthèse : 12 domaines » (layer mode: "Summary: 4 layers" / « Synthèse : 4 couches »; `plural`), then `<div id="u-sum-list">`. Always present (any page can show cards by zooming out, and the canvas is silent at every scale); it is an optional control, never needed to use the page.
- **Content**, per constellation in the order of the canvas's left-to-right placement (by `x`), built with DOM calls and `textContent` (names are untrusted):
  - a heading line: the constellation's name (`h4`, with its domain dot in domain mode, `paintDot`), and a **Zoom** button ("Zoom" / « Cadrer », `aria-label` "Zoom on Vehicle" / « Cadrer Vehicle »: the label contains the visible word, WCAG 2.5.3) → `zoomOnConstellation`;
  - the counts line (the card's), and the trajectory line while the mode is on;
  - one button per row (`ul`/`li`), its text the row in words: "Raw vault: 180 tables (Hub 70 · Link 66 · Satellite 44)" / « Raw vault : 180 tables (Hub 70 · …) » (label and `L.colon`, `plural`, kinds in `kindOrder` with `num`; kind labels as written in the config, no agreement needed), title "Zoom on this layer" / « Cadrer cette couche » (layer mode: "this domain" / « ce domaine ») → `zoomOnRow`;
  - "Main tables: a, b, c" / « Tables principales : a, b, c » when the card lists any.
- **In sync**: rebuilt by `renderSummary()` at the end of every `layout()` (filters, grouping, fold, detail level) and on trajectory enter / move / leave, only while the section is open; opening it (`toggle`) builds it. Cost: a few hundred DOM nodes, under 20 ms on the 1,500-table payload.
- **Keyboard**: Tab reaches the summary after the Domains list; Enter / Space opens it; Tab walks the Zoom and row buttons; Enter zooms the canvas (focus stays on the button; the camera animates as for a double-click). Native focus ring. On coarse pointers the buttons are 44 px targets (`.panel` rule). At 360 px the text wraps (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), no horizontal scroll.
- **Language**: every string in `T`, en and fr (§ Strings).

### 7. Performance budgets (large project and the 1,500-table payload)

- Page open ≤ 3 s and a filter change ≤ 1 s, as today (cards on at Fit on both).
- `perf.cards` (the `summarise` time inside a layout) ≤ 50 ms on the 1,500-table payload and on the large project; `perf.cardLayout` (one `cardLayout`, the push) ≤ 15 ms on the large project at 1280 and 360 px (measured 1.4 and 4.5 ms), part of the frame budget below.
- A screen frame at Fit with cards fully on (`perf.draw`) ≤ 50 ms; a frame inside the cross-fade (both layers) ≤ 100 ms; on the large page.
- Zooming across the threshold runs no layout (`layoutRuns` unchanged) and moves the camera only as the zoom asks (§ Test plan).
- `renderSummary` with the section open ≤ 50 ms on the 1,500-table payload.

### 8. Test hooks

- `geometry()` gains, top level, `summary: { cell, scale, stars, alpha }` (`CELL` in world px, `summaryScale`, `starsScale`, the current `summaryAlpha(t.k)`) and `routes: [{ source, target, c, dir }]`; each constellation gains `summary: { x, y, w, h, tables, entities, rows: [{ key, n, kinds: [[kind, n]] }], main: [ids] }` (world rect; `entities` null while the fold is off).

### Error handling

Nothing new can fail: no file, no config key, no format. A constellation too small for any text keeps its frame; `measureText` is the only text measure. Without localStorage nothing changes (nothing is stored).

## Rejected options

- **A threshold on the drawn star radius** (the brief's literal wording): the drawn radius is floored at 2.5 screen px, so it never falls below the threshold; the unfloored world radius ignores the room between stars and the detail level.
- **A threshold per constellation**: mixed maps at one zoom for small differences in median box; a small constellation next to a huge one has stars of the same room anyway.
- **A fixed scale threshold** (for example `OVERVIEW_K`): it does not depend on the project, so acme_retail would open on cards (it opens below 0.6) or the large project on stars.
- **A threshold on the Fit scale** (cards exactly at Fit): one wheel notch would remove them on any project, and a small project would show cards at Fit.
- **Hysteresis or an animated switch**: a pure function of the zoom is continuous, symmetric and exported as seen; an animation needs a timer, `prefers-reduced-motion` handling and differs in the export.
- **A morph of the stars into the card**: a star has no place in a card (rows are counts, not tables); a cross-fade in the same disc gives the same continuity at no cost.
- **Cards sized by their content at a constant screen size, anchored on the disc and left where they land** (rejected as such; the chosen rule adds the push of § 3): they overlap on 6 or 7 pairs at Fit on the large project. **The force made aware of card footprints** stays rejected: the footprints depend on the zoom (a layout per zoom). The push is in screen space, after the layout.
- **The inscribed rectangle** (the first proposal of this note): measured too small, § 3. **Shrinking overlapping cards instead of moving them**: 5 to 7 cards fall below the requirement on the large project at 1280 px. **Clamping cards into the stage**: a card near the edge would jump when its disc leaves the stage, or need a weight function; at 360 px a few cards at Fit are cut by the stage's edge (by 10 to 15 px in the mock-ups), and the text summary lists them; to revisit if users report it.
- **Making d3-force aware of card footprints**: the inscribed card never leaves its disc; changing the force would move every constellation of every page, acme included, and invalidate the computed placement under existing pins.
- **Text that scales with the card**: illegible at Fit on 12 cards.
- **Rows counted from the bands' `n`**: in domain mode a band counts a folded table in its parent's band.
- **Listing technical or descriptive tables as main tables** when a constellation has no core table on a page with core kinds: arbitrary names (finding 1).
- **Entities on every card whatever the fold**: the meta line shows entities only while the fold is on (D-062); one rule.
- **A visually hidden list for screen readers**: its focusable buttons would be invisible; a list without buttons gives keyboard users no way to zoom.
- **A payload key with per-constellation counts or main tables**: the counts depend on the filters and the grouping, chosen in the page; `mainTables` already ranks from the payload's relations (D-058). The payload stays byte-identical.
- **A new asset file `cards.js`**: needs a view global (forbidden by `render/CLAUDE.md`) or a dozen parameters; the asset list and the reference pages' structure change for no user benefit.
- **A control to switch cards off**: the brief asks for no new mandatory control; zoom is the switch.

### Transition (D-063)

`a = summaryAlpha(k)`; the card layer is drawn at `a`, the stars layer at `1 − a`: that layer holds table links, stars and table cards, badges, orbits, trajectory arrows, table names, the constellation name, count and composition bar above its disc, band markers and band names (layer mode), all of which fade out as the cards fade in; nothing else changes for them. Disc halos and domain bands, drawn first, stay at every zoom (the card sits on its constellation's footprint). Routes are drawn at full alpha below scale 0.6, their ends moved by `a` to the card edge (`edgeOf`), so the arrowhead never ends under a card. A card's fill is opaque white at the layer alpha: halfway the text is at 50 % over 50 % stars (a transient of about two wheel notches, 1.15² ≈ 1.3; legibility is judged at rest, `a` = 0 or 1). The transition is a pure function of `k`: no animation, no timer, nothing for `prefers-reduced-motion`. The card's position follows the push, which relaxes smoothly as discs spread with the zoom.

### States

- **Hover over a card** (mouse): its border goes to `CARD_BORDER_HOVER` (3 px); the existing tooltip (`domainAt`, card rectangle first) with the action line `T.frame · T.rowZoom` (layer mode `T.frameC · T.rowZoom`). Over a row: the row's background takes the constellation's mark colour at 12 % (`CONTRAST.edge + '1f'`), the cursor is `pointer`, and the tooltip gets one more line: domain mode "Layer: Raw vault · 277 tables" (`T.layerOne`), layer mode the existing "Domain: Sales · 42 tables" for the row. Over the header: cursor `grab` (`grabbing` while dragging). Elsewhere on the card: default cursor. Touch: no hover; a tap on a row zooms (a tap is a click); a double-tap on the card zooms; a one-finger drag pans, a pinch zooms (D-045).
- **Header = drag handle**: the card's top 22 px, full width, at levels `rows` and `bar`; the whole card at `name`, `count`, `frame` (they have no row). Drag moves the constellation and pins it as today (mouse only, scope `names`).
- **Focus**: the canvas draws no focus on cards (it is not a keyboard widget); the keyboard path is the panel's text summary. `Esc` unchanged.
- **Dimmed** (trajectory on, the constellation holds none of it): the whole card at `DIM_CTX` (0.45), as its disc.
- **Trajectory** (cards on, the constellation holds trajectory tables, drawn at every level): a frame outside the border, 4 px away: start, two dark rectangles 4 and 8 px away, 1.5 px, `C.text` (the star's double ring); upstream only, 2.5 px dashed `C.up` (dash 6 3); downstream only, 2.5 px solid `C.down`; both, upstream at 8 px and downstream at 4 px. Line under the counts (11 px, muted; at levels `rows`, `bar`, `name`): `start · 5 upstream · 2 downstream` (`L.lgStart` only on the card holding the start, then `trajectoryCounts('both' | 'up' | 'down', up, down)` restricted to the direction on, so "5 upstream" alone when the direction is upstream); fr « départ · 5 en amont · 2 en aval ». The counts of all cards add up to the trajectory bar's.
- **Filtered out**: a layer, domain or kind with no visible table has no row and no segment (never a row at 0); a constellation with no visible table has no card and no disc (no empty card); counts and bars follow the filters at once. With every constellation filtered out the canvas shows `L.empty` as today and `#u-sum-list` too.
- **Single entity-less card** (source system): "72 tables", rows, no main tables, no `0 entities`.
- **Loading / error**: none; the cards are computed synchronously with the layout.
