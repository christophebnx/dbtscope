# M8.4 — Source systems apart, readable routes (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE

Every change below is foreseen by `docs/briefs/2026-11-12-m8-synthesis.md` § 4 and § 8 (its "Without a preset" bullet as corrected on 2026-11-12) and by `docs/roadmap.md` M8.4 (it also takes the roadmap's "Later" item on curved routes and neighbours lit on hover). `dbt-erd.yml` gains one optional key on domain rules (brief § 4: "only gains optional keys"); the universe payload gains one key per domain; `dbt-erd-layout.json`, its format and version, the localStorage keys and every layout scope are unchanged, so no released format changes and no migration. The open points the brief leaves open (how the override reads, what "smaller" and "stacked" mean, how the flow order is computed, the density rule, the route geometry, where the filtered-routes count shows, how keyboard focus reaches a constellation, the "no crossing" measure) are decided below with their reason; none is a choice between options with no documented preference that only the user can make. ## Context

Request: set source systems (domains made only of technical tables, flag computed in Python, overridable per domain rule) apart on the upstream side of the universe as one group at overview scale, order business domains along the data flow, and make routes readable (edge to edge, curved around other constellations, arrowed, strongest only by default, all of one constellation on hover or focus). Roadmap M8.4; brief § 8 is the spec (findings 3 and 4).

Current state:

- `domain/universe._domains` builds `UniverseDomain {key, label, color, owner}` in payload order (size descending, `""` last); label and colour come from `configured = {r.key: r for r in config.domains}` (the last rule of a key). `domains` rules are plain `config.Rule`s (`PresetRule` in presets); kind roles are known per node through `_kinds` (`UniverseKind.role`, D-059; source, seed and snapshot are `technical`).
- `universe.js layout()`: `agg` counts links between passing tables of two constellations (relations and / or lineage per **Links**), `flow` the net lineage direction; `routes = {source, target, c, dir}` with `dir` set when `|flow| > 0.2·c`. d3-force (seed 42) places every constellation: collide, link (by `c`), `forceX(rank·W)` with `rank` the mean layer rank of a domain's tables (`flowX` 0.06), `forceY(0)`, charge; 400 + 100 ticks; saved pins (`fx`, `fy`) win.
- `paint()` draws routes when `k < 0.6` or cards are on: straight centre-to-centre lines (`routeLine`), width `(1 + log2 c)·1.6/k`, one arrowhead in the gap between rims when `dir ≠ 0`; every route is drawn. Hover on a constellation shows its tooltip only. `drawTrajectoryArrows` (cards) reuses `routeLine`.
- Domains panel: one heading with **all · none**, rows in payload order (`refreshPanel`, `filterRow`); `DOM_POS` (payload order) orders the domain bands of layer mode and card rows.
- Summary cards (M8.3): one per constellation, `cardRect`, `planCard`, `drawSummary`, `summaryHits`, `#u-summary` text alternative.
- Large project (`tests/large_project.py`, `LARGE_CONFIG`): 1,419 tables, 5 business domains (by `domains` rules on schema) and 7 `raw_<system>` domains (by `group_by: schema`) of 36 sources + 36 `stg_*` models each (504 tables, all `technical` with `data_vault`). M8.3 measured Fit 0.181 at 1280 × 800, page open 0.45 s, filter change 52 to 70 ms.

## Chosen design

### 1. The source-system flag (Python, `domain/`)

**Rule** (`universe.is_source_system(roles, override) -> bool`, pure, public):

- `override` is not `None`: it is the answer.
- Else true when the domain holds at least one table and the role of every one of its tables' kinds is `technical`.

"Its tables" are the nodes of the universe payload (after `universe.select` / `exclude`), whatever the page's filters: Python decides, the page never recomputes it (brief § 4). The `""` domain ("No domain") follows the same rule; no rule can override it (no rule has an empty key).

**Override.** A new model `config.DomainRule(Rule)` with `system: StrictBool | None = None` (`None` = not set); `Config.domains` becomes a `DomainRuleList`. In presets `PresetDomainRule(DomainRule)` with `PRESET_SETTINGS`, so a preset may set it. `system` belongs to domain rules only: on a kind or layer rule it is ignored in `dbt-erd.yml` (lenient, invariant 9) and refused as an unknown key in a preset (D-030), as `role` on a domain rule (D-059). A non-boolean (`"yes"`, `1`, `maybe`) is the usual one-line error `config <path>: domains[N].system: …; fix the file`, exit 1 (through `errors.validation_message`, as `universe.fold`).

- Which rule: the **first** rule of `config.domains` whose `key` is the domain's key and which sets `system` (true or false). Reason: several rules often share one key (one per schema of a domain); the user writes the override once, on any of them, and the first one wins as in every other rule list (D-015). Label and colour keep today's lookup (unchanged bytes).
- It applies to any domain key, also one that comes from a dbt group, `meta.domain` or `group_by` (`raw_crm`): the user adds `- {key: raw_crm, system: false, match: []}` (a rule that selects nothing only names the domain, as for labels today; the user guide shows it).
- `universe_data` passes `_domains` the role of each node's kind (the `role` it already computes per node) and calls `is_source_system` per domain.

**Payload.** `UniverseDomain.system: bool = False`, **last key** of a domain (D-048: no omission at the default). The order of `domains` is unchanged (Python does not sort for the panel, D-059's reason: the page orders).

**Without a preset** (brief § 8, corrected): only the built-in technical kinds make a source system. A domain made only of sources, seeds and snapshots gets `true`; a domain holding any model of a kind with no role does not. A user kind rule without a role that captures sources (say `kinds: [{key: src, match: ["type:source"]}]`) makes them role none, so their domain is not a source system; the user guide says so (set `role: technical`, or `system: true`).

No CLI change: the summary does not list domains today, and the panel heading makes the result visible (rejected options).

### 2. The page: source systems set apart (`render/assets/universe.js`)

`SYS = new Set(DATA.domains.filter(d => d.system).map(d => d.key))`. Everything below applies only when `SYS` is not empty and at least one domain is not a source system (`HAS_SYS`); otherwise the page draws as M8.3 (a project whose domains all mix models and sources sees no change; a project made only of sources is not "set apart" from anything).

**Panel order.** `DOM_POS` (the order of the Domains panel, read by layer-mode bands, card rows and `#u-summary`) becomes: business domains in payload order, then source systems in payload order. On pages without source systems it equals today's order.

**Domain mode only.** In layer mode a source system is a domain band like any other (its colour, its tint, its rim arc); only its place in `DOM_POS` changes. Reason: layer constellations are layers; the source systems are already gathered in the Sources and Staging layers there.

#### 2.1 Placement

Order of `layout()` after the constellations are built:

1. **Business domains and pinned source systems** go through d3-force as today (seed 42, same forces), with two changes: (a) unpinned source systems are left out of the simulation (they are placed in step 2); pinned ones stay in it as fixed bodies (`fx`, `fy`) so business domains do not collide with them; (b) the x target of a business domain comes from the flow (§ 2.2).
2. **Unpinned source systems** are placed in a **grid on the left** ("stacked"), deterministic, no simulation:
   - Order: by the barycentre y of the business domains they have routes to, weighted by route weight `c` (a system with no business route goes last), then by `DOM_POS`. Reason: each system sits level with the domains it feeds, so its routes run roughly horizontally and cross nothing.
   - Rows and columns: one column while the stack's height (Σ of `2·(1.15 r + 30)`, the collision diameter) is at most the height of the business domains' bounding box (at least the largest system's diameter); else the fewest columns that make it fit, filled column by column. Reason: a single column of 7 large-project systems is about 1.3 times the business cluster's height, so Fit would shrink every card; a grid keeps Fit set by the business domains.
   - Position: the grid's right edge sits `GRID_GAP` (60 world px, the route distance of today's link force) left of the leftmost business disc's collision edge; rows are vertically centred on the business box's centre; cell size = the largest system's collision diameter.
   - Without business domains passing the filters, the grid is centred on 0.
3. Pins win (D-013, D-040): a pinned constellation, system or business, stays where it was saved. Since a drag pins every constellation of the scope, an arrangement saved before M8.4 holds pins for all of them and loads exactly as it was. **Reset layout** clears the pins and gives the new placement. No scope, key or format change.

**"Smaller."** A source system's disc keeps the size its packing gives (D-040: its stars are already small-tier, D-058); the page does not shrink it geometrically, so its names stay readable when zoomed in and its boxes never overlap. It reads smaller by its treatment (§ 2.3) and, at overview, by the one group card that replaces the seven (§ 2.4). Acceptance: the group card's screen area at Fit is less than half the sum of the seven individual cards' areas measured in M8.3.

#### 2.2 Business domains along the data flow

- **Directed routes.** A route between two constellations is directed when it carries lineage and `|net flow| > 0.2 · lineage count` (§ 3.1); its upstream end is the one whose tables are read.
- **Flow columns.** `flowColumns(keys, directed)` reuses `common.js flowRanks` (one implementation of "columns along the data flow", invariant 2) with the business domain keys as members, the directed business-to-business routes as `[upstream, downstream]` edges and `layerOf = () => null`. Members are passed sorted by (today's mean layer rank, key), so the cycle breaking of `flowRanks` is deterministic and falls back on layers.
- **x target.** `rank = column` for a business domain with at least one directed business route; the others take the mean column of the ranked ones (so the forces place them, as today). `forceX(rank · W)` with `flowX` for domains raised from 0.06 to `FLOW_X_BUSINESS` = 0.15 (the developer may tune it in [0.06, 0.3] to meet the acceptance, and records the value). Without any directed business route, every business domain has the same target and the placement is today's, apart from source systems.
- Result: a domain mostly read by others sits left of its readers. Layer mode is unchanged (`rank` = layer order, `flowX` 0.3).
- **Acceptance** (deterministic, after Reset): for every directed business route that is not in the `back` set of `flowRanks`, the upstream domain's centre x is less than the downstream one's; every source system's centre x is less than every business domain's.

#### 2.3 Neutral treatment at star scale

- Halo and rim in `C.muted` at today's halo alphas (no domain colour), name in `C.text` at a fixed 12 px 600 (business names keep `10 + r·k/25`, 13 to 20 px), count line and composition bar as today. Reason: "neutral" is the layer-mode convention (D-048), already accessible (text 4.5:1, marks 3:1).
- The domain colour stays the panel dot and the layer-mode band colour.
- An individual source-system summary card (when not grouped, § 2.4) is drawn as a layer card: `C.muted` border, `C.text` name.

#### 2.4 The source-systems group card (summary scale)

- **When.** In domain mode, while the summary weight `a > 0` (D-065), and at least two source systems pass the filters, and the bounding box of their discs intersects no business disc (true unless the viewer has dragged systems among business domains; then each system keeps its own card, as M8.3). One rule, continuous with the crossfade: the group card fades in with `a` exactly as other cards; individual system cards are not drawn while the group is.
- **Footprint.** Width `1.8 · max r` of the grouped systems (one system card's width), height its content, at most the grid's height; centred on the grid's bounding box. It moves with the systems (dragging its header moves and pins them all, in the current scope, as dragging one constellation pins every one today).
- **Content** (`summaryOfGroup(keys)`, by the room rules of `planCard`, D-066 / D-067): header "Source systems" ("Systèmes sources"); count line `plural(7, sysN) · plural(504, L.tables)` ("7 source systems · 504 tables"); one row per system in grid order (top to bottom, column by column): label, count, kind bar (Types order, D-066); no main tables (no core table). The rows reuse the layer-card row shape (label, bar, count) without the colour dot (neutral).
- **Interactions.** Hover: tooltip "Source systems", counts, "Click a row to zoom on it"; over a row, the row's system name, its count and layers line, and "Click: zoom on raw_crm". Click on a row frames that system's disc at least at `kStars · 1.05` (`zoomOnRow` with the whole disc). Double-click on the card frames the grid at least at `kStars · 1.05` (it lands on stars, as D-066). Hover lights the group's routes (§ 3.4).
- **Text alternative.** `#u-summary` lists the business constellations as today, then one `<h3>` "Source systems" with the count line and a `<ul>` of systems (count and kind split per row), whatever the scale.
- **Trajectory from afar** (D-066): the group card carries the summed counts of its members' trajectory tables and the frame; aggregated arrows from systems are bundled through the group like routes.

#### 2.5 Domains panel

- With `HAS_SYS`: two headings, each `<h3>` with its own **all · none**: "Business domains" ("Domaines métier") over `#u-domains`, then "Source systems" ("Systèmes sources") over a new `#u-systems`. Rows through `filterRow` as today (dot, label, count, owner title). **all / none** of a heading tick or untick only its rows, then `refreshPanel` and `relayout` as today.
- Without: one heading "Domains" and one pair of links, exactly as today (`#u-systems` and its heading `hidden`).
- Panel order of Tab stops: business heading links, business rows, systems heading links, system rows. Coarse pointer: rows and links 44 px (existing CSS rules apply to the new markup).

### 3. Routes

#### 3.1 The route model (computed in `layout()`, cached)

- **Per pair** of constellations (or of the group and a constellation, § 3.3): `rel` = relations between passing tables of the two, `lin` = lineage links, `c` = the count the **Links** selector shows (`rel`, `lin` or both, today's `agg`), `f` = net lineage flow (today's `flow`), `dir`: `+1` / `-1` when `|f| > 0.2 · lin` (the downstream end is the reader), `2` (both ends) when `lin > 0` otherwise, `0` with no lineage. Reason: the direction is about lineage only, so a route heavy in relations no longer hides a clear lineage direction (today's threshold on `c`). Routes with `c = 0` under the current **Links** value are not routes.
- Counted over passing tables, folded ones included (D-063): a fold never changes a route.

#### 3.2 Geometry: edge to edge, around other constellations (`routePath`, `routeLine`)

- **Path** (`routePath(a, b, obstacles)`, world space, computed once per layout and after each drag move, cached per pair): from centre to centre, the first of these that clears every obstacle other than `a` and `b`:
  1. the straight segment;
  2. a quadratic Bézier whose control point is offset perpendicular to the segment by `h · L` (`L` the centre distance), `h` tried in the order `+0.15, −0.15, +0.3, −0.3, +0.5, −0.5, +0.75, −0.75, +1.0, −1.0` ("+" = left of `a → b`);
  3. a path through one waypoint: for the first obstacle the straight segment hits, the two points at distance `R_obs + CLEAR` on each side of its centre, perpendicular to the segment; each half (a → waypoint, waypoint → b) tried as in 1–2; then smoothed (Catmull-Rom through the three points); depth at most 2 waypoints.
  If nothing clears, the candidate that crosses the fewest obstacles (then the smallest `|h|`) is kept and the route is flagged `clear: false` (a test hook; the acceptance requires none on the test projects).
- **Obstacles.** Every other drawn constellation as a circle of radius `R_obs = 1.2 · r + 20` (world), which holds its disc and its summary card (card corners within 1.18 r, D-065), so one cached path serves both scales; the group card counts as one obstacle (its rectangle inflated by 20) when it is drawn. A path is sampled at 24 points; it clears an obstacle when every sample segment stays outside it.
- **Ends.** The path is drawn from where it leaves `a`'s outline to where it enters `b`'s: the disc (radius `r`) at star scale, the drawn card rectangle at card scale, interpolated by the summary weight in between (the clip radius along the path moves continuously from the rim to the card edge). Reason: edge to edge on screen, with no jump when crossing the band.
- **Arrowheads** (`routeLine` returns the clipped path with its end tangents): on a route with `dir = ±1`, one arrowhead at the reader's end, its tip on the reader's outline, along the path's tangent; with `dir = 2`, one at each end; none with `dir = 0` (relations only). Size `9 / k` as today.
- `routeLine(a, b)` stays the one helper that turns a pair into a drawable path; `drawTrajectoryArrows` uses it too, so aggregated trajectory arrows also go edge to edge and around the cards. Two routes of one pair in opposite directions (trajectory arrows up and down) are offset by the next `h` candidate so they never coincide.
- Width `(1 + log2 c) · 1.6 / k` (unchanged), colour `C.route`, arrow `C.arrow` (ui-designer may retune both, keeping 3:1 for the arrowheads, D-042).

#### 3.3 Bundling source systems at the summary scale

- While the group card is drawn, routes between a source system and a business domain are bundled into one route **group → domain** (counts summed, `f` summed, `dir` recomputed); routes between two source systems are not drawn (they are inside the group). The individual system routes fade out by `1 − a`, the bundled ones in by `a`, as stars and cards (D-065). Pointer and tooltip act on the set of the current half (`a ≥ 0.5`: bundled), as cards do.
- Density, hover and the filtered count (below) apply to each set on its own.

#### 3.4 Default density, the count of filtered routes, "all routes"

- **Strongest routes** (`strongRoutes(set)`): for each constellation (the group counts as one), its routes sorted by `c` descending; a route is drawn when its `c` is at least the third largest `c` of either of its ends (`ROUTES_PER = 3`, ties included, brief's proposal). Reason: "either end" keeps a small domain's main links even when they are weak for its large neighbour. Deterministic (counts only).
- **Count of filtered routes.** While routes are drawn (`k < 0.6` or cards on) and the current set hides some, the meta line gains a last entry "18 of 40 routes" ("18 routes sur 40", `routesOf(shown, total)`, numbers through `num`). Reason: the meta line is already where the page says what it draws; a legend would be a new canvas element to export and to place at 360 px. Not shown when every route is drawn or when routes are not drawn at this zoom.
- **All routes** control: a checkbox row "Show all routes" ("Afficher toutes les routes") in the universe panel, right after the **Links** select; hidden when neither set hides a route (small projects, acme_retail if so). Kept for the session only (no config key, no localStorage key). Checked: every route of the current set is drawn, and the meta entry is gone.

#### 3.5 Hover, keyboard focus, tooltip

- **Spotlight** (`spot`, a constellation key or `'@systems'` for the group): set by hovering a disc or card on the canvas, and by keyboard focus or mouse hover on a row of the Domains panel (domain mode; source-system rows spot their system at star scale, the group at card scale) or of the Layers panel (layer mode). Reason: the canvas has no keyboard focus (M8.3 known gap); the panel row is the keyboard's handle on a constellation, and a filter row is already focusable. Cleared on `mouseleave`, `focusout`, filter changes and relayouts.
- While a spot is set (outside the trajectory mode): **every** route of that constellation is drawn (density ignored for it) at full alpha in `C.hl`-like ink (ui-designer), its neighbours (the other ends) at full alpha, every other constellation (disc and card) at `DIM_CTX` (0.45) and every other route hidden. Drawn on screen only; a spot set from the panel also frames nothing (no camera move).
- **Route tooltip** (`routeAt(px, py)`: the nearest drawn route within `max(6, width/2 + 3)` screen px of its clipped path; tables and constellations take precedence): "raw_crm → Vehicle" (upstream first; "Vehicle — Claim" without direction), then "12 relations · 30 lineage links" (only the kinds the **Links** selector includes; plural agreement), then the direction line: "Vehicle reads raw_crm" / "Each reads the other" / "No lineage". A bundled route lists its systems: "From: crm 40 · erp 32 · …" (first 12 then "+ N", as `TIP_DOMAINS`). No action line (a route has none).
- `#u-summary`: each constellation gains a line "Routes:" listing every route of its own (not filtered by density), heaviest first, with the other end, the counts and the direction words; the source-systems block lists the bundled routes.

#### 3.6 Trajectory, export

- **Trajectory mode** keeps its own edges and dimming (D-018): routes are drawn at `DIM` with the default density, the spotlight is off, the meta entry stays; aggregated trajectory arrows (card scale) go through `routeLine` (§ 3.2).
- **Export** (`image()` through `draw`): routes, curves, arrowheads, the group card and the "all routes" state as on screen. The spotlight is pointer or focus state and, like hover, is left out (the export button takes the focus anyway).

### 4. Performance

On the large project (browser, `large_page`, 1280 × 800), folded, links `both`:

- route computation (`perf.routes`: route model, both sets, paths, density) ≤ 30 ms per layout;
- a filter change ≤ 1 s (brief), at Fit (cards) and at `kStars`;
- a frame (`perf.draw`) ≤ 50 ms at Fit, with and without a spot, and with all routes;
- a drag step of a constellation (paths recomputed) ≤ 16 ms;
- page open ≤ 3 s; existing summary, trajectory, flow, fold and search budgets unchanged.

Cost bound: with `C` constellations, at most `C(C−1)/2` routes, each checked against `C` obstacles over ≤ 13 candidates × 24 samples; the 1,500-table payload keeps its budgets.

### 5. "No route crosses a constellation it does not touch, at Fit" — how it is measured

- Hook: `geometry().routes` gains, per route of the current set (drawn or not): `a`, `b` (keys, `'@systems'` for the group), `rel`, `lin`, `c`, `dir`, `drawn`, `bundled`, `clear`, and `pts`, the clipped path as world points (24 samples); `geometry().outlines` gives each drawn constellation's current outline: `{k, disc: [x, y, r]}` at star scale or `{k, rect: [x, y, w, h]}` (world) for a drawn card, and the group card's rectangle with its member keys.
- Test (Python, from the hook): a route crosses an outline when one of its path segments intersects the outline shrunk by 1 world px; outlines of the route's own ends (the group's members included for a bundled route) are skipped.
- Acceptance: at Fit, 0 crossings for **every** route (drawn or not, so the spotlight of any constellation and "all routes" are covered), and `clear` true for every route, on: the large project in domain and layer mode with links `both`, at Fit (cards) and at `kStars · 1.05` (discs, the individual system routes); acme_retail at Fit (stars). The counts per run go to `docs/progress.md` (brief § 10: route crossings before and after; "before" is measured once on the M8.3 geometry with the same test function over straight centre-to-centre segments).

## Rejected options

- **The flag computed in the page from kind roles**: brief § 4 ("Python decides"); the override would then live in two places.
- **A `system` key on any rule, or a top-level `source_systems:` list**: the domain rule is where a domain is named, labelled and coloured; a list would be a second way to name domains (invariant 2).
- **The last rule of a key for `system` (as label and colour)**: a value written on the first of several same-key rules would be silently lost.
- **Sorting `domains` in Python (business first)**: the page owns panel order (D-059's reason); every payload of every project would reorder.
- **A neutral colour in the payload for source systems**: changes palette assignment and bytes of business domains on every page; the page can draw neutral on its own.
- **A CLI summary line for source systems**: the summary does not list domains; the panel shows the result; one more line to keep in the user guide's checked summaries for little gain.
- **Source systems in the force simulation with a strong left force**: they spread vertically and still collide into the business cluster; the order and the column are then tuning, not a rule.
- **A single column of systems**: on the large project it is taller than the business cluster and lowers the Fit scale of every card.
- **Shrinking source-system discs geometrically** (scale factor, or names without reserved room): their names become unreadable at every zoom, or boxes overlap (D-040).
- **Group card in layer mode**: layer constellations are layers; the systems are already grouped there.
- **Ordering business domains by net outflow score** (sum of `f`): one large reader can flip a chain; `flowRanks` already gives columns with deterministic cycle handling (invariant 2).
- **Routes recomputed per frame with screen-space clearance**: per-frame cost and flicker while zooming; a world-space clearance that holds both disc and card serves every zoom.
- **Edge bundling or force-directed edge routing libraries**: a new library (D-045's rule: none for what a few lines do), non-deterministic results.
- **Top-N per constellation counted on one end only**: a small domain whose links are all weak for its big neighbour would lose them.
- **Density as a fraction of routes, or a weight threshold**: depends on the project's size; the brief's proposal reads in one sentence.
- **A legend on the canvas for filtered routes**: one more exported element and a 360 px layout problem; the meta line already counts what is drawn.
- **A config key or a remembered choice for "all routes"**: a permanent `dbt-erd.yml` surface (invariant 9) or a new localStorage key for a reading aid.
- **Canvas keyboard focus (roving tabindex on constellations)**: the M8.3 known gap, a larger subject; the panel rows give keyboard access now.
- **Arrowheads on relation-only routes**: a relation has no reading direction (D-018's reason for lineage-only trajectories).
- **Exporting the spotlight**: pointer state, left out as hover (D-021).
