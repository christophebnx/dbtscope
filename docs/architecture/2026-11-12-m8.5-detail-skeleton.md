# M8.5 — Detail skeleton for large groups (design note)

Status: APPROVED FOR AUTONOMOUS MODE (the user asked for the whole of M8 to be built without stopping; every point the brief leaves open is settled below and recorded in D-068 to D-072). One decision, D-072 (`select` of the `data_vault` preset), is marked **awaiting the user's confirmation**: it does not block, and reversing it is one line of the preset.

## Context

Request: when the detail view would draw a large group, draw its **skeleton** instead — every drawn table except the descriptive tables folded into their parent (M8.2), each parent showing one last row "+ N folded tables" — in a layout that fits the drawing area, with the same fold / show choice as the universe, and a focus that draws a table's folded tables and walks through connectors. Roadmap M8.5, brief `docs/briefs/2026-11-12-m8-synthesis.md` § 9 (finding 7 of § 2; constraints § 4; § 3 "Out": splitting the detail into sub-areas and a relation-driven placement of the raw vault stay out).

Current state of the code (after M8.4):

- `domain/detail.py`: `select` keeps the tables in `select` / `exclude` scope and the relations between them; `detail_data` builds `DetailEntity(id, name, group, kind, description, columns)` sorted by name, `DetailKind` with its `role` (D-058). No parent. `cli._views` calls `select` + `detail_data`, then `universe_data`.
- `domain/fold.parents` (D-061) gives the universe's `UniverseNode.parent` (same domain only).
- `render/assets/detail.js`:
  - **Group scope**: a group shows its own tables plus the other-group parents its tables point to (dashed, `isForeign`). With several groups it opens on the **largest group**; "All groups" is offered.
  - **visibleIds()**: the scope, or with a focus a breadth-first walk of `state.depth` steps over `neighbours` (every relation, both directions) restricted to the scope; then the kind filter (the focus always kept).
  - **What depth 1 reaches today** (checked in the code, lines 117–129): the focus and every table one relation away, either direction. From a Data Vault hub: its satellites (they point at it), its links (they point at it), its PIT if selected. **Not** the hubs across its links (two steps: hub ← link → hub), nor the effectivity satellites of its links (hub ← link ← effsat). From a link: its hubs and its effectivity satellites.
  - **buildDot**: `rankdir=LR; nodesep=0.35; ranksep=1.4`; HTML-like tables (header 16 pt bold, key rows, `+ N columns`); edges parent → child with `dir=back`; sinks spread over 3 columns by `minlen` 1–3 when more than 12 (an `unflatten -l 3` imitation). On the large project's `vehicle` group this gives the strip of finding 7 (four ranks wide, about seventy rows high).
  - **Drag**: positions saved in the layout's `detail` section under `viewKey()` = `group|focus|depth|keys-or-all|ticked kinds`; when any drawn table is pinned, a second render with `neato` pins every table (`pos="x,y!"`, `inputscale=72`). `centres(svg)` reads table centres from Graphviz's polygons.
  - **Meta line**: `${drawn} / ${scope} tables · ${relations} relations · dashed: other group`.
  - Handle: `{shown, relayout, focus, svgImage}`; no state hook.
- `render/assets/universe.js`: `foldMemory` (key `dbtscope-fold:<title>`, `folded` / `shown`, written only when it differs from `defaults.fold`), `state.fold = FOLDABLE ? memory ?? defaults.fold : false`, the panel control `fieldset#u-fold`, strings `T.fold`, `T.foldTitle`, `T.foldedN`.
- `boot.js`: starts `startDetail(PAGE.detail, LAYOUT, EXPORT)` and `startUniverse(PAGE.universe, openInDetail, detailIds, LAYOUT, EXPORT, openFlow)`; `openInDetail(id)` shows the tab and calls `views.detail.focus(id)`.
- Viz.js 2.1.2 `full.render.js` (Graphviz 2.40.1). Its documented engines are `circo`, `dot`, `fdp`, `neato`, `osage`, `twopi`; `sfdp` is not built in (no GTS / triangulation). The bundle's strings are not greppable, so the protocol's step 0 probes them.

Numbers on the large project (`tests/large_project.py`, preset `data_vault`, `group_by: schema`, preset `select` = hubs, links, NH / same-as links and satellites incl. effectivity satellites; PITs, dimensions and facts are **not** selected):

- `vehicle` group: 70 hubs + 66 links + 135 satellites (incl. 26 effectivity satellites of links and the two special vehicle satellites) = **271 own tables**, plus **F** other-group hubs referenced by the 14 vehicle links that take a hub of another domain (F ≤ 14; the developer writes the exact number into `tests/fixtures/large-data-vault/expected.json`). It is the largest group, so the detail opens on it.
- Every one of the 135 satellites has a parent in the vehicle group (step 1 for all but `NO_RELATION_SAT`, step 2 for it and `TWO_PARENT_SAT`); `OTHER_DOMAIN_SAT` is in group `claim`, its parent in `vehicle` (D-061: not folded). So the skeleton draws **136 own tables + F** other-group hubs.
- "All groups": 175 + 191 + 422 = 788 tables, skeleton 366.
- `acme_retail` (`expected.json`): 54 detail tables, groups of 4 to 13 tables, all under the threshold, so its detail never becomes a skeleton (also with "All groups": 54 ≤ 60).

## Chosen design

### 1. Payload: the parent of a detail entity (D-068)

- **`DetailEntity.parent: str | None = None`**, the **last key** of a detail entity (after `columns`), the parent's `unique_id` (invariant 11), `null` otherwise. Same name and meaning as `UniverseNode.parent` (one word per concept, D-027).
- **Which parent.** The detail does **not** run its own parent rule: it takes the universe's parent (one implementation, invariant 2; the same table is the same entity in both views). A detail entity `t` gets `parent = p` when the universe gave `t` the parent `p`, **`p` is also in the detail payload**, and **`group_of(t) == group_of(p)`** (same detail group). Otherwise `null`:
  - parent outside the detail scope (e.g. a satellite whose hub is excluded): no parent — there is nothing to fold it into;
  - parent in another detail group: no parent — a group never loses one of its own tables to a dashed foreign table (the D-061 rule applied to groups, as D-062 applied it to layers);
  - a table in the detail but outside the universe scope (`universe.select` narrower than `select`): no parent.
- **Code.** `detail_data(tables, relations, config, parents: Mapping[str, str] | None = None)`: `parents` is the universe's `{child id: parent id}`; `detail_data` keeps the entries that pass the two conditions above (it already computes `group_of` per table). `cli._views` builds the universe payload first and passes `{n.id: n.parent for n in universe.nodes if n.parent is not None}`; the returned tuple keeps its order `(detail, universe)`. No new module, no second call of `fold.parents`. `domain/` stays pure.
- **Roles** are already on `DetailKind.role` (M8.1); the page reads a table's role as `KIND[e.kind].role`.
- **No new `defaults` key and no new config key.** The detail uses the same fold choice as the universe (§ 4): its default is the existing `universe.fold` (payload `PAGE.universe.defaults.fold`), read by the shared fold service. Rejected: a `detail.fold` key (two defaults for one choice the brief wants shared) and a detail `defaults` object (one more key for a value the page already has). The user guide says that `universe.fold` sets the opening fold choice of both views (its name is kept: renaming it would break `dbt-erd.yml`, invariant 9).

**Reference-payload diff** (one regeneration, checked by the developer with a script before regenerating, written in `docs/progress.md`): in every case of `tests/fixtures/m3-reference-pages/payloads/*.json` (`none`, `fr`, `edge`, `layers_domains`, `kinds`, `star_schema`, `data_vault` and the offline twins) each **detail entity** gains `,"parent":null` as its last key, after `"columns":[…]`. Nothing else in the payloads: no reference project has a descriptive table with a parent (M8.2's note). Removing that key from the new payloads gives the old ones byte for byte. Every `expected.json` hash and size changes (assets change too).

### 2. When the skeleton applies (D-069)

In `detail.js`, with `V` = `visibleIds()` (scope or focus set, kind filter — unchanged in meaning, with the focus walk of § 5):

- A table `t ∈ V` is **foldable** when `PARENT(t) ∈ V`, `t` is not the focus, and `PARENT(t)` is not the focus (the focus's own folded tables are always drawn in full, whatever the fold state).
- **Skeleton** ⇔ the fold is on (§ 4) **and** `V.size > SKELETON_MIN` **and** at least one table of `V` is foldable.
- `SKELETON_MIN = 60`, a constant at the top of `startDetail` (one place, no config key: a project that wants every table drawn turns the fold off; a key is added only if a real project asks for another threshold). "Exceeds" is strictly greater.
- Below the threshold, with no foldable table, or with the fold off: the drawing is **exactly today's** (same DOT, same layout code path). So `acme_retail`, pages without roles and the star schema example draw as before.
- The threshold is on **tables to draw before folding** (`V.size`, foreign tables and the focus set included), so focus and filters can turn the skeleton on or off.

### 3. Skeleton content (D-069)

- **Drawn**: every table of `V` that is not foldable (`drawn = V − folded`). On a Data Vault group that is its core and connector tables (the brief's "core and connector only"); a descriptive table with no parent, a table with no role or a technical table in scope is drawn as an ordinary table, since it has no row to stand in for it (every table reachable, nothing lost). Other-group tables (dashed) are drawn as today.
- **Folded**: `FOLDED: Map<parent id, [folded ids]>`, sorted by name then id, visible (kind filter applied) only.
- **Row of an entity**: after its column rows, an `<HR/>` then one last row `<tr><td colspan="2" align="left">…</td></tr>` in the muted ink of the "+ N columns" row (`grey`, not italic), text:
  - one kind: `+ ${plural(n, L.foldedN)} · ${label}` → en `+ 3 folded tables · Satellite`, fr `+ 3 tables repliées · Satellite`;
  - several kinds: `+ ${plural(n, L.foldedN)} · ${label} ${num(c)}, ${label} ${num(c)}` in payload kind order, at most `KIND_LIST` (3) then `, + ${num(rest)}` → `+ 4 folded tables · Satellite 3, PIT 1`.
  This rule works for any preset and both languages (D-039): the noun that agrees with the number is ours (`foldedN`, French 0 and 1 singular); kind labels are written as in the config and paired with a count, never inflected (the card rows' precedent, D-064). A kind with `label: null` shows its key. Text through `dotText`.
- **Tooltip**: the table's tooltip (the node's `tooltip`, the SVG title shown anywhere on the table, the row included) gains after the name line: a blank line, `${plural(n, L.foldedN)}:` (`L.colon`), then one line per folded table `name · Kind`, at most 12, then `+ ${num(rest)}`; the description follows as today. A per-cell tooltip is rejected: Graphviz emits a cell `TOOLTIP` only with an `HREF`, which would put links in the page and the exported SVG.
- **Relations**: only relations between drawn tables are drawn (`buildDot` already filters on `ids`; it receives `drawn`). Relations of folded tables are not drawn.
- **Meta line** (skeleton): `${num(V.size)} / ${plural(scope.size, L.tables)} · ${plural(folded, L.foldedN)} (${kinds}) · ${plural(relCount, T.rels)}` + ` · dashed: other group` when grouped; `kinds` = `Label count` pairs of the folded tables, payload kind order, at most 3 then `+ N`. En: `285 / 285 tables · 135 folded tables (Satellite 135) · 160 relations · dashed: other group`; fr: `285 / 285 tables · 135 tables repliées (Satellite 135) · 160 relations · pointillé : autre groupe`. The first number keeps counting tables (folded ones included): counts are never reduced. The relation count is that of what is drawn. The Types list keeps counting every table of the scope (unchanged).
- **Search box** (`#d-q`, highlight): a name matching a folded table highlights its entity (the row stands for it). The focus box's list (`#d-tables`) keeps every table of the scope, folded ones included: typing a folded table's name focuses it (§ 5).
- **Kind / group filters**: unticking `Satellite` while folded removes satellites from `V`; the rows shrink or vanish and the skeleton condition is re-evaluated (with no foldable table left, the drawing is today's drawing of the remaining tables). Unticking `Hub` leaves the hubs' satellites without a visible parent: they are drawn as ordinary tables (D-062: a filter never makes a ticked table disappear); effectivity satellites still fold into their visible links.

### 4. One fold choice for both views (D-069)

- **Service.** `boot.js` builds `FOLD` (a stateful service, like `LAYOUT` and `EXPORT`, passed as a parameter; no view declares a global): `FOLD.on()` → the stored choice (`dbtscope-fold:<title>`, `folded` / `shown`) else `PAGE.universe.defaults.fold`; `FOLD.set(on, from)` writes the key only when it differs from the default (removes it otherwise), then calls every listener but `from`; `FOLD.listen(fn)`. `universe.js foldMemory` moves into it unchanged (same key, same values, same "written only when it differs" rule; never in the layout, never exported). `startUniverse(..., FOLD)` and `startDetail(DATA, LAYOUT, EXPORT, FOLD)` gain the parameter.
- **Each view keeps its availability**: universe `state.fold = FOLDABLE && FOLD.on()` (unchanged behaviour); detail `foldOn = DETAIL_FOLDABLE && FOLD.on()` with `DETAIL_FOLDABLE` = some detail entity has a parent. (Detail parents are a subset of universe parents, so a detail control never exists without the universe one.)
- **Changing it in one view changes the other.** On a listener call a view sets its checkbox and redraws now if its tab is visible, else marks itself stale and redraws in `shown()` (the detail's Graphviz render is not run in a hidden tab).
- **Control in the detail panel**: `fieldset#d-fold` right after the Group select (before "Around the focused table"), the same markup as `#u-fold`: checkbox `#d-fold-on`, label `L.fold` (moved from universe `T.fold` to `common.js L`, the same text in both views), muted second line `#d-fold-kinds` (descriptive kinds with a parent in the payload, labels as in the config, first 3 then `+ N`), count `#d-fold-n` (tables with a parent in the current scope), title `T.foldTitle` (detail's own: en "Draw each descriptive table as a last row of its entity when more than 60 tables are drawn. Double-click a table to see its folded tables."; fr "Dessiner chaque table descriptive comme une dernière ligne de son entité au-delà de 60 tables. Double-cliquer une table pour voir ses tables repliées."). Hidden when `!DETAIL_FOLDABLE`.
- **Warning, no modal, no confirmation**: when the fold is off, `V.size > SKELETON_MIN` and some table of `V` would be foldable, a muted line `#d-fold-warn` under the control (`role="status"`, polite) reads en `${plural(n, L.tables)}: the drawing will be hard to read`, fr `${plural(n, L.tables)} : le schéma sera difficile à lire` (`T.heavy`). Empty otherwise. The drawing is then today's full drawing. A confirmation is rejected: showing is an explicit choice, a click away from folding again.
- `L.foldedN` moves to `common.js` (universe `T.foldedN` removed; same strings).
- Hint: the detail hint gains, while the control is available and on, ` · a last row "+ N": double-click the table to draw them` / ` · une dernière ligne « + N » : double-cliquer la table pour les dessiner` (`T.hintFold`).

### 5. Focus: folded tables in full, connectors as passages (D-070)

Present behaviour: § Context (depth 1 from a hub reaches its satellites and links, not the hubs across). New walk in `visibleIds()`:

- `level(focus) = 0`. Expanding a table `x` of level `d < state.depth`: every scope neighbour `n` not seen gets level `d + 1`. If `n` has role `connector` and `n` is not the focus, `n` is a **passage**: each of `n`'s scope neighbours `m` not seen whose role is **not** `descriptive` also gets level `d + 1` (one passage per step: those `m` are expanded at the next step only, even when they are connectors themselves).
- So "direct links" from a hub = the hub, its satellites, its links **and the hubs across them** (Done-when 2); a link's effectivity satellites come at level 2 (through the link as an ordinary step) and the satellites of the hubs across at level 2. From a link (focus): its hubs and its effectivity satellites (no passage at the focus). From a satellite: its parent(s).
- The kind filter applies after the walk as today (the focus always kept); an unticked connector still passes (as today depth 2 reaches across a hidden link).
- **Folded tables around the focus**: the focus's own folded tables (and a focused folded table itself) are always drawn (§ 2 "foldable"). Other tables of the focus set follow the skeleton rule of § 2: a focus set of more than 60 tables with the fold on draws its other entities with their rows; below, everything in the set is drawn in full.
- **Only when roles exist**: the rule needs a kind of role `connector`; with none (no preset, no `role` keys) the walk is exactly today's. It applies whether or not the skeleton is on (it is the brief's rule for focus), so on `acme_retail` focusing a hub now also shows the hubs across its links; its opening drawing (no focus) is unchanged (Done-when 3).
- Entry points unchanged: double-click on a table, the focus box (every scope table listed, folded ones included), `openInDetail` from the universe (also for a folded table, which is in the detail payload when the preset selects it: its group is its parent's).

### 6. Shape of a skeleton: chosen by measurement (D-071)

Folding alone leaves a hub column and a link column. The shape algorithm applies **only to skeleton drawings** (non-skeleton drawings keep today's DOT, which keeps Done-when 3 byte-exact). The developer implements the candidate options behind a temporary internal switch, measures them with the protocol below, keeps only the chosen one (no dead code, no option shipped), and writes the measures into `docs/progress.md` and the outcome line of D-071.

**Candidates, in preference order** (dot first: it keeps the parent-to-child reading, crossing minimisation and today's look):

1. **O1 — dot, today's DOT + `pack=true` + `ratio=compress` with `size` = the drawing area in inches.** Cheapest (attributes only). `compress` really repacks ranks in dot (unlike a numeric `ratio`, which only stretches). Expected to fail: the vehicle skeleton is one giant component (66 links over 70 hubs) plus a few isolated hubs, so packing components side by side cannot wrap it; measured for the record. Aspect is measured on the layer's bbox (a `size` scale-down must not count as reshaping).
2. **O2 — dot with wrapped ranks imposed by us** (as flow.js imposes columns with `rank=same`). Order the drawn tables: connected components of the drawn relations, largest first; inside one, a breadth-first order from its core table with the most relations (neighbours by name, then id); isolated tables last by name. Rows of capacity `c = round(sqrt(n · A · h̄ / w̄))` (`A` = the drawing area's width / height; `w̄`, `h̄` the mean estimated table width and height plus `nodesep` / `ranksep`; widths estimated from the header's characters at 16 pt bold, heights from the row count — estimates, never measured, as the universe does). `rankdir=TB`, `newrank=true`, each row a `{rank=same; …}` subgraph, rows chained by invisible edges `id="skel-chain-<r>"` (`style=invis`), real edges `constraint=false` (they still drive the order inside rows); `ranksep=0.6; nodesep=0.3`. The chain edges are removed from the SVG right after rendering (before `graphZoom`), so highlight and export never see them. Variant **O2-LR** (`rankdir=LR`, rows become columns) is measured too; between the two, the one with fewer edge crossings wins (then shorter total edge length).
3. **O3 — grid placement + Graphviz for the edges.** Same order as O2; table sizes from a first, edge-less `dot` render (each node's polygon box, a new helper `boxes(svg)` beside `centres`); rows filled to the width `W = sqrt(Σarea · A)` with 24 pt gaps; then one `neato` render with every table pinned (`pos="x,y!"`, `inputscale=72`, `splines=true`, `overlap=true`), the path dragged tables already use. Two renders; full control of the aspect; more crossings expected.
4. **O4 — `neato` free**: `overlap=false`, `sep="+12"`, `splines=true`, `mode=major`, `start=42` (deterministic). Round shapes; loses the left-to-right reading.
5. **O5 — `fdp`**: the same attributes as O4.
6. (**O6 — `sfdp`**, only if step 0 finds it in the bundle: the same attributes.)

Not measured, rejected on principle: a numeric `ratio` or `ratio=fill` (stretches whitespace, no reshaping), `twopi` / `circo` (radial / circular: long edges across the drawing, no hierarchy), `osage` (places clusters, ignores edges for placement), `patchwork` (a treemap, no edges).

**Cost of each for drag, saved positions, highlight, export.** All options produce a Graphviz SVG with `g.node` polygons, so `centres()` reads positions and the existing pinned re-render (neato, every table pinned) works unchanged; for O3 the pinned positions override the grid in its one pinned render (no extra render). Highlight and export use `g.node` / `g.edge`: unchanged, except O2's chain edges, removed after rendering. Saved positions: § 7.

**Measurement protocol** (headless Chromium, the browser-test setup, viewport 1280 × 800, no network):

0. **Engines.** Render `digraph{a->b}` with each engine name (`dot`, `neato`, `fdp`, `sfdp`, `osage`, `twopi`, `circo`, `patchwork`) through `GRAPHVIZ.render(dot, {engine})`; record which succeed.
1. **Inputs.** (i) the large project, detail tab, group `vehicle`, fold on, key columns (the default); (ii) the large project, "All groups" (788 tables, skeleton 366); (iii) the **1,500-table worst case**: a synthetic detail payload of one group with 300 hubs, 300 links of 2 or 3 hubs (seeded `random.Random(1500)`), 900 satellites spread 1 to 5 per hub and link, each with its parent (skeleton 600 tables), written with a test helper `detail_page(folder, detail_payload)` next to `universe_page` in `tests/browser/conftest.py`.
2. **Measures per option and input**, median of 3 runs: **cold time** = from the tab click (or the group change) to the mounted zoom (`zv` set; Viz instance creation included for the first render of the page); **warm time** = a second render; **aspect** = drawing width / height in Graphviz points (the size `graphZoom` reads); **fit scale** `k_fit` (`zv.get().k` after Fit); **name size after three wheel notches** = `16 × k_fit × 1.1487³` CSS px (d3's wheel: `2^(0.002 × 100)` per notch of `deltaY` 100) — checked once against a real `page.mouse.wheel` ×3; **overlapping tables** (pairs of `g.node` boxes that intersect: must be 0); **edge crossings** (pairs of edge paths that intersect, from `getPointAtLength` samples every 4 pt, edges sharing an end excluded) and **total edge length** (Σ `getTotalLength`, divided by the mean table width).
3. **Acceptance of an option**: on (i) `0.5 ≤ aspect ≤ 3`, cold time < 5 s, no overlap; on (iii) cold time < 15 s.
4. **Decision rule**: take the **first option in the preference order that is accepted and gives names ≥ 8 CSS px after three notches on (i)**. If no accepted option reaches 8 px, take the first accepted option: the brief's "after one double-click **or** three wheel notches" still holds through the double-click (§ Test plan, Done-when 1), and the 8 px shortfall is written in `docs/progress.md` with the best value. If (iii) fails for every option while (i) passes, take the first option accepted on (i) alone and record the worst-case time as a known limit (it is not a Done-when).
5. **Stop condition**: if **no option** reaches `0.5 ≤ aspect ≤ 3` with a cold time < 5 s on (i), Done-when 1 of brief § 9 cannot be met. The developer then ships the rest of M8.5 (payload, skeleton rows, control, focus rule) with the option of best aspect among those under 5 s (today's DOT if none), marks M8.5 "blocked" in `docs/progress.md`, and reports to the user: the full table (option × input × cold / warm time, aspect, `k_fit`, name px after three notches, overlaps, crossings, edge length), the closest option and by how much it misses, and the two ways forward the brief keeps out of scope (§ 3 "Out": splitting the detail into sub-areas; a relation-driven placement of the raw vault) or a relaxed aspect band — a question for the user, not a choice made alone.

### 7. Saved positions, export, layout format

- **Layout format v1 untouched** (D-013): no new section, the `detail` section stays a mapping of view keys (`domain/layout.LAYOUT_SECTIONS` accepts any key).
- **View key of a skeleton drawing**: `viewKey()` + `'|skeleton'` while the skeleton is drawn; unchanged otherwise. Positions dragged in a skeleton belong to the skeleton's coordinates and never move a table of the full drawing (and the reverse). Existing keys (all saved from full drawings) keep matching the full drawings: **old layouts load unchanged**. A folded table's stored position stays in its full-drawing key (the store is never pruned) and applies again when the table is drawn there (fold off, or focus). An entity's position dragged in the skeleton applies to the skeleton only.
- **Export** SVG and PNG: `svgImage` copies the drawing as on screen; rows are part of the tables' labels and appear as drawn. Tooltips are stripped as today.

### 8. `select` of the `data_vault` preset (D-072, awaiting the user's confirmation)

Today the preset's `select` draws hubs, links, NH links, same-as links and every satellite form; PITs, ref hubs / ref sats, bridges, dimensions and facts have kinds but are not drawn in the detail. **Decision: unchanged** — dimensions and facts do not enter the detail by default.

- In a Data Vault project, dimensions and facts are data products read from the vault (lineage), related to one another but not to hubs or links: in the vault's group they would be an unconnected second drawing; their own ERD (fact → dimension) is better seen in their own group, which a project gets with one line.
- On the large project they would add 13 core tables to the `vehicle` skeleton (149 + F), past the brief's "136 or fewer" (Done-when 1).
- It changes the detail of every existing Data Vault project that relies on the preset, without being asked.
- `acme_retail` pins its own `select` (`*_hub`, `*_link`, `*_sat`, `dim_*`, `fct_*`), so its detail is unchanged either way (Done-when 3).
- **Reversal**: one line, `select: [… , "dim_*", "fct_*"]` in `src/dbtscope/presets/data_vault.yml` (plus the preset's doc and the reference `data_vault` payload regeneration). If the user asks for it, Done-when 1's count becomes 149 + F.

### 9. Error handling

No new config key, no new error. Graphviz failures keep today's path (`L.error` in the box, Viz instance recreated). A skeleton render superseded by a newer one is dropped by `renderSeq` as today.

### 10. Test hooks (`detail.js`)

The handle gains **`state()`**: `{ group, focus, depth, fold: { available, on }, skeleton, threshold: SKELETON_MIN, drawn: [ids], folded: { parentId: [ids] }, rows: { parentId: text }, meta, warn, layoutMs, size: [w, h], dot }` — `layoutMs` the last render's time (tab click or change to the mounted zoom), `size` the Graphviz drawing size in points, `dot` the last DOT text sent to Graphviz (first render). `focus(id)` and `svgImage(scale)` unchanged.

## Rejected options

- **A detail parent rule of its own** (`fold.parents` over the detail's tables and groups): the step-2 pool would differ from the universe's, so one table could have two parents; the universe's parent filtered by scope and group gives one answer.
- **Folding into a parent of another group** (a dashed foreign table): the group would lose one of its own tables to a table drawn only as context.
- **A `detail.fold` key or a detail `defaults` object**: the brief wants one choice for both views; `universe.fold` already holds it.
- **Two independent fold choices** (one per view): a viewer folding in the universe would find the detail unfolded, and the reverse.
- **A config key for the threshold**: no project asked; turning the fold off already draws everything.
- **Hiding tables with no parent** (descriptive without parent, no role, technical) in the skeleton: they would be unreachable from the drawing with no row standing for them.
- **Dropping other-group tables from the skeleton** to reach a literal "136": they show where the group connects; Done-when 1 counts the group's own tables (D-069).
- **Inflecting kind labels** ("3 satellites"): impossible in general for config labels in two languages; `Label count` pairs and our own plural noun are used instead (D-039, D-064).

### Shape: readability targets and preference (for D-071)

Measured at 1280 × 800 on the vehicle skeleton (148 drawn: 136 own + 12 dashed). Name size = 16 pt × the zoom scale on screen.

| Moment | Goal | Floor to ship the option | Before M8.5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit | at least 5.3 px (names are coloured, distinguishable blocks; legible text is not expected at Fit) | 4 px | 0.64 px |
| after 3 wheel notches (×1.516) | **at least 8 px** (architect's rule 4) | 6 px, the shortfall recorded | 0.97 px |
| after one double-click on a drawn table (focus set, fitted) | at least 10 px (acme at Fit shows 8.9 px for 17 tables; a focus set is of that order) | 10 px, asserted | n/a |

- **"Fits the drawing area"**: at Fit the whole drawing is inside the area (shared `fit()`, contain and centre, no padding: unchanged for every view) and covers **at least 60 % of the area** (drawing × scale, width × height, over the area's). The shape aims at the **area's own aspect** `A = #d-graph.clientWidth / clientHeight` at render time (991 / 680 = 1.46 at 1280 × 800 with the panel open; about 0.7 at 360 px portrait), clamped to the acceptance band 0.5 to 3; when the box has no size, 1.5. The aspect achieved is judged against the brief's band (0.5 to 3, D-071) and, as a goal, within 0.7× to 1.5× of `A`. At 360 px the same rule gives taller rows (a drawing about 0.7 wide to tall); names stay below 4 px at Fit there by arithmetic (about 3 px), so the double-click path is the way to read, and the hint says so. The shape is computed when a drawing is rendered; resizing the window or toggling the panel refits but does not re-shape (letterboxing is accepted there).
- **Preference order**: the architect's order stands (O1 dot with pack, O2 dot with imposed rows, O3 grid, O4 neato, O5 fdp), judged by acceptance first. From a reader's side two more tie-breaks, in this order: (1) **a link near its hubs**: the mean edge length in mean table widths (`Σ getTotalLength / edges / mean width`); an accepted option whose mean is more than 1.5× that of the best accepted option is passed over, because a link far from its hubs defeats the skeleton; (2) **reading order**: rows read left to right then top to bottom (`rankdir=TB`, rows) over columns (LR) when their crossings are within 10 %; an isolated hub never sits between connected ones (isolated tables last, by name). Hubs of one link adjacent in the order, a link right after its first hub in the breadth-first order, are what the order of O2 / O3 produces and what the layout must keep.
- Deterministic: the same group gives the same drawing (order by relations, name, id; no random).
