## Context

Request: when the selected tables of the detail view exceed a threshold and some have a parent (M8.2), draw only the core and connector tables. Each holds one row that counts its folded tables. The layout must fit the drawing area, and the view must offer a fold / show control, a focus that shows everything around a table, and exports and arrangements as today. Roadmap M8.5; brief § 9 is the spec (finding 7).

## Chosen design

### 1. Payload: parents and roles in the detail (`domain/`)

- **`DetailEntity.parent: str = ""`, last key of an entity.** It is the parent's `unique_id` (invariant 11), or `""`.
  - **Source.** The value comes from the **universe's** computation: one rule, one result. A table folds into the same parent in both views (invariant 2, D-062). It is not recomputed over the detail's tables: the detail has groups, not places, and a second run of `fold.parents` over another pool could give another answer for the same table.
  - **Interface.** `detail_data(tables, relations, config, parents: Mapping[str, str])` sets `parent = parents.get(uid, "")` only when that parent is itself one of the detail's `tables`, else `""`. A table whose parent is not selected for the detail is drawn as today.
  - **Call site.** `cli.py` computes `universe_data` first, then passes `{n.id: n.parent for n in universe.nodes if n.parent}` to `detail_data`. This is orchestration only; the rule stays in `domain/fold.py`.
  - **Outside the universe.** A detail table outside the universe scope (a `universe.exclude` the detail does not share) has no parent.
- **`DetailKind.role: Role = "none"`, last key of a detail kind**, from `KindRule.role` (`"none"` when unset; `other` → `"none"`). It is used by the focus rule (§ 4: a `connector` is a passage). This is the "detail payload gets roles in M8.5" of D-059.
- **No other payload change.** The detail Types list keeps its payload order: ordering it by role, as the universe does, would reorder acme_retail's detail panel, and done-when 3 forbids that. The fold control needs no flag from Python: the page shows it by rule (§ 3), as D-063 did. The threshold is a drawing rule of the page, like `FLOW_MAX` (D-036), not a payload value.
- **Pages without roles or parents.** Every entity ends with `"parent":""` and every detail kind with `"role":"none"`; nothing else changes.

### 2. The skeleton (`render/assets/detail.js`)

**Sets.** They are computed once per render, as in the universe (D-063):

- `PARENT` (id → parent id, from `e.parent`) and `ROLE` (kind → role).
- **visible**: today's `visibleIds()`.
- **folded now**: visible tables whose parent is visible, while the skeleton is on (below).
- **drawn**: visible minus folded now.
- `held(p)`: the folded-now tables of `p`, in name order.

**Trigger.** `skeleton()` is true when all of these hold:

1. no focus is set (§ 4);
2. the **scope** holds more than `SKELETON_MIN` = **60** tables (the brief's proposal);
3. at least one scope table has its parent in the scope;
4. the viewer's fold choice is "folded" (§ 3).

Reasons:

- The count is the **scope** (the group's tables), not the filtered set. Ticking or unticking a kind then never switches the drawing between two layouts: a viewer who unticks Satellite on the vehicle group keeps the skeleton layout, with entities holding nothing.
- 60 is about the size Graphviz still draws legibly at Fit in the 1280 × 800 box with today's layout. The acme_retail groups (at most 54 tables) all stay under it.
- Below the threshold, or with no parent in the scope, `skeleton()` is false and the detail is today's, byte for byte in its DOT (done-when 3).

**Content of the skeleton.**

- **Drawn tables.** The drawn tables are the core and connector tables, plus every visible table that has no parent. A descriptive table without a parent (D-062: no relation and no stem, a tie, another place) stays drawn as its own table, as in the universe. Drawing it is the only way it stays reachable, and the brief's "core and connector only" describes the folded case.
- **Columns hidden.** At the keys level a skeleton table draws **no column rows** (no key rows, no "+ N columns" row): only its header and, when it holds tables, the folded row. **All columns** still draws every column, as the viewer's explicit choice.
  - Reason, the estimate for the vehicle skeleton. With key rows, a table is about 230 × 105 pt plus gaps (header 32 pt, 1 to 4 key rows of 24 pt, the "+ N columns" row, the folded row). That gives about 6 M pt² for 136 tables, a Fit scale of about 0.3 in the 1280 × 680 drawing box, and 16 pt names at about 7 px after three wheel notches (×1.52): not readable (done-when 1).
  - With header and folded row only (about 56 pt high), the area is about 2.9 M pt², Fit is about 0.42, and names are about 10 px after three notches.
  - Columns are what a focus (one double-click) shows. The relation's column stays in each edge's tooltip, and the table's tooltip gains its column count ("12 columns, 3 keys").
- **The folded row.** It is the last row of an entity that holds tables, one cell across the table, muted, in italics like today's "+ N columns" row. Text: `+ <n> <folded table|folded tables> · <kind labels>`, for example "+ 3 folded tables · Satellite" or "+ 4 folded tables · Satellite 3 · PIT 1".
  - The kind labels are those of the folded tables, in Types order (`labelOf(KIND[k])`). Each label is followed by its count only when more than one kind is folded.
  - Plural agreement is done by `plural(n, L.foldedN)`. French: "+ 3 tables repliées · Satellite".
  - Reason for not writing "+ 3 satellites": config labels are singular nouns ("Satellite", "Ref sat", "PIT", French "Lien NH"). The payload has no plural form for them, and inflecting by rule fails for acronyms and for French compounds. The universe already says "3 folded tables" with the same pair (D-063), so both views use one wording.
- **Tooltip of the row.**
  - Graphviz writes a cell's tooltip into SVG only when the cell has an `HREF`, which would make it a link. So the folded list goes into the **table's** tooltip (the SVG `<title>` that `nodeDot` already writes): after the description, a blank line, "3 folded tables", then one line per table, `name · Kind`, the first `TIP_FOLDED` = 12 (as the universe) then "+ N". It shows over the folded row as over the whole table.
  - If the developer finds that Viz.js writes a cell-level title without an `HREF`, the cell may carry the same text. The table tooltip stays either way.
- **Relations.** Relations of folded tables are not drawn: `buildDot` draws only relations between drawn tables. The click highlight (`neighbours`) lights drawn neighbours only.
- **Highlight box (`#d-q`).** A drawn table is lit when its name matches, or when the name of one of its folded tables does. A viewer looking for `car_status_sat` thus sees `car_hub` lit. Reason: brief § 4, "every folded table stays reachable".
- **Focus box and datalist.** They keep listing every scope table, folded ones included. Focusing a folded table draws it in full (§ 4).
- **Types list.** It keeps counting every scope table per kind, unchanged (brief: "The Types list keeps counting every table"). Unticking a kind removes its tables from the visible set: folded rows lose them, drawn tables of that kind disappear, and a folded table whose parent is unticked is drawn as its own table (D-063's rule, no ghost).

### 3. The fold control (`detail.js`, `page.html`)

- **Its own state, with its own memory**, not the universe's.
  - Reason: the two choices answer different needs. The universe fold reduces stars at overview, and a viewer who shows satellites there (to see them as stars, or to drag them) does not thereby ask Graphviz for a 271-table strip. The detail fold is about Graphviz legibility.
  - A shared state would also make a change in one tab relayout the other, hidden tab, and the universe would pay its relayout for a detail click.
  - The brief's "the same fold / show choice as the universe" is honoured by the same words, the same widget and the same default behaviour (folded); see "Rejected options".
- **Default** folded. No config key: `universe.fold` is a universe setting (D-063) and the detail fold acts only above the threshold, where showing is rarely wanted. A key can be added later on request; no `dbt-erd.yml` surface is added now (invariant 9).
- **Memory.** localStorage `dbtscope-detail-fold:<title>`, values `folded` / `shown`, written only when it differs from the default (choosing "folded" again removes it), never in the layout, never exported. Without storage the choice lasts for the session.
  - The helper `memory(name, values)` moves from `universe.js` to `common.js` (as `viewerMemory`), so both views use one implementation (invariant 2). `byMemory` and `foldMemory` are unchanged in behaviour.
- **Where.** A fieldset "Entities" / "Entités" in the detail panel, after **Group** and before **Around the focused table**. One checkbox row as in the universe: "Fold Satellite · PIT" (descriptive kind labels with at least one parent in the scope, Types order, the first 3 then "+ N"), the count of scope tables with a parent on the right, and the same `title`. The shared strings (`foldH`, `foldRow`, `foldTitle`, `foldedN`) move to `common.js L`, because both views show them with the same text (render/CLAUDE.md rule).
- **When shown.** When conditions 2 and 3 of the trigger hold for the current group. It is hidden otherwise, so acme_retail's detail panel is unchanged.
- **While focused** it stays visible but is disabled, with the `title` "A focus draws every table" / "Une table ciblée affiche toutes les tables". Its choice applies when the focus is cleared.
- **Warning.** While the control is shown and unchecked (so the whole scope is drawn), one line under the row, `role="status"`: "271 tables: the drawing will be hard to read" / "271 tables : le schéma sera difficile à lire" (`num`, `plural(n, L.tables)`). The count is the visible tables, the number Graphviz is about to draw. The line is absent while folded, while focused, and when the visible tables are 60 or fewer (a filtered scope).
- **Changing it** re-renders (Fit, as a group change), keeps the focus box as it is, and announces "Descriptive tables folded: 136 tables drawn" / "Descriptive tables shown" in a `sr-only` live region `#d-live` (ui-designer finalises).

### 4. Focus: folded tables in full, a connector is a passage

- **Focus never folds.** While a table is focused (double-click, the focus box, a double-click from the universe through `focus(id)`), `skeleton()` is false. Every visible table is drawn with its columns, in today's layout, whatever the fold choice. Reasons:
  - It is the brief's rule ("draws its folded tables in full, whatever the fold state").
  - It is the one place where columns are read.
  - Its size is bounded by the depth selector as today.
  - One sentence explains it.
- **A connector is a passage.** In `visibleIds()`, when the walk reaches a table whose kind has role `connector` and which is not the focus, that table's neighbours join **the same level**. The rule repeats through chains of connectors, since a passage costs no level. The focus itself spends levels normally.
  - At "direct links", a hub shows its satellites and PITs, its links, and the hubs and effectivity satellites across those links (done-when 2).
  - A focus on a link shows its hubs and satellites, as today.
  - Depth 2 from a hub reaches the satellites and links of the hubs across.
  - The kind filter still applies after the walk, so a passage through an unticked Link kind still brings the hubs on the other side. The link itself is not drawn; its hubs show without the edge, which is today's behaviour for any unticked kind in the middle of a walk.
  - Without roles, or without `connector` kinds (`shop`, no preset), the walk is exactly today's.
  - Reason: in a Data Vault a link carries no business meaning of its own. "Which entities is this one directly linked to" is answered through it, and the brief asks for it.
- **Effect on acme_retail.** Its default drawings are unchanged (done-when 3). A focus on one of its hubs at "direct links" now also shows the hubs across its links. This is the new rule, described by the brief and recorded in D-076, and the existing focus test is updated to it.

### 5. Shape of the skeleton's layout

#### 5.1 Why something is needed

Folded, the vehicle group is about 58 to 70 hubs (referenced) and about 66 links (referencing). With `rankdir=LR`, dot puts the hubs in one column and the links in the next one or three (the `minlen` 1 to 3 spread of today's sinks): a strip 2 to 4 tables wide and 25 to 70 high. Its aspect is about 1:6, far from 1:2 to 3:1.

#### 5.2 Chosen: columns imposed on dot, as many as the drawing area asks for (`skeletonColumns`)

Only while `skeleton()` is true. Other drawings keep today's DOT exactly.

1. **Sides.** A drawn table is on the **left side** when no drawn relation has it as child (hubs, and isolated tables), else on the **right side** (links and anything that references). This is the same left-to-right reading as today: referenced on the left.
2. **Size estimate per table** (points, never measured, as the universe's `box`, D-040):
   - width = `max(name chars × 9.6 + 20, folded row chars × 7 + 16)`;
   - height = 32 + (24 when it holds tables), or today's row count × 24 + 32 under **All columns**.
3. **Column count `K`.** For each `K` from 2 to the number of drawn tables:
   - deal the tables (step 4);
   - estimate the drawing size: W = Σ column widths + (K − 1) × `ranksep`, H = the tallest column (heights + `nodesep` per table).
   
   Keep the `K` whose `log(W / H)` is nearest to `log(box width / box height)`, clamped so that W / H stays within [0.5, 3]. Ties go to the smaller `K`. The left side takes `a = max(1, round(K × left / drawn))` columns and the right side `K − a` (at least 1 when it has tables).
4. **Dealing** (deterministic):
   - Left tables are sorted by drawn degree (descending), then name, then id, and each goes into the left column with the smallest estimated height so far.
   - Right tables are sorted by the mean column index of their drawn parents, then name, then id, and each goes into the right column with the smallest height, ties to the column nearest that mean.

   Unconnected parts (isolated hubs, a small component) take their room in the same columns, so they are packed beside the rest instead of making a strip of their own.
5. **DOT.**
   - `rankdir=LR; newrank=true`.
   - One invisible anchor node per column, chained by invisible edges (`style=invis`, `minlen=1`), with each column's tables in `{rank=same; anchor; …}`, so the columns hold even when a column has no edge.
   - A relation whose child's column is right of its parent's column gets `minlen` = the difference (as the flow view, D-036). Any other relation gets `constraint=false`.
   - `nodesep` and `ranksep` for the skeleton start at 0.25 and 0.9 (the flow's values); the developer may tune them within [0.2, 0.5] and [0.6, 1.4] and records them.
   - dot keeps its crossing minimisation inside each column.
6. **Saved positions** work as today on top of it: a moved table triggers the `neato` pass with every table pinned at the dot positions of this layout (§ 7).

Reasons:

- **Imposed columns are the flow view's measured technique.** `rank=same` per column plus `minlen` laid out 146 tables in about 0.45 s on the 1,500-table payload (M5.3). It is deterministic, and it keeps the ERD's left-to-right reading and dot's orthogonal-ish edges.
- **`K` is chosen from the drawing area's own aspect,** so the drawing fills a 1280 × 800 window and a portrait tablet alike, within the brief's bounds.
- **Expected result on the vehicle skeleton** (estimate): about 136 tables of 200 to 260 × 56 to 80 pt, so K ≈ 9 to 11 columns of 12 to 15 tables, about 2,900 × 1,300 pt (W/H ≈ 2.2, inside [0.5, 3]). Fit ≈ 0.42 to 0.5 in the 1280 × 680 box; names at 16 pt are 10 to 12 px after three notches.

#### 5.3 Rejected options for the shape

These are the developer's fallbacks, in this order if § 5.2 misses a bound in § 5.4:

1. **dot with tuned `ranksep` / `nodesep` and the sink spread widened to `K` columns** (today's `minlen` trick on the right side only). The left side stays one column, so the strip's height is the hub count: about 60 tables × 80 pt = 4,800 pt high, aspect ≈ 1:2 at best. It is kept as the first fallback because it is the smallest change.
2. **`neato` (or `fdp`) with `overlap=prism`, `splines=true`, `sep`.**
   - For: aspect near 1, structure readable as clusters.
   - Against: spline routing around 136 HTML-label nodes is the slow part in Viz.js (seconds); the result is not deterministic across Graphviz versions; links and hubs mix, losing the left-to-right reading; a dragged table then moves nothing else.
3. **`sfdp`.** Fast, but its edges are straight lines through tables, and Viz.js's build may not hold it with HTML labels. Last resort.
4. **dot `pack` / `packmode` of connected components.** The vehicle skeleton is one large component (links join random hubs, one in five across domains), so packing changes nothing. Kept only as a note.
5. **`ratio` / `size` attributes.** They scale the positions after the layout and do not re-rank: the strip stays a strip, stretched.
6. **`rankdir=TB`.** The hubs become one row 60 tables wide: the strip turned 90°.

### 6. Meta line (`#d-meta`)

- **Today's form, kept** when the skeleton is off: `drawn / N tables · R relations · dashed: other group`.
- **Skeleton:** `136 / 271 tables · 135 folded · 214 relations · dashed: other group`.
  - `136` = drawn tables.
  - `271` = scope tables (`plural`, never reduced by the fold, brief § 4).
  - `135 folded` = folded-now tables (`T.foldedShort`: "folded" / "repliées", a participle, so English needs no plural and French uses `plural`: "1 repliée", "135 repliées").
  - The relations are those **drawn**.
- **The `title` of `#d-meta`** gives the split by kind: "Hub 70 · Link 66 · folded: Satellite 135" (kind labels then counts, so no inflected config label; see § 2). This takes the brief's "70 hubs · 66 links · 135 satellites folded" without plurals made from config labels.
- The ui-designer may reorder or shorten within these rules.

### 7. Export and arrangements

- **SVG and PNG** export the drawn SVG through `svgImage` / `pngImage`, unchanged: the skeleton, its folded rows and its columns as on screen. Tooltips are removed as today, the anchors are invisible nodes with no shape, and the `.export` controls stay in the toolbar.
- **Scope of a stored arrangement** (D-013 extended as D-064 did):
  - `viewKey()` gains a sixth part `fold`, **only while `skeleton()` is true**: `group|||keys|hub,link,sat|fold`.
  - Every key of a drawing that is not a skeleton is exactly today's, so pre-M8.5 arrangements (all of drawings that were not skeletons) keep applying: acme_retail, focused drawings, and the large groups when the viewer shows the folded tables.
  - Reason for a separate key: the skeleton's dot coordinates come from another layout. A pin saved in one drawing would put a table in a meaningless place in the other, and the `neato` pass pins every table.
- **A table hidden by the fold keeps its stored position:** it lives under the unfolded key, which skeleton drags never write. Within one key, `startDrag` already keeps every entry when it adds one, so positions of tables filtered out by a kind are kept too.
- `domain/layout.check_layout` accepts any detail key: no change. Reset layout clears every key (`LAYOUT.clear`, unchanged). Older pages ignore the `|fold` keys and keep them on export (D-048's rule).

### 8. The `select` of the `data_vault` preset: unchanged

Decision: dimensions and facts do **not** enter the preset's `select`. The preset's detail stays the raw vault: hubs, links and satellites.

Reasons:

- **Done-when 1.** The brief's "136 tables or fewer" is the skeleton of the preset's current selection. The vehicle schema also holds 10 dimensions and 10 facts, all `core`, so they are never folded and would make the skeleton 156 tables.
- **They are another layer.** They sit in Data products (D-060) and form a separate star (fact → dimension relations) next to the vault. One drawing would then show two models side by side; the universe already shows both, by layer.
- **No page changes for a choice the brief reserves for the user.** Every page of a `data_vault` project without its own `select` would change. Keeping D-060's state needs no approval, and the question goes to `docs/progress.md` as an open point for the M8 close.
- **The way to include them is one line,** already shown by acme_retail's own `select` (`"dim_*", "fct_*"`). The user guide's preset section says so.

- **Responsibility map**, new or changed rows:
  - "Parent of a descriptive table" → also "carried to the detail payload by `detail_data(parents)` from the universe's result (D-072)";
  - "Which detail tables are folded now, held, drawn; whether the detail is a skeleton (page, D-073)" → `detail.js skeleton`, `held`, the three sets;
  - "Columns of a skeleton drawing (page, D-074)" → `detail.js skeletonColumns`;
  - "Neighbourhood of a focused table, connectors as passages (page, D-076)" → `detail.js visibleIds`;
  - "A viewer's choice kept in localStorage outside the layout (page)" → `common.js viewerMemory` (replaces the `universe.js memory` row).
- **Glossary (D-027):**
  - **skeleton**: the detail drawing of a large group with its descriptive tables folded (M8.5). Not summary, outline, overview. French UI: not shown as a word; the panel says "Entités" and the fold row.
  - **passage**: a connector table that the focus walk crosses without spending a level. Internal word, not in the UI.
  - "entity", "fold", "parent" keep their M8.2 meanings in the detail.

## Rejected options

- **Recomputing parents for the detail in `detail_data`** (`fold.parents` over the detail's tables, with groups as places). This would give a second answer for the same table, since a group is not a place. Moving `domain_of` / `_layer_of` into a shared module only to rebuild members would also add an import path for nothing.
- **Parents as a separate map at the payload's top level.** One more key shape; the universe already puts `parent` on the node.
- **Ordering the detail Types list by role.** It would change acme_retail's detail panel (done-when 3).
- **The trigger on the filtered (visible) count.** Unticking a kind would switch layouts back and forth around 60.
- **A trigger and threshold computed in Python.** It is a drawing rule, like `FLOW_MAX`, and the rule would exist twice.
- **"+ 3 satellites" from config labels inflected by rule.** It is wrong for acronyms and for French, and the payload has no plural forms.
- **Cell-level tooltips through `HREF`.** They make every row a link, and clicks would navigate.
- **Keeping key rows in the skeleton.** Names would be about 7 px after three notches (§ 2), which fails done-when 1.
- **One fold state shared with the universe** (same key `dbtscope-fold:<title>`). Different needs, and a cross-tab relayout (§ 3).
- **A `detail.fold` config key.** A permanent `dbt-erd.yml` surface for a default that matches the trigger's purpose.
- **Folding inside a focus above the threshold.** A focus is where the viewer asks for everything; depth already bounds it.
- **A passage through any table of degree 2, or through every non-core table.** That is a guess from shape; the role says what a connector is (D-059).
- **The shape options of § 5.3.** Reasons there.
- **One detail key for both drawings.** Pins from one layout would be applied in the other.
- **Adding `dim_*` / `fct_*` to `data_vault`'s `select`.** § 8.

## Impacts

Decisions to append to `docs/decisions.md` (documentation step, with these texts; the developer completes the measured values):

- **D-072 — The detail payload carries each table's parent and each kind's role.**
  - `DetailEntity.parent` (last key, `""` for none) is the universe's parent (D-062), kept only when that parent is also a detail table: one rule, one result for both views. `detail_data` takes the parent map, which `cli` builds from the universe payload.
  - `DetailKind.role` (last key, `"none"` when unset).
  - The detail Types list keeps its payload order.
  - Refines D-059 (the detail's roles, announced for M8.5) and D-062 (its result also feeds the detail).
- **D-073 — A large detail group is drawn as a skeleton: core and connector tables, each with one row counting its folded tables.**
  - **Trigger.** No focus, more than 60 tables in the group's scope, at least one table whose parent is in the scope, and the viewer's choice "folded" (default).
  - **Drawing.** Folded now = visible tables whose parent is visible. Descriptive tables without a parent stay drawn. At the keys level no column rows are drawn (All columns draws them). The folded row reads "+ N folded tables · Satellite 3 · PIT 1". The table tooltip lists the folded tables (12 then "+ N") and the column count. Relations of folded tables are not drawn. The highlight box also lights a table whose folded tables match. The Types list counts every table.
  - **Meta line.** `drawn / scope tables · N folded · drawn relations`, with the split by kind in its title.
  - **Control.** "Entities" in the detail panel, shown when the group passes the threshold and has parents, disabled while focused. It has its own state, kept in localStorage `dbtscope-detail-fold:<title>` (written only when not the default, never exported). While unchecked, the warning "271 tables: the drawing will be hard to read" shows.
  - `viewerMemory` is the one helper for viewer choices.
  - Export as on screen.
  - Refines D-063 (the detail's own fold, same words) and D-021 (exports unchanged in form).
- **D-074 — A skeleton is laid out in columns imposed on dot, as many as the drawing area's aspect asks for.**
  - Sides (referenced tables left, referencing right), estimated sizes, `K` chosen so that the estimated W / H is nearest the box's, clamped to [0.5, 3]. Greedy deterministic dealing; invisible anchor chain and `rank=same` per column; `minlen` for rightward relations, `constraint=false` for the others. Skeleton `nodesep` / `ranksep` as measured.
  - Measured on the vehicle group: (W / H, time, header px, values).
  - Other drawings keep today's DOT.
  - Refines D-014 (Graphviz still lays out in the page) and reuses D-036's imposed columns.
- **D-075 — A skeleton keeps its own arrangement.**
  - The detail key gains the part `|fold` only for a skeleton; every other key is unchanged, so earlier arrangements apply to the drawings they were made on.
  - A table hidden by the fold keeps its stored position under the unfolded key.
  - Format, version, file name and localStorage key unchanged.
  - Extends D-013's content as D-064 did.
- **D-076 — Around a focused table, a connector is a passage.**
  - A connector reached by the focus walk (not the focus itself) adds its neighbours at the same level. At "direct links" a hub shows its satellites, its links and the hubs across them.
  - Without connector kinds the walk is unchanged.
  - The kind filter still applies after the walk.
  - A focus never folds.
  - Before M8.5 the hubs across needed depth 2. Refines the detail focus of v0.2 (no earlier decision recorded it).
- **D-077 — The `data_vault` preset's detail `select` stays the raw vault.**
  - Dimensions and facts are not added (§ 8): the skeleton count of done-when 1, a separate layer, and no page change for a choice the brief leaves to the user. A project adds them by its own `select`, as acme_retail does.
  - Confirms D-060's "`select` is unchanged". Open point for the user at the M8 close.
